The small realm they occupied could not contain it, and cracks of black spatial fissures began to appear.

Heaven and earth trembled, gradually disintegrating.

Merely the aura he emanated had already created an apocalyptic scene.

This terrified the You Clan members below to no end.

"The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance is currently exploring your sacred land; I intend to take the opportunity to assassinate them in secret."

"Are you willing to lend this Immortal a hand?"

Li Fan asked in an icy tone.

There was no second option. Coupled with their hatred for the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, the You Clan members below quickly chose submission.

When he smoothly brought them back to the Spiritwood Realm and, as per custom, they were to pay respects to the Faceless True Immortal.

The group of You Clan members acted as if they had seen something utterly terrifying; they all collapsed to the ground in an instant, trembling uncontrollably.

Chapter 1098: Surge Of The Dark Tide

Seeing the abnormal state of the You Clan people before him, Li Fan remained composed. He ignored their extreme fear and forced them to complete the initiation oath in front of the Faceless True Immortal statue.

Only after they finally calmed down did Li Fan inquire about the reason for their terror.

The leader of the You Clan trembled as he spoke. After a long while, he managed to explain: “We have never had the fortune to see the True Immortal statue before. But the moment we laid eyes on it, our clansmen instinctively felt boundless fear.”

“We live in perpetual darkness year-round, and our bodies have adapted to that unique environment. Under normal sunlight, we can endure for a short time, but in front of the True Immortal statue...”

“It is like a light source in utter blackness, a thousand times brighter than the sun in the sky, suddenly appearing.”

The You Clan leader's words came in fits and starts, but Li Fan understood his meaning.

Just as mortals would feel fear when standing at the edge of an abyss, the You Clan people sensed extreme terror upon seeing the Faceless True Immortal statue, as if facing a deadly threat.

Yet the True Immortal's web was formless and intangible. Even Li Fan, as the proxy of the Faceless True Immortal in the Xuanhuang Realm, might not detect its presence otherwise.

In contrast, every member of this You Clan group, even the newborns among them, without exception, felt the power of the True Immortal.

“It seems this is inseparable from the darkness power they wield.”

Next, Li Fan learned from the You Clan leader about their understanding of the darkness.

The You Clan people were originally humans as well.

According to the ancient legends passed down in their tribe, in ancient times, their world still had blue skies, white clouds, and bright sunlight.

But one day, after the sun set, it never rose again.

People begged in despair amid the darkness, yet no miracle came.

The vast majority vanished forever into the blackness.

Those who survived were, without exception, all favored by the darkness power.

Under the erosion and transformation of the darkness power, their bodies gradually changed.

They became headless horrors, with their facial features crammed onto their chests.

In truth, the You Clan people did not understand the essence of the dark power. They simply worshiped it as the sole lifesaving straw, a terrifying evil god that brought doom to the normal world, offering prayers and pleas.

And this dark power, as if it could comprehend their words, would truly respond and serve them.

The You Clan people did not believe they had mastered the dark power; rather, it was the grace of the great God of Darkness.

...

What the You Clan leader said differed from the introduction given by Dharma-Transmitter Zhou.

The You Clan people were clearly natives of the Dark Realm, yet Dharma-Transmitter Zhou claimed they were merely a special group that had discovered and exploited the power of the Dark Realm.

On such a minor detail, Dharma-Transmitter Zhou had no reason to deceive him.

“It seems the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance's discovery of the Dark Realm was not as simple as she glossed over.”

A glint of cold light flashed in Li Fan's eyes.

Next, he pondered the manifestation of this darkness power.

“Prayer, then awaiting a response?”

“No wonder the ritual to open the passage to the Dark Realm also involves song and dance.”

Li Fan's gaze flickered.

He was no stranger to powers that responded to the stirrings of a cultivator's heart, carrying such a nature.

The golden Source Power Essence: the remnant power discarded by True Immortals in their pursuit of the root of the Dao. It was the existence known as the Flower of the Dao.

“Since they can sense each other, there must be some connection between the two.”

According to the introduction from the leader of the You Clan, although the power of darkness only manifests concretely in their homeland, it is actually omnipresent throughout the entire Xuanhuang Realm. It simply does not condense in a specific form, making it difficult for ordinary people to detect. However, through special methods of prayer and invocation, one can cross the barriers between small worlds and call upon its power.

Under Li Fan's witness, the leader of the You Clan wore a devout expression and chanted incantations in a low voice.

He launched an attack toward a ferocious beast ahead that resembled a lion-tiger hybrid.

This lion-tiger beast was a new breed recently cultivated by the empire: its flesh was as hard as steel. Even a full-force strike from an ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator would scarcely harm it.

But an extremely eerie scene unfolded.

Without any warning, the once majestic lion-tiger beast suddenly collapsed, its limbs going limp as it fell to the ground.

Its eyes were filled with terror; it struggled helplessly to rise.

Yet beneath its body, it was as if an invisible quagmire existed. Countless hands seemed to tug at it relentlessly from the mire.

Under Li Fan's gaze, the lion-tiger beast was slowly engulfed by the darkness.

Not even bones remained.

From beginning to end, Li Fan never "saw" the existence of the darkness.

But the lion-tiger beast had indeed vanished right before his eyes.

Li Fan carefully examined the ground where the darkness had descended: no trace of power lingered.

"Come and go without a shadow, impossible to guard against." Li Fan stroked his chin and fell into deep thought.

Meanwhile, the group of You clansmen once again closed their eyes in collective devotion, praying.

"It seems only they can elicit a response from the darkness through such prayer. Ordinary humans, no matter how closely they mimic the form, cannot summon the power of darkness."

"That makes sense. Only by joining our Federation of All Worlds, swearing the oath before the True Immortal Image, and integrating into the True Immortal webs can his prayer be sensed by me. Presumably, the power of darkness also possesses such an invisible net."

Li Fan pondered silently: "So the key lies in how to join this net without being devoured by the darkness."

In the Dark Realm, the bizarre state of Mo Rubin's corpse was still fresh in his mind. Merely physical contact with it had led to such an outcome. It was hard to imagine what would happen if one's body and soul were fully immersed in the darkness, and one could survive.

Next, Li Fan ordered his deathsworn subordinates to attempt imitating the communication with the darkness, which confirmed his speculation.

They vanished without a sound, swallowed by the darkness: no trace of life or death.

Moreover, repeatedly invoking the power of darkness in a short time seemed to trigger subtle changes.

As the lord of the Spiritwood Realm, Li Fan faintly sensed the entire world dimming for an instant.

After confirming with the leader of the You Clan, Li Fan knew this was no illusion on his part.

Rather, the darkness indeed showed a trend of flowing toward this place.

Li Fan decisively halted the experiments for the time being.

After questioning various members of the You Clan at length, Li Fan finally gained a general understanding of the current state of the power of darkness in the Xuanhuang Realm.

It was like an ocean of darkness gathered on the "back side" of the Xuanhuang.

Though fixed in place by some mysterious positional force, the inherently pervasive nature of the darkness caused it to occasionally seep through any possible "crevices," invading the Xuanhuang and even the various small worlds.

The intrusion would ultimately return to the Dark Realm.

This cycle repeated, forming something akin to "tides."

Chapter 1099: Stealing Darkness To Seek A Chance For Survival

"The more frequently one begs for the power of darkness, the more gaps it creates. The likelihood of darkness invading becomes greater."

Li Fan calmly analyzed the characteristics of the power of darkness.

One could imagine that if the mysterious force fixing the ocean of darkness did not exist, the Xuanhuang Realm would long ago have been eroded into a thousand holes by this dark tide.

"The existence of the Immortal Ruins created the ocean of darkness. Then what is it that, without anyone noticing, silently dissipates this crisis?"

Thinking further of Mo Rubin's desperate attempt to enter the Dark Realm, Li Fan was almost certain that something related to the Xuantian King existed there.

Or perhaps his residual power.

Or even...

Himself.

From the You Clan, he selected three young juniors and had them separately teach the rituals for communicating with the power of darkness.

Li Fan then took the You Clan leader and a dozen or so sturdy clansmen out of the Spiritwood Realm.

The existence of the dark tide would lead to invasions by the power of darkness. Naturally, Li Fan would not hold the ritual in his own home.

Fortunately, he knew of many unclaimed small worlds.

Half a day later, Li Fan selected a suitable one and sneaked inside.

This small world had no surface.

Only endless underground caverns. The only sources of light were the occasional glowing moss on the cavern walls.

It was quite suitable for the You Clan to live in.

Under Fang Zaiji's urging, the You Clan leader began the ritual to open the passage to the Dark Realm.

Afterward, Li Fan ordered him to lead the way.

Li Fan himself, along with Tianyang, Master Qiaogong, Liu Ruchen, and Fang Zaiji; the five of them held hands tightly and followed closely behind.

They entered the boundless dark world once more.

Li Fan had already adapted somewhat.

The Master Qiaogong and the others, however, clearly showed tension and fear emanating from their bodies.

Even though they had heard about it beforehand and made sufficient mental preparations.

Being truly immersed in it still made it hard to shake off these emotions.

The group, linked as one, communicated via divine sense.

"Can you sense the presence of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance people here?"

Li Fan asked the You Clan leader.

He gave a negative reply.

"The power of darkness is too profound. Even we, the long-time inhabitants, find it hard to see things around us. Moreover, I have not been home for a long time. Please allow me some time to adapt, lord emmisary..."

Hearing this, Li Fan immediately stared at him warily.

Fortunately, it was just him overthinking.

After a while, the You Clan leader finally roughly determined the direction.

"Follow me. Not far ahead is where our residence used to be. If the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance has set up camp here, they would surely choose that place."

In the darkness, they advanced for an unknown amount of time.

The You Clan leader suddenly stopped.

"As expected, there are people, and quite a few. The sea of darkness has been affected, sending out chaotic fluctuations." He explained to Li Fan and the others, his tone filled with hatred.

Only then did Li Fan notice.

The inexplicable pulsation he had sensed from Mo Rubin's corpse last time seemed to have vanished.

And in the darkness above, positions could not be discerned at all.

"It seems we can only search for clues from the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's camp."

Li Fan thought to himself.

"Can you figure out how many people are inside?"

The You Clan leader sensed for a moment.

"Probably around five. As for strength, similar to this one..."

He was referring to Liu Ruchen among the group.

"You can actually discern our strength?" Fang Zaiji asked curiously.

"Outside, naturally not. But in this sea of darkness, for us You Clan people, it is a simple matter." The You Clan leader said with some pride.

"It's like objects of different weights causing different changes when placed in water. He must have discerned it through that." Master Qiaogong gazed into the endless darkness around them and explained.

Fang Zaiji then understood.

"Can you see what the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance people are doing here?" Li Fan asked with a frown.

"We need to get a bit closer."

After pondering for a moment, Li Fan agreed to his request: "Do not force it. Now is not the time to clash directly with the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance."

Having fought the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance before, the You Clan leader naturally understood the terror of their strength.

Indicating understanding, he then slowly approached step by step toward the front.

“This is...”

“How dare they!”

The You Clan leader's emotions suddenly fluctuated greatly.

Fortunately, they were in the Dark Sea, so they were not discovered by the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance members.

The You Clan leader shared the scene he sensed.

Several white shadows formed a circle.

In their center seemed to exist a small vortex formed by shadowy power.

This vortex was very small, just a bit less than the size of a palm.

Its appearance was also quite strange, with the vortex center pointing upward, opposite to the usual ones seen in everyday life.

But the several Dao Integration cultivators from the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance guarding it seemed extremely tense.

“What is this?” Fang Zaiji asked with some doubt.

“They are stealing the great power of darkness!” the You Clan leader said through gritted teeth.

Li Fan and the others were all startled upon hearing this.

“Even with the restrictions from the Dark Sea's own power, it can't stop these shameful thieves!” The You Clan leader wished he could rush over immediately to stop the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's actions.

Fortunately, he still had some reason and only said it verbally, without actually acting on it.

“What do you all think?”

Looking at the scene in his mind, Li Fan asked the group.

“Darkness power deviates from the Immortal Ruins. Clearly, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance is not satisfied with just the small amounts infiltrating outward during the tides; they want to extract dark power on a large scale.”

“It seems they ultimately couldn't resist the temptation to escape the Immortal Ruins.”

Liu Ruchen, who received the shared intelligence from Li Fan, couldn't help but sigh softly.

“I just don't know what method they used to create this vortex. It can't be that they specifically used a small realm to attract darkness power, right...” Fang Zaiji said casually.

As soon as he finished speaking, he himself was stunned.

“It's not impossible. It's just a small realm with no special value. If it can draw in such massive darkness power, even destroying it would be worth it,” the Master Qiaogong said confidently.

“Superior Emmisary, perhaps we can imitate the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance.” He even suggested that Li Fan do the same.

“I have a premonition that combining this darkness power with source power essence and applying it to immortal puppets might take it further, creating true True Immortal Puppets.” The Master Qiaogong's tone carried a hint of fanaticism.

Although Li Fan had similar thoughts, he did not immediately agree.

Instead, he asked the You Clan leader if he could sense any remnants of cultivators' corpses that had fallen nearby.

“Huh? If lord emmisary hadn't mentioned it, I almost wouldn't have noticed.”

“But this doesn't seem like a corpse.”

“It seems more like an empty shell.”

Chapter 1100: Old Acquaintance Fairy Immortal Puppet

"Even our clansmen, upon dying in the Dark Sea, cannot achieve this."

"It is too light, too light."

After the You Clan leader's explanation, Li Fan instantly understood.

It was still judged by the fluctuations caused while in the Dark Sea.

Mo Rubin's corpse did not conform at all to the normal state of an ordinary being after death.

"Either it is merely an empty shell serving as a smokescreen, or..."

"Or this person was deeply favored by the darkness; upon his death, the vast majority of his spirituality returned to the Dark Sea in an instant. Only the decaying parts remained, slowly dissolving into the darkness." The You Clan leader suddenly mentioned another possibility.

"However, even the most powerful priests in our clan's history did not reach such a state after death. So, I am more inclined to believe what we saw was merely an empty shell."

Upon hearing this, Li Fan's gaze flickered.

He had been somewhat shocked by the You Clan leader's words just now. But if he connected the darkness power here with the Xuantian King, then Mo Rubin's abnormality after death made sense.

"Yet we cannot let our guard down. The Xuantian Sect is profoundly treacherous, with endless emerging methods. We must remain vigilant."

"Regardless of whether he truly died or not, my plan will not change in the slightest."

With a thought flashing through his mind, Li Fan inquired further about the details of Mo Rubin's corpse.

The location where Li Fan had seen him not long ago was clearly not here.

Unexpectedly, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance had found a way to resist the darkness contagion in such a short time and transferred him here.

"If it were truly just a worthless empty shell, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance would not go to such lengths to transfer it. In my view, the vortex they stole from the darkness likely relies on Mo Rubin's corpse to achieve stability," Master Qiaogong stated his opinion.

"This power of darkness has some similarities in nature to source power essence. By common sense, with the Dark Sea's immense scale, if such a venting outlet truly appeared, it would inevitably form an unimaginable energy vortex. But here, it is a bit too calm."

Master Qiaogong spoke with conviction, leaving the You Clan leader momentarily stunned.

He observed carefully for a long time before saying in some surprise: "Lord emmisary, this immortal master is correct! The originally violent power of darkness, after being filtered and influenced by that empty shell, has actually become stable!"

"Even the darkness can be soothed; exactly what..." The You Clan leader's tone was one of astonishment and uncertainty.

Hearing this, Li Fan immediately fell into contemplation.

If the Dark Realm truly had some connection to the Xuantian King, then aside from his former companions in the Xuantian Sect, what else could draw his particular attention.

It was probably only the statue of the Faceless True Immortal.

But it was not yet certain what kind of upheaval the True Immortal statue would cause if it appeared in the Dark Realm. So, whether to do so or not, Li Fan still needed to consider carefully.

The primary task of this trip was still to take the opportunity to study this mysterious power of darkness. And to probe news of Mo Rubin.

"Can we approach here and secretly open a breach connecting to a small realm?" Li Fan asked. "With the effect of Mo Rubin's corpse, the caused fluctuations should not be too great."

The You Clan leader pondered for a moment and gave an affirmative reply.

"But it is best not to draw too much power of darkness; otherwise, two vortices of equal size might gradually draw closer due to their mutual interaction and form a larger whole."

"Hmm, we only intend to draw a little for research. We will not be too greedy."

With Li Fan making the decision, the group quickly sprang into action.

Under their leader's guidance, the You Clan members secretly opened another spatial passage almost right next to the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's outpost.

The process went quite smoothly from start to finish, without being discovered by the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance.

Of course, this might also be because they had devoted all their attention to guarding that massive vortex.

"It is truly wondrous; the surging Dark Sea has actually become gentle here." Witnessing this divine scene firsthand, the You Clan leader could not help but let out a sigh of admiration.

And on the other side of the passage, in the underground world.

The shadowy power, once freed from its cage, immediately reverted to its true nature.

It surged outward at an astonishing speed, engulfing everything around it.

The last traces of light in the underground world soon vanished completely.

Countless living beings died silently, without understanding why.

Fortunately, the scale of this subterranean small realm was not too vast.

After swiftly filling it, the darkness power began to flow back toward the Dark Realm.

Truly like the ebb and flow of tides.

At the entrance connecting the underground world to the Xuanhuang Realm, Li Fan keenly detected the darkness power beginning to seep through.

However, since they had not yet opened the passage, this process was extremely slow.

“If we must pass through here for entry and exit, then it won’t be long before the overflowing darkness power is detected by the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance,” Liu Ruchen said with a furrowed brow.

“I’ll find a way,” Master Qiaogong volunteered. “Source power essence and darkness power are mutually incompatible. Perhaps we can build a buffer zone here. Of course, this may require a large amount of source power essence.”

As the Master Qiaogong spoke, he could not help but glance at Li Fan.

Li Fan felt no attachment to these remnants of True Immortal power. He had already accumulated a considerable quantity over this period, with no particular use for it.

Still, he pretended to ponder for a long time before reluctantly agreeing.

Master Qiaogong was overjoyed at this and immediately set to work, summoning his cherished creation to assist.

A stunningly beautiful female Immortal Puppet, obedient to the Qiaogong in every way.

It employed the same technology as the Tianyang Immortal Puppet, though it was unclear where Master Qiaogong had obtained the puppet’s conscious personality.

It was during the construction of the “buffer zone” that Qiaogong boasted casually and revealed her origins.

“Back when the [Ancestor] collapsed, I barely survived the calamity by the skin of my teeth and fled my homeland. But some fellows were not as fortunate as me. Their bodies were destroyed in the disaster, leaving only a backup of their consciousness, which I carried with me.”

“Don’t let her current obedience fool you; in the past, she scolded me plenty! Hahaha!”

“Back when it was the Heavenly Spirit Sect, I had also prepared a puppet body for her. But it was too rigid; I didn't like it. After playing with it for a short while, I lost interest and destroyed her.”

“How could it compare to this one now: lifelike, almost indistinguishable from a real person.”
