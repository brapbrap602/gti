The purple sea of thunder submerged Li Haoran, fiercely hot, causing even space to become tattered and worn, and in this vast sea of thunder, only Li Haoran’s treasures emitted white light.

"Could it be that Senior Brother Li could safely pass the tribulation relying on magic artifacts?!" someone exclaimed softly, the effectiveness of these defensive treasures was heaven-defying, suggesting they might hold up till the end of the Heavenly Tribulation.

"Wait, why has Senior Brother Li put away his magic artifacts?"

Li Haoran, in a departure from his usual behavior, retracted his magic artifacts and instead opted to resist the Thunder Tribulation with his physical body.

Li Haoran recalled the tribulation passing experiences taught by Lu Yang, relying solely on magic artifacts was not wise, as it would not achieve the effect of cultivation through Heavenly Tribulation.

He abandoned his strengths and faced the Thunder Tribulation head-on!

Boom—

Li Haoran was struck by the Thunder Tribulation, his skin split and flesh burst, and his bones crackled loudly, audible even from a great distance.

The crowd’s hearts tightened at this sight.

"Why has Senior Brother Li closed his eyes?"

The crowd was shocked; they had never heard of a cultivator daring to close their eyes during the tribulation!

Li Haoran closed his eyes and entered a profound state, the Thunder Tribulation not only struck his body but also posed a threat to his Divine Soul.

However, for Li Haoran, this was a beneficial affair, as the Thunder Tribulation acted like a key, viciously striking his Divine Soul and unlocking long-sealed memories.

The Reincarnation Dao Fruit Prototype, lurking unnoticed deep within his soul, began to stir, historical snippets from ancient times flashed through his mind like kites, gradually awakening the memories of his first lifetime.

The same Thunder Tribulation that enveloped him, split his skin and flesh, now felt incredibly dear to him.

Li Haoran ceased to resist the Thunder Tribulation, spread his arms wide, and allowed it to consume him.

The crowd was horrified; was he seeking death?

Only a few discerning individuals saw the signs; the Thunder Tribulation that flowed through Li Haoran’s body refined his body and soul, but did not harm him at all. He had reached the pinnacle of control over the Thunder Tribulation, a feat only possible for those historic figures who had survived the tribulation, and only Li Haoran could achieve this!

He draped the sea of thunder over himself like a cape, his eyes emanating golden electric sparks, pointing toward the sky.

Just above him, the ongoing Heavenly Tribulation disappeared from inside out, exposing the clear blue sky.

All eyes were focused on Li Haoran, as if he had become the most dazzling entity between heaven and earth.

He surveyed the insignificant beings below him and slowly said, "I remember now, I am Ying..."

Boom—

Before he could finish his sentence, a sudden change occurred.

The dissipating Thunder Tribulation halted midway and even began to replenish its gaps.

The Heavenly Tribulation came anew, and suddenly three imposing figures appeared.

"What’s happening!"

"Who are they?"

"That, those three are Immortal Jiuchong, Time Immortal, and Qilin Immortal!" someone shouted, recognizing the origins of those three figures.

"What, those three Ancient Immortals, why have they appeared?"

This was an unprecedented situation!

Seeing his three old friends, Li Haoran revealed a subtly meaningful smile, "You three..."

"Youngster, did you think you could pass the Thunder Tribulation so easily? We three seniors have come to test you!" Immortal Jiuchong said cheerfully, taking the lead in attacking.

The other two immortals also took action, they have been waiting for this day for a long time!

Li Haoran’s expression changed drastically, these three rascals, have they no shame!

"Why have they suddenly started fighting?" the onlookers below were confused.

"Has he summoned the legendary Thunder Tribulation..." Gu Junye stood with his hands behind his back, smiling lightly as he watched the four immortals brawl.

"Do you know what’s going on?" Daoist Xiantian was surprised; Gu Junye always seemed to know some ancient secrets unknown to others.

"This is the legendary test of Thunder Tribulation. Immortal Jiuchong and the other two immortals’ strength is remembered by history, inscribed within the Heavenly Tribulation. Senior Brother Li’s strength has summoned this test from the immortals; if he can pass this test, he will gain an extraordinary opportunity!"

Daoist Xiantian was still puzzled: "Then why doesn’t the Thunder Tribulation feature the Yingtian Immortal?"

"Naturally, because Yingtian Immortal isn’t as powerful as the legends claim," Gu Junye confidently declared.
