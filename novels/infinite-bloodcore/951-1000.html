Chapter 951: Long Fu vs Yun Zhong

The dragonman youngster’s duel with Yun Zhong began with a chase.

As an archer, Yun Zhong’s foremost tactic was controlling distance between his opponent.

He could use the battle skill [Swift Move] as the base, then add on [Wind Walk Step] to hasten himself.

Yun Zhong fought and shot while retreating.

The two gold levels proceeded to fight in the air like this.

All of the spectators looked up, their eyes shining with excitement.

Flying battle skills were not common, and even if one was a gold level fighter, they normally fought on the ground most of the time. Seeing airborne combat like this was a true treat.

Yun Zhong flew in the air skillfully, in coordination with his battle skills. He was simply as nimble as a swallow, but regretfully, the dueling arena’s air space was also limited.

As the dragonman youngster adapted, Yun Zhong’s was forced into narrow space.

Battle skill – Misty Cloud Walk!

Yun Zhong used an original battle skill to step on the white clouds and curled thick fog around his body, increasing his speed and concealing himself, fading from the dragonman youngster’s field of view.

The dragonman youngster used three types of detection battle skill, none were able to find a trace of Yun Zhong, leading to a grave expression.

Battle skill – Dragon Pearl, Explosive Flame.

He condensed a few dragon pearls, then bombarded where Yun Zhong was last seen, as well as the space nearby.

Boom, boom, boom.

The flames exploded, clearing out some clouds, but a second later, the thick fog instantly filled it back in.

Yun Zhong was nowhere to be seen.

Arrows shot out from the clouds, not only were they continuous, but they also shot from every direction.

The audience cheered.

With this, Lord Yun Zhong will bring down Long Fu!”

“Don’t tell me this is how I lose?”

“Looking on helplessly as the empire takes away the Godhead?”

Qi Ci Lang was also obstructed, and unable to get closer.

Qi Ci Lang was bewildered, but didn’t forget to bite back at the Ice Sculpture King, “What qualifications do you have to ridicule me? I am only 50 steps from the Godhead, while you are over 100.”

The Ice Sculpture King coldly snorted, and after a moment of silence, he clenched his teeth in firm resolution.

A moment later, he twisted his alchemy device and vanished, returning to the top of Tranquil Hill.

The duelists were in the process of wantonly slaughtering the imperial secret spies.

In the distant skies, the saint level shield guardian was entangled with Honey Snow Tower.

The Ice Sculpture King was in a terrible mood as he swept his eyes around, landing on Long Meng.

Qi Ci Lang’s killer words stung Long Meng.

Chapter 967: The Dueling Godhead is Mine!

“Who goes there?!”

The Dueling Godhead stirred!

The death divinity attracted it, and it floated towards Teacher Si Ling.

The death divinity simultaneously used formless power to pull it in.

After both rushed towards each other, the death divinity touched the Dueling Godhead and was absorbed by the latter.

This scene stupefied the other three.

Or was it that even legend levels were not enough to access the greatest secrets of Godhead?

Qi Ci Lang taunted, “Ah haha, truly tragic.”

“As a saint level, obtaining divinity isn’t easy.”

“A true pity, four portions of divinity isn’t enough to obtain the Dueling Godhead. Your investment has come to naught, hahaha.”

Just as he laughed, Qi Ci Lang’s body suddenly surged with a divine aura.

This was not the Dueling Godhead’s aura, but that of other gods.

As Qi Ci Lang gazed with amazement, a drop of dark green divinity separated itself from his body.

“Divinity? How can my body have divinity in it?!” Qi Ci Lang stroked his chest with extreme puzzlement.

The divinity and the Godhead were attracted to each other.

Thus, the Dueling Godhead traveled 10 steps towards Qi Ci Lang.

Qi Ci Lang’s body had the Beloved bloodline. This kind of bloodline, this bloodline naturally agreed with and could bear the weight of many divine powers, thus, he often obtained the gods’ favor and doting.

Qi Ci Lang had taken part in many gods’ large religious festivals and obtained the divine blessings of different gods many times. Among the strongest of them was the seven Immortality Blessings. One had to meet seven different requirements to break these blessings, and only then could he be slain.

Because of this, he had the nicknames Seven Lives and Beloved Child of Seven Gods.

Although Long Meng had killed Qi Ci Lang more than seven times, he didn’t meet the requirements to break the blessings, so his murders were wasted efforts. He had to resolve the seven divine blessings first before he could reap Qi Ci Lang’s life.

“So, as it turns out, my Immortality Blessings were woven with divinity.” Qi Ci Lang realized at that moment what the empire’s exquisite plan was.

He was overjoyed at this unexpected news, “Good, the Dueling Godhead is mine!”

He was only 50 steps away from the Dueling Godhead initially. He had seven divinities on him, enough for him to reach the Dueling Godhead.

But Teacher Si Ling had just used 4 Death Divinities, pulling the Dueling Godhead away from its original spot, and farther from Qi Ci Lang.

There were roughly 60 steps between them.

This was because Teacher Si Ling, the Dueling Godhead, and Qi Ci Lang were not in a straight line to each other. Although Teacher Si Ling and Qi Ci Lang were opposite to each other, the straight line between them and the Godhead formed an oblique angle.

Even so, based on Qi Ci Lang’s assessment, with his Immortality Blessings converted into 7 divinities, he could bring the Godhead 70 steps towards him. 70 steps was greater than 60, therefore, he would definitely pull the Godhead towards him.

“I win, the greatest winner here is me, only me!!” Qi Ci Lang laughed up a storm, utterly pleased with himself.

Teacher Si Ling was silent.

The Ice Sculpture King loudly roared, “No—!

Long Meng rigidly clenched his teeth, extremely unreconciled.

“Who?!”

“Who are you?”

“Hahaha, you’ve too slow.”

As soon as he saw the Dueling Godhead approaching Qi Ci Lang, the dragonman youngster took out the [Mermaid Fairy Tale] and threw out a death divinity.

The Godhead was attracted to it, pulling away from Qi Ci Lang.

Qi Ci Lang: …

The others: ?!!

Long Meng stared, “That mysterious person has divinity too?”

The king grabbed his crown, threw it to the ground, and ruthlessly stomped, utterly devoid of noble demeanor, “Why does it seem like every competitor came prepared with divinity?!”

Qi Ci Lang clenched his teeth, the Dueling God was further from him, but he still had hope.

Because the dragonman youngster and he weren’t in a straight line, they were relatively opposite to a certain degree.

Qi Ci Lang had seven divinities.

He threw out another divinity.

The dragonman youngster kept pace.

Qi Ci Lang was startled, and he furiously roared, throwing out his last one.

The dragonman youngster had tossed out three Death Divinities.

Qi Ci Lang seemed to fall into an icy hole.

It was finished, he did his utmost to reach out, but the Godhead was still a step away.

The dragonman youngster threw out his final divinity.

Thus, the Dueling Godhead almost brushed past Qi Ci Lang.

“No—!” Qi Ci Lang screeched to the heavens, his voice filled with sorrow, ire, loathing, and powerlessness.

The Godhead grew 10 steps closer to the dragoman youngster; it was only two steps away.

The young man’s divinity was exhausted.

Long Meng keenly sensed this, and he blurted out, “Their divinity is used up!”

Qi Ci Lang’s sorrowful screech came to a sudden stop, then he coughed intensely.

“Hahaha, you failed too!” The Ice Sculpture King loudly derided, extremely irritated.

Teacher Si Ling ferociously glared at the dragonman youngster, “How can this be? He also took out death divinity, and all four of them are just like mine?!”

“I am also the Dueling God’s holy warrior.”
