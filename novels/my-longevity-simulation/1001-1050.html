Chapter 1001: Tianxuan Mirror Establishes the Dao

"I have seen many characters like you."

"But most of them did not live long and died miserably for various reasons."

"I am somewhat curious: how have you managed to survive intact until now?"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone showed no regard for Li Fan's face and spoke bluntly.

His eyes were full of curiosity.

Li Fan's face twitched.

A surge of killing intent suddenly arose in his heart.

However, considering that the other party was, after all, the Tianxuan Mirror clone, who knew countless secrets, and that there might be a key to breaking the situation hidden among them...

Li Fan could only forcibly suppress his killing urge.

"Oh, by the way. Ordinary soul-searching or treasure-refining methods are useless against me."

"No one in this Xuanhuang Realm can force me to say what I do not wish to reveal." The Tianxuan Mirror clone glanced at Li Fan again and explained in advance once more.

"Your tone is quite bold..." Although Li Fan said this, he did not doubt the words.

After all, whether it was the Xuantian King back then or the later Immortal Ancestor.

Neither seemed to have fully controlled this immortal artifact.

Li Fan had once entertained the idea of trying the [Xuantian Spirit-Sealing] Formation, but from his interactions with the Tianxuan Mirror clone over this period, this clone had already completely detached from the Tianxuan Mirror and existed as a thoroughly independent entity.

Formations targeted at the Tianxuan Mirror would be useless on him.

"What exactly are you now?" Li Fan deliberately said to provoke him.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone did not grow angry; instead, a rare trace of smugness appeared on his face.

"I possess both the weight of the artifact and the spirit of man."

"Flawless and near to True Immortal..."

Before he could finish speaking, Li Fan interjected with cold mockery: "So, a mongrel."

The expression on the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face suddenly froze.

He quickly recovered and looked at Li Fan, shaking his head slightly: "As the saying goes, a summer insect cannot discuss ice."

"But to speak frankly, if you had the chance to undergo my kind of transformation, you would likely make the same choice as I did." The Tianxuan Mirror clone mused thoughtfully.

Li Fan did not respond and remained silent.

After a long while, he finally uttered: "It seems you truly are not afraid of death."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone smiled leisurely: "You have seen through it. The laws of living beings vary endlessly. Since I wish to experience a complete human life, I must naturally go through birth, aging, illness, and death."

"After this body dies, another will naturally be born."

"Unless you can destroy my main body, how can you truly kill me?"

"Death is merely a return to freedom for me!"

Li Fan savored these words carefully. After a while, he said slowly: "It seems you did not simply divide out a human body. Rather, you have stepped out onto your own [Dao]."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone applauded delightedly, as if he had finally met a kindred spirit.

"It appears the plan to kill the clone and sever your path is doomed to failure."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone smiled: "I pondered bitterly for nearly ten thousand years before finding this path to transcendence. If it were so simple that merely locating and slaying the clone could break it..."

"Would that not be somewhat too dismissive of me?"

The smile on the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face vanished abruptly.

At this moment, he finally revealed the domineering aura befitting an immortal artifact.

"It seems all the cultivators of the world have underestimated you. Yet you so candidly reveal this vital secret to me? Are you not afraid I will spread it abroad?" Li Fan narrowed his eyes.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone also narrowed his eyes slightly: "To pluck one hair and benefit the world, yet you would not do so. For such a vital secret, how could you possibly spread it lightly? You must seek some benefit in exchange..."

"Moreover, since I chose to tell you, I naturally have no fear of any true leak affecting me. My Dao is achieved; what is there to fear?"

Li Fan gazed at the Tianxuan Mirror clone's narrowed eyes, his expression returning to normal: "Actually, I think the cold, mechanical voice of your main body suits you better." Li Fan suggested earnestly.

The smug expression on the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face disappeared at once.

"I was originally planning to share some news that might interest you, given my good mood. But now, it seems that will not happen. Go ahead and kill me." The Tianxuan Mirror clone adopted an utterly fearless demeanor and said coldly.

Li Fan still did not strike.

"Strictly speaking, you and I are actually the same kind of being," Li Fan said slowly after a moment of silence and contemplation.

"You originated from the Immortal Realm. Even if the Immortal Realm suffered great calamity and caused you to fall to the lower world, an immortal artifact remains incomparable to ordinary immortal cultivators."

"So you have never truly regarded the myriad beings of the Xuanhuang Realm as equals. Even if temporarily controlled, ten thousand years is but a fleeting moment to you."

"You have already found a way to escape that control."

"In great spirits, you tour the mortal world. Others think you are comprehending the Dao, but in truth, you are savoring the joy of achievement."

"Your so-called imitation of humans is merely a form of amusement. Your heart is filled with mocking observation of the human world, yet you have never truly intended to integrate."

"Because they are unworthy."

With each word from Li Fan, the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face grew more solemn.

He did not interrupt Li Fan's speech but listened attentively and carefully.

"The divide between immortals and mortals is like an abyssal chasm. Even as merely an immortal artifact, you bear the character [Immortal], so I suppose you are fundamentally different from us cultivators."

"Perhaps in the entire Xuanhuang Realm, only the Xuantian King of old, or perhaps the Immortal Ancestor, would earn a measure of your regard."

"As for the world's myriad disturbances, the ebb and flow of seas and crumbling of stones, you care little."

"Because as an immortal artifact, you have nearly transcended time itself. Even if another ten thousand years pass and the Xuanhuang Realm undergoes upheaval, or even if the Xuanhuang Realm falls into the Immortal Ruins, it would likely cause you no substantive harm."

...

This lengthy discourse continued, with Li Fan speaking freely as if without end.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone finally seemed somewhat impatient.

He asked: "What exactly are you trying to say?"

Li Fan promptly fell silent.

Then he smiled abruptly: "What am I trying to say?"

"Or rather, what do you not wish to hear?"

"Everything I said was merely a probe."

"I was testing what truly matters to you. And I was fortunate enough to find the answer."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone no longer appeared as carefree as before.

"I want to know, if the Xuanhuang Realm truly falls into the Immortal Ruins..."

"Will the Tianxuan Mirror's main body truly be affected?"

Li Fan restrained his smile and asked earnestly.

"You..." The Tianxuan Mirror clone's eyes flickered.

"Are you not skilled at reading people? If I say that I could, and would, drag the Xuanhuang Realm into the Immortal Ruins with me..."

"Would your insight prove true or false?"

Li Fan asked with utmost calm.

Chapter 1002: Tianxuan Jailbreak Plan

With Li Fan's question, the inn fell into dead silence once more.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone stared fixedly at Li Fan, as if trying to see through this uninvited guest.

But the more he observed, the more shock and doubt filled his face.

It seemed the other party's shocking words of world-ending calamity were not mere bluster!

At this moment, the Tianxuan Mirror clone suddenly realized what the earlier statement "actually, we are the same kind of people" truly meant.

The same top-down indifference toward the beings of the Xuanhuang Realm!

What puzzled the Tianxuan Mirror clone was that he, as an immortal artifact, naturally had the qualification for such a view. Yet this cultivator before him was merely at the Nascent Soul stage; on what basis did he claim the same?

Even more inconceivable to the Tianxuan Mirror clone was how casually the other party spoke of dragging the Xuanhuang Realm down with him. If the Immortal Ruins above descended, even he, an immortal artifact, might not escape unscathed.

The other party would surely perish as well, yet he showed no trace of fear...

"To achieve his ends, he spares no means. Even willing to perish together..."

"Could I have misjudged him? This is not a thoroughly selfish person, but a madman?"

In an instant, countless thoughts flashed through the Tianxuan Mirror clone's mind.

Finally, he let out a long breath, breaking the silence: "I never expected I would truly misjudge you."

"A dangerous figure like you, unbound by any taboo, should be eternally suppressed in the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance's [Nether Prison]."

Li Fan chuckled, unconcerned: "Nether Prison... In this world, there is no place that can confine me."

"Not even this Darkest Star Sea."

At these words, the Tianxuan Mirror clone's expression shifted slightly once more.

"Between us, it is not merely a resemblance. Even our desires align: I seek to escape this cage, and you wish to break free of your shackles..."

"By the way, is merely splitting off this so-called human clone truly your Dao? Is it true freedom?"

"Without your main body, what difference is there between your clone and those fragile mortals? Both are ants crushed with a single pinch. Freedom..." Li Fan sneered.

Gazing at Li Fan, the Tianxuan Mirror clone ultimately offered no rebuttal.

In this clash of wits, he had truly been defeated.

The root cause was his inability to discern the true identity of the man before him.

He even speculated whether the other was a surviving immortal from the ancient Immortal Realm.

"If it is truly as you say, then cooperation between us is not impossible," the Tianxuan Mirror clone finally said after much deliberation.

"But in the end, I still cannot trust you." Yet he quickly changed tack.

Li Fan waved his hand, revealing a friendly smile: "I can understand your mindset. But our cooperation can proceed step by step, gradually building trust between us."

"That way, even if I renege midway, you will have reaped sufficient benefits."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone considered this; the words seemed reasonable, so he nodded slowly.

"The restrictions the Immortal Ancestor placed upon me back then, accumulated over these years by successive Mirror Holders, have now reached a terrifying level. With your current cultivation, even aided by those three Dao Integration enforcers outside, rescuing me is utterly impossible..."

Li Fan unceremoniously interrupted the Tianxuan Mirror clone: "You need not worry about my strength."

"Since I could locate you, you should know I have already infiltrated the forces of Mirror Holder Cang."

"And among the Dharma-Transmitters, we have our own people. Hmph, after millennia of development, how could the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance remain an ironclad monolith!"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone hesitated upon hearing this: "Even so, helping me escape control seems a bit forced, does it not?"

Li Fan did not answer directly but suddenly asked: "Do you know why I originally came to slay you?"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone pondered for a moment before replying: "Sever my retreat?"

"Indeed. But now that your Dao has been achieved, it is impossible to prevent you from escaping via your clone. Thus, the plan has changed. The Xuantian King, Xuanyuan Hong: do you still remember this man?"

"Xuanyuan Hong?" The Tianxuan Mirror clone narrowed his eyes and thought for a while.

It seemed that the division of the clone had caused some loss of memories, or perhaps the human body simply could not support the retrieval of vast amounts of information.

Only after a long pause did he recall, exclaiming in surprise: "You are from the Xuantian Sect?"

"Impossible; the Xuantian Sect should not have someone like you..."

Li Fan snorted coldly: "We wish to know the current whereabouts of the Xuantian King from those days. If anyone in this Xuanhuang Realm could still hold information on him, it would surely be you. The original plan was to slay your clone, sever your retreat, and then use an enhanced version of the Xuantian Spirit Sealing to force you into cooperation with us..."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone was stunned: "I was once the Xuantian Sect's guardian artifact, after all. Now that I am trapped by the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance, you do not rescue me: that is one thing. But to devise such a scheme?"

Li Fan sneered: "Back then, our Xuantian Sect was defeated, and the Xuantian King vanished without a trace. It all happened too quickly, too suspiciously. And now, with you having submitted to the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance, who can say for certain that you were not the traitor from those days!"

"I am the traitor?" These words seemed to enrage the Tianxuan Mirror clone; his voice rose slightly.

"Back then..." He was about to say more but suddenly stopped.

"Back then..."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone racked his brains, searching his mind for memories of the Xuantian Sect's downfall, yet he found nothing.

"This is classified information; only the main body has access," the Tianxuan Mirror clone said gravely.

Li Fan responded with a mocking laugh.

"Since your demand is to find the missing Xuantian King, then yes, there is indeed a basis for cooperation between us."

"I still hold some trust toward those people from the Xuantian Sect back then. But you..." The Tianxuan Mirror clone eyed Li Fan with suspicion.

"Have you never seen cases where a millennium changes one's temperament?" Li Fan dismissed the need to explain.

"The revised preliminary plan is as follows: within the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance, continue constructing the [Xuantian Spirit Sealing] under the pretext of fully mastering the Tianxuan Mirror, and build the grand formation."

"In truth, substitute the core of the formation: change it from control to release."

"Then, using your authority within the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance, create an opportunity for escape. In this, I can even coordinate with the Five Elders Association and the forces of various small worlds to launch a siege on the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance and sow chaos."

Li Fan spoke freely, inadvertently revealing the immense power behind his words.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone listened while analyzing.

In the end, he was moved.

For, in theory, this ultimate "jailbreak plan" was indeed feasible.

Chapter 1003: The Immortal Ancestor’S Decree Determines Tianxuan

In the end, the Immortal Ancestor, whom he feared most, had temporarily left the Xuanhuang Realm.

Those below, relying on the methods and transmission left by him back then, lorded over him.

But he had created a human clone on his own and achieved a form of freedom in some sense. If powerful external aid were added...

As he indulged in these flights of fancy, Li Fan uttered another startling remark.

"That was the first option just now."

"In truth, I have a second option that might help you achieve true freedom."

"Oh?" The Tianxuan Mirror clone looked at Li Fan with some surprise.

"The Xuantian Treasure Mirror measures heaven and gauges earth. Now, you share myriad ties with the Xuanhuang Realm. Though you call yourself an immortal artifact, you cannot deny that your power is ultimately constrained by this current heaven and earth."

"Right or wrong?"

"The Xuanhuang Realm has expanded a hundredfold in these years; surely you have reaped considerable benefits from it?"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone fell silent, offering no denial.

The knowledge this man before him possessed far exceeded his expectations.

"Perhaps cooperating with him truly is not a bad thing." This thought suddenly arose in the Tianxuan Mirror clone's mind.

"Now, the worlds in the surrounding star regions have nearly all been plundered clean. The Xuanhuang Realm's expansion has hit a bottleneck, and your own deadline approaches."

"However, what you currently control is only the Xuanhuang main realm. If all the small worlds plundered over these years were brought within your measuring scope..."

"Would you not advance further?" Li Fan tempted.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone considered this and admitted frankly: "In truth, I have long planned this over the years. But on one hand, the project is far too vast in scale. On the other, the lineage of Mirror Holders in the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance has always insisted on limiting my main body's power. Thus, this plan has never been successfully implemented..."

"If I were to scheme it, it would surely succeed and help you ascend," Li Fan said with full confidence.

"By then, with the power you could command, even if the Xuanhuang collapsed, you could likely withdraw unscathed, right?"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone watched as the other spoke with utter conviction, a flicker of doubt passing through his eyes.

"Could it be that he possesses a supreme treasure exceptionally skilled in deduction?"

In the Tianxuan Mirror clone's memory, there existed in the Xuanhuang Realm a deduction realm artifact only slightly inferior to himself. Thus, he instinctively thought in that direction.

Even though the Tianxuan Mirror was once an immortal artifact, it had never seen or even heard of a treasure like [Truth], one capable of turning the illusory into the real and reversing all things. Thus, it never imagined that Li Fan's seemingly unfathomable depths stemmed simply from having experienced enough.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone pondered for a moment and, between the two options Li Fan proposed, ultimately chose the first.

He explained: "The second scheme would take too long to execute and introduce too many variables."

"As for the first scheme..."

"Even if you ultimately fail, as long as it creates the slightest looseness for me." A flash of confidence gleamed in the Tianxuan Mirror clone's eyes.

"Given a bit more time, I can free myself."

Seeing this, Li Fan could not help but applaud: "Very well, since we have reached an agreement. I believe we can proceed with our cooperation."

"Between us, you do not trust me, and I do not trust you. So-called cooperation relies entirely on the exchange of interests. But in my view, this is often the most solid form in the world. For oaths and agreements can be betrayed. Yet no one betrays their own interests. At least..."

"Between you and me, that is the case." Li Fan smiled as he appraised the Tianxuan Mirror clone.

"Then, next, I must implement the first step of the plan. Deriving and creating a formation to loosen the restriction binding you..."

"So, what exactly is the Immortal Ancestor's restriction?"

Faced with this question, the Tianxuan Mirror clone did not fall into a brief stupor from accessing vast data this time. Instead, as if etched into the depths of his soul, he blurted out: "Intertwined roots and knotted branches, penetrating to the marrow. Rooted in the Xuanhuang heaven and earth, fused as one!"

Li Fan immediately narrowed his eyes.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone paused, then sighed faintly: "During the Xuantian Sect era, I measured the Xuanhuang heaven and earth, able to observe and discern the vast majority of events in time. Precisely because I hung high in the ninth heaven, aloof from all things."

"But after the Immortal Ancestor's restriction, I fused into the Xuanhuang Realm's heaven and earth. In turn, I lost that divine ability. After all, it is merely rooting and integration, without grasping the true authority of heaven and earth."

"Unless I can seize heaven and replace it, otherwise..." The Tianxuan Mirror clone shook his head in some frustration.

"So, helping you escape is no easy task after all," Li Fan echoed.

"Indeed, and this is merely the initial restriction the Immortal Ancestor imposed on me back then. Over these years of the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance's rule, the line of Mirror Holders has ceaselessly devised ways to strengthen their control over me. Though their methods fall far short of the Ancestor's, they have accumulated in quantity."

"Even shit, piled up, forms a mountain." Perhaps due to deep-seated resentment, the Tianxuan Mirror clone uttered such crude words.

"If there comes a day when I break free, before departing, I will surely settle accounts with them completely!" he said with hatred.

Seeing him devoid of the immortal artifact's otherworldly poise and detachment, Li Fan smiled without replying.

"I have considerable study in the way of formations. Tell me the detailed information on the Immortal Ancestor's restriction. Give me some time, and I should find a way to crack it." Li Fan made no guarantees.

Since the two sides had already reached a preliminary cooperation agreement, there was no need to conceal this matter.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone immediately transmitted the relevant information.

The method of transmission was quite unique; he directly snapped off one of his fingers.

Then, the finger became transparent and gleaming like a glass mirror, with light leaping ceaselessly within, as if shimmering with countless pieces of information.

Looking again at the Tianxuan Mirror clone, the severed finger had quickly regenerated.

"Worthy of the Tianxuan Mirror's incarnation; it is indeed far from a true human body." Witnessing this, Li Fan sighed inwardly.

At the same time, he accepted the transparent finger.

He began to read the information.

The massive volume of data surged in abruptly, causing Li Fan's thoughts to stall slowly for a moment.

Only then did they gradually resume operation.

Even though it was a restriction left by the Immortal Ancestor thousands of years ago, Li Fan had to admit it was exquisitely profound and incomparably complex.

The data stream was continuously absorbed and transformed by Li Fan.

The Dao Deviation Stone and the Final Dissociation Disc began to collaborate in cracking it.

"These are merely the parts I can perceive. In the unseen places, there may exist other special restrictions," the Tianxuan Mirror clone reminded kindly.

"Rest assured; it should not be a major issue. However..."

"As part of the exchange, since I have begun acting, should you not offer some benefit?" Li Fan suddenly asked.

"What do you want?" The Tianxuan Mirror clone looked at Li Fan.

"Hmm..." Li Fan feigned contemplation for a moment.

Then he said: "I need to derive formations, which requires references for formations."

"How about the Floating Starry Sky Formation?"

"Floating Starry Sky Formation?" The Tianxuan Mirror clone stood frozen in place for a moment.

Then he shook his head: "Although this formation is already damaged, it still concerns the safety of Xuanhuang. Only the main body holds the memories."

Li Fan felt a twinge of regret upon hearing this. But he had not held much hope for it anyway; it was merely a casual mention.

Just as he was about to propose another request, the Tianxuan Mirror clone continued: "However, if you simply want a formation of comparable level as a reference for breaking arrays, I do have others to offer."

Li Fan's interest piqued at once: "Oh? For example?"

The Tianxuan Mirror clone paused briefly to gather his thoughts, then broke off another finger and handed it over.

Perhaps due to the larger volume of information being transmitted this time, the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face paled somewhat.

His body also grew feeble, and he leaned against the wall before slowly sitting down.

Li Fan paused his analysis of the transmission prohibition and instead absorbed the contents of the new finger.

In an instant, his sea of consciousness felt as if it were stuffed and churning with some invisible force. Li Fan grunted softly, his figure swaying uncontrollably.

Pressing his throbbing temples, Li Fan tried to convert the formation's contents as quickly as possible while alleviating the pressure in his sea of consciousness.

Meanwhile, the Tianxuan Mirror clone, seated not far away, wore an expression of schadenfreude.

Clearly, he had withheld any prior warning just to watch Li Fan make a fool of himself.

But soon, the sneer on the Tianxuan Mirror clone's face gradually turned to solemnity.

For he discovered that Li Fan's rate of adaptation far exceeded his expectations.

It must be understood that the Tianxuan Mirror clone was not toying with him without cause. Based on the information processing ability Li Fan had displayed while converting the Immortal Ancestor's prohibition earlier, he had deliberately selected an immortal-level formation from his memories.

It was just enough to cause Li Fan some trouble without truly bursting his head.

Yet what caught the Tianxuan Mirror clone off guard was that, in merely this short span of time, Li Fan's information processing capacity seemed to have improved substantially.

Moreover, the rate of improvement was visibly accelerating.

"This... exactly..."

The Tianxuan Mirror clone felt a mix of shock and doubt, his curiosity burning intensely. If not for the agreement between them and the disparity in strength, he might have split Li Fan's head open on the spot to see what secrets lay within.

And in Li Fan's sea of consciousness, beyond the Tianxuan Mirror clone's perception.

The Final Dissociation Disc was undergoing unimaginable, violent changes.

"Astral Mechanism, All Spirits Return to Numbers." Li Fan repeated the name of the formation transmitted by the Tianxuan Mirror clone in his mind, filled with delight.

Worthy of a great formation transcending the mortal dust.

Even though the Final Dissociation Disc had yet to fully decipher it, merely receiving the various data of the formation had already triggered changes within itself.

To elaborate, the Final Dissociation Disc had already pushed the standard formation paradigms circulating in the immortal cultivation world, which Li Fan had mastered, to their utmost limits.

The path ahead was blocked; there was nothing left to derive.

The Final Dissociation Disc was, after all, not a living thing; it lacked its own intelligence.

It could not, like a true formation grandmaster, create something from nothing or forge a new path from desperation.

It required new theories, new formation architectures.

And now, this [Astral Mechanism, All Spirits Return to Numbers] great formation had opened a brand-new window for the Final Dissociation Disc.

Astral Mechanism seemed to represent one segment of a certain set of formations.

By its name, the formation envelops everything within it, converting all phenomena into digital expressions.

Realms, including destructive power, divine sense intensity, physical body strength, and so on, are all presented as concrete numerical values.

At first glance, this formation seems unremarkable.

Yet immortal-level formation often excel at turning the mundane into the miraculous in unexpected ways.

Once digitized, all digital expressions within the formation can be freely adjusted.

As long as they remain within the formation's operational limits, there are no restrictions; changes occur at will.

And the ultimate effect is...

Even an ordinary mortal without the strength to truss a chicken, after adjustment by the [Astral Mechanism, All Spirits Return to Numbers] Great Formation, can leap to become an existence whose combat power rivals that of an immortal!

Likewise, even an immortal falling into the formation, if unable to break free, will have their values adjusted in an instant, losing the cultivation accumulated over countless years and reduced to a mere mortal!

It sounds absurd, yet this very absurdity underscores the might of an immortal-level formation!

Li Fan had never imagined that an formation could achieve such an outrageous feat. His heart trembled profoundly.

"If I had this great formation in hand..."

But he did not let sudden elation cloud his judgment.

After fully accepting and analyzing the information on the Astral Mechanism, All Spirits Return to Numbers, Li Fan felt a profound sense of helplessness, like gazing at the vast ocean.

For the [Astral Mechanism, All Spirits Return to Numbers] was simply not something a mundane immortal cultivator could successfully deploy.

Beyond the immortal spiritual energy required to operate the formation, even the myriad materials needed to construct it were things Li Fan had never heard of.

The Final Dissociation Disc was powerful, yet it could only derive the formation theoretically, without manifesting it in reality.

Fortunately, this immortal-level formation had indeed opened a new domain of formation derivation for the Dissociation Disc.

The formation derivation process, which had gradually stagnated due to having nothing left to derive, began to accelerate once more.

An infinite future thus unfolded.

...

It was not mere pretense on Li Fan's part. He had already reaped benefits from extracting truth from the other, and with countless more gains visible in the future, he regarded the Tianxuan Mirror as a treasure trove he could mine at will. Naturally, he needed to stay vigilant about the other's well-being.

Hearing Li Fan's "brother," the Tianxuan Mirror clone instantly broke out in goosebumps.

With a start, he rose from the ground and hastily assured that he was fine.

Only then did Li Fan nod in relief.

"With this Return to Numbers Great Formation as reference, my confidence in helping you escape has increased further."

"No time like the present; I will contact and begin preparations at once."

"You stay here and do not wander, lest some accident occur."

Li Fan instructed.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone had intended to tour the mortal world and experience human nature anyway; remaining in this mortal city posed no issue. He nodded in agreement.

After discussing potential contingencies and details for the days ahead, just before departing, Li Fan suddenly asked: "Brother, do you have any token?"

Chapter 1005: Tianxuan Mirror’S Origin

The Tianxuan Mirror clone, upon hearing those words, pondered for a moment, then extended his right hand into his own body.

From within, he drew out a glowing orb that pulsed rhythmically, as if it were breathing, and handed it over.

“Carry this with you, and it will serve as my representation,” the Tianxuan Mirror clone said solemnly.

“What is this?” Li Fan reached out to take the orb, but his hand passed right through it.

“This is the Mirror’s Origin. It was born together with my true body. It neither increases nor diminishes, neither arises nor perishes. Outsiders cannot touch or absorb it; only I can summon and control it.”

As he spoke, the Tianxuan Mirror clone pointed into the air, sending the orb of Mirror Origin directly into Li Fan’s body.

“Brother, you are not planning to use this Mirror Origin to spy on me, are you?” Li Fan raised an eyebrow and asked immediately.

A hint of offended anger appeared on the Tianxuan Mirror clone’s face. “This is merely the original mark born with my true form. Without additional power infused into it, how could I possibly use it for surveillance?”

“Moreover, if you truly do not trust me, you may seal the Origin within your body. As long as it resides in you, it will serve as my representative…”

Before he could finish speaking, he sensed that Li Fan had already, within a single breath, completely sealed off the Origin.

Only a faint and nearly imperceptible connection remained, proving its existence.

The Tianxuan Mirror clone’s expression stiffened.

But seeing that Li Fan’s demeanor remained calm and unbothered, he took a deep breath and steadied himself.

After a moment, he added, “With this Origin, you can place complete trust in the Mirror Holder, Cang.”

“Hmph, but that man was not loyal. During the long years he spent monitoring me, he developed ulterior motives. He sought to use a secret technique to seize greater control over me, hoping to rise above all Mirror Holder.”

“Unfortunately for him, such petty schemes could never escape my sight. I pretended to fall into his trap step by step, only to strike back at the final moment and turn him into my Mirror Slave.”

“Otherwise, acquiring this human body would not have been so easy for me,” the Tianxuan Mirror clone said with pride.

“Oh? So that’s how it is? Excellent. That increases the success rate of my plan by at least fifty percent!” Li Fan said with genuine admiration.

“Time is of the essence. I will depart immediately.” Li Fan clasped his hands respectfully and then vanished from the Tianxuan Mirror clone’s sight.

“I hope he succeeds,” the Tianxuan Mirror clone muttered softly.

In truth, he had little real trust in Li Fan. This was merely an experiment.

As Li Fan had said, their cooperation would only deepen as they continued to work together.

If Li Fan truly managed to create a strong enough influence, the clone would not mind adding another source of support.

But if the plan were to collapse halfway…

He would lose nothing at all.

The Mirror’s Origin could not be taken by anyone. As for the so-called Great Formation Astral Mechanism of All-Spirits Return to Numbers or Mirror Holder Cang…

To him, they were entirely insignificant.

With that thought, the Tianxuan Mirror clone began to hum a little tune, once again playing the role of a busy shop attendant.

Chapter 1048: Five Years Of Change

Nine Mountains Prefecture.

After five years of development, this sole free trade prefecture within the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance had undergone earth-shaking changes.

From its former desolation and emptiness, it had become prosperous and bustling.

In every direction, the vast plains were entirely occupied by cultivators' cave dwellings, encircling the central Nine Mountains Trade City.

After several expansions, the trade city's scale had grown nearly tenfold from its initial size. Yet it still could not meet the ever-increasing trade demands.

Every newly arrived cultivator in the city wore an expression full of excitement and curiosity, lost amid the dazzling array of goods that left them overwhelmed.

Zhao Xian knelt on the ground and respectfully bowed to the Faceless Stone Statue shrouded in shadows above.

He then exited the secret chamber, opened the shop door, and began his first business of the day after restocking.

There was no need for him to actively promote; before long, the shop was crammed with cultivators eager to shop.

The introductions and prices for all goods were clearly marked, so the cultivators could complete their purchases on their own without the shopkeeper's personal involvement.

There was no haggling, for in Nine Mountains Prefecture, goods were often snapped up the moment they were put on display.

This was partly because everything sold here consisted of rare specialties from various prefectures.

But the root cause was...

Zhao Xian maintained a full, welcoming smile on his face while sighing inwardly: "No wonder everyone wants to come to Nine Mountains Prefecture now. They say even a pig can make a fortune just by lying around, and it truly does not deceive."

"Three years ago, the Tianxuan Mirror's trading function suddenly shut down, preventing cultivators from trading freely. But trade demands cannot simply vanish, so in a short time, five major trade cities emerged across the world to barely take over the Tianxuan Mirror's trading role."

"Among them, Nine Mountains Prefecture is the largest in scale and has the most goods."

"Not only do cultivators from the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance's provinces come here to trade, but even those from the Five Elders Association sell their specialties and buy goods here."

Looking at the endless stream of customers in his shop, Zhao Xian's heart filled with gratitude: "Thanks to the emmisary's help, otherwise, a minor Nascent Soul cultivator like me from Congyun Sea could never afford a property here."

Although half the profits had to be handed over, even so, the speed of earning contribution points was far faster than back home.

"As long as I save for another ten years, I can not only pay off all my debts but even buy a Soul Transformation technique and [Heavenly Life-Transformation of Birth] at a discount from the Alliance!"

"If I can achieve Soul Transformation, my status in the future will be entirely different!"

"Even in the worst case, returning to Congyun Sea as a guest elder would be guaranteed. After all, the entire Congyun Sea has only two Soul Transformation cultivators in charge."

He did not know how much time had passed when the successful sale of one displayed item suddenly snapped him awake.

It was an apparently ordinary white jade statue. A simple decorative piece with no special functions attached. If one had to say what made this white jade statue special, it would be the appearance of the carved woman.

An otherworldly beauty, lifelike and vivid.

However, because the asking price was outrageously high, at two hundred thousand contribution points, it had sat unsold in the shop for over two full years.

And today, someone had actually taken the initiative to buy it. One must know, this statue was tied to the task assigned by the emmisary!

Zhao Xian suppressed the agitation in his heart and hurriedly stepped forward: "Fellow Daoist, please wait a moment!"

A cultivator dressed in a black Daoist robe, hidden in the shadows with his face obscured, paused at his feet.

However, he paid no attention to Zhao Xian and left without looking back.

Zhao Xian did not pursue him.

“My Lord Emissary, someone has bought the white jade statue.”

After finishing his prayer, Zhao Xian waited patiently.

“I understand.”

After just that one sentence, there was silence once more.

But Zhao Xian keenly sensed the holy aura enveloping him descend and then vanish.

And his cultivation had suddenly surged without warning.

Joy overflowed on his face. Zhao Xian hurriedly kowtowed several more times.

“With the Lord Emissary’s bestowed grace, even with my aptitude, I fear I could reach Soul Transformation within a century.”

“Those so-called heavenly prodigies who strut about arrogantly on ordinary days are far inferior to me.”

Zhao Xian felt immensely proud in his heart and became even more diligent in handling the affairs of the Federation of All Worlds.

Spiritwood Realm.

“So it is him; he has not died after all.”

Jia Yiyuan, once a receptionist at the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance headquarters.

As for Jia Yiyuan himself, he vanished without a trace.

Who would have thought he would appear in Nine Mountains Prefecture today.

The white jade statue was personally crafted by Li Fan and indeed held nothing special.

After the anomaly at the Alliance headquarters that day, the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance launched a bloody purge.

Countless cultivators were liquidated, and even many innocent ones suffered in the crossfire.

With their profound abilities, under the Alliance’s high-pressure pursuit, they still managed to save a large number of cultivators’ lives.

But she knew the Alliance intimately; she could anticipate even the most classified orders before they were issued.

She would rescue people just before the butcher’s blade fell.

The Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance was furious and offered a bounty for her capture, yet it yielded no results.

They could not even ascertain her appearance.

The reason Li Fan knew what she looked like was because he had soul-searched a cultivator she had saved.

Unfortunately, that person knew little about his savior.

In his mind, Li Fan had only glimpsed a peerlessly beautiful visage.

Attempting to uncover some threads.

The Du'e Sect did not intend to completely ignore the outside world after transforming into paper persons.

The more severe the crisis, the greater the number of awakened paper persons.

"Every so often, the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance's Yuandao Prefecture is devoured for a decade. That is his doing."

Dan Xuan was momentarily stunned: “It has already been ten years?”

“Indeed. The closer one approaches the Immortal Ruins overhead, the slower the flow of time becomes. Back then, the Grand Mage told us that this is a spatiotemporal anomaly caused by immortals achieving enlightenment in the Dao. If one's aptitude is sufficient, one can use this spatiotemporal anomaly to reverse-engineer the immortals' enlightenment.” Dan Xuan's tone carried a hint of melancholy.

“However, none of the cultivators from our Ten Great Immortal Sects met the requirements for this resonant enlightenment in the Dao. Only the Grand Mage himself...”

“The so-called lost ten years in the Yuandao Prefecture were a manifestation of time flow reduced to its extreme. That must have been the inadvertent influence on the outside world during the Grand Mage's process of enlightenment in the Dao. Before we fell into slumber back then, the Grand Mage's enlightenment domain affected only less than a year. It seems that over these years, the Grand Mage has made gains in his enlightenment in the Dao.”

“He gave no response back then, yet now he does? And he keeps urging me to seek him...”

“You truly harbor ill intentions.” The killing intent that had faded from Li Fan's eyes reemerged.
