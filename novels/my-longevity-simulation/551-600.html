Chapter 551: Blessings and Misfortunes are Hard to Foresee

In an unknown space.

A group of cultivators, led by Long Yueyou and including Zhou Kebao, had gathered together to discuss Li Fan's recent actions.

Ever since they suffered a hidden loss at Li Fan’s hands and were forced to collaborate with him, there had been a silent resentment simmering among them. After all, they were all influential figures in their own right. Being outmaneuvered by Li Fan was a blow not only to their finances but, more importantly, to their pride.

Although they had outwardly formed a “friendly cooperative” relationship with him, this didn't stop them from secretly waiting for a chance to give Li Fan, whom they saw as a "fat sheep," a hidden blow at the right time to vent their frustration.

As such, they were keeping a close watch on Li Fan’s every move, and his recent commission of He Zhenghao to amass massive resources had not escaped their notice.

"Everyone, what do you think? Should we follow Li Fan’s lead and join in the purchases, or…?"

"Hah! You think we’ll profit by following him?" One person sneered disdainfully. "Everyone knows he’s as sly as they come. Just look at his past actions—every time he makes a big move, it’s always part of some scheme to take advantage of others."

"Remember how the loss from the Samantabhadra True Leaf incident wasn’t that long ago. Have you all forgotten already?"

"Fellow Daoist Zhu, you can’t put it that way," someone countered. "Things aren’t as they were before. Now that Li Fan holds an official position as the Secret Envoy of Congyun City, he can’t act as recklessly as before. If he were to disrupt the market or destabilize the Immortal Alliance maliciously, headquarters wouldn’t let him off easily."

This statement gained nods of agreement from many of the cultivators present.

"Exactly! In my opinion, he must have foreseen something significant to make such a large-scale move. Let’s not forget that the fundraising project hosted by He Zhenghao was something Li Fan established right after his rise. His early reputation owes much to it. I don’t believe he would tarnish his own reputation."

"Let’s follow him! With our combined wealth, surely those unruly gangs can’t compare to us!"

…

The group chattered noisily, and most of them seemed inclined to follow Li Fan’s actions, even if they harbored grudges against him. Despite their misgivings, they had to admit their respect for his abilities.

As the discussion grew lively, Zhou Kebao remained silent, frowning all the while.

Seated at the center, Long Yueyou, maintaining a dignified expression despite his faint smile, noticed Zhou Kebao’s expression and asked, "Fellow Daoist Zhou, what are your thoughts on this matter?"

Without hesitation, Zhou Kebao replied, "Without a doubt, we should purchase the same items listed by Li Fan."

Long Yueyou was slightly surprised at Zhou Kebao’s decisiveness but listened attentively as he continued.

"The prices of these items are likely to see a significant increase soon."

Zhou Kebao’s frown deepened. "But what puzzles me is this—if everything on this list goes up in price, there’s only one possible explanation."

"And that would be… a large-scale war is about to break out."

Zhou Kebao flicked his fingers, and a light screen appeared mid-air, displaying a list identical to the one Li Fan had handed to He Zhenghao.

Following Zhou Kebao’s words, the group fell silent, staring at the fluctuating characters on the screen with expressions of shock and doubt.

"A war? With the Five Elders Association? That seems unlikely. It’s been years since there was a real battle. Although tensions along the borders have risen recently, it hasn’t escalated to the point of open conflict, has it?"

"Exactly! Not long ago, the Five Elders Association blatantly seized all of Ten Thousand Immortal Island, and we still endured it. If war does break out, it would have to be an event of unimaginable magnitude," most of the cultivators present responded skeptically.

"Has anyone heard any suspicious rumors lately? If war were imminent, we would have seen at least some preparations," Long Yueyou asked, his tone serious.

Silence fell momentarily.

Then, the cultivators each took out their communication talismans and started to gather information through their own channels.

But one by one, they shook their heads, finding no unusual intelligence.

"Strange…" Zhou Kebao murmured, his confusion deepening.

The cultivators looked to Long Yueyou, awaiting his final decision.

Long Yueyou narrowed his eyes, pondering. He recalled his past dealings with Li Fan and sensed that he could never quite see through the man.

With a plan forming in his mind, Long Yueyou spoke, "Better safe than sorry. Li Fan does have some skill."

"He must have foreseen something important. Let’s follow his lead this time."

Seeing some lingering hesitation among the group, Long Yueyou smiled reassuringly. "Feel free to go ahead and make your purchases. I’ve reviewed the list. While these items aren’t commonly needed, their prices are stable, and they can be stored for long periods."

"Even if this turns out to be another trap set by Li Fan, we won’t suffer much loss."

"Of course, if any of you still have concerns…"

"If any unexpected situations arise, our Long Clan will buy all of your items at your purchase price."

"I assure you, you won’t suffer any loss," Long Yueyou declared solemnly.

The crowd was stunned.

Long Yueyou had spoken on behalf of the Long Clan, not just himself. This indicated…

Seeing their surprised and delighted expressions, Long Yueyou, full of pride, continued, "Indeed, my elder brother recently broke through to the Dao Integration realm."

"After some maneuvering, he will soon be appointed to the headquarters of the Administrative Hall."

"The official order will be issued shortly, so even if I hadn’t mentioned it, you would have known soon enough."

Hearing Long Yueyou confirm this personally, the cultivators erupted with joy.

This small group of theirs had grown intricately connected over the years, operating as a unit. Their fortunes were intertwined, with Long Yueyou at the helm.

The Long Clan wielded considerable influence, with ties spanning various states within the Immortal Alliance.

Their only shortcoming had been the absence of a true pillar of strength.

But now, with Long Yueyou’s brother ascending to Dao Integration and soon to be stationed at Immortal Alliance headquarters, this lone weakness was about to be resolved.

An Administrative Hall Dao Integration cultivator would occupy one of the highest positions in the Immortal Alliance’s power structure.

The cultivators offered their sincere congratulations to Long Yueyou.

"The Tianling Prefecture is known for its harsh environment. Brother Jiuyuan’s long-awaited rise to prominence is truly worth celebrating!"

Chapter 552: The Return of the Heavenly Calamity

Long Jiuyuan was, naturally, the elder brother of Long Yueyou.

Gifted with exceptional talent, he was the most outstanding individual among the younger generation of the Long Clan.

It took him just over a century to advance from Qi Condensation to the peak of the Soul Transformation stage. Unfortunately, due to the scarcity of the 'Spirit of Heaven and Earth', such a genius remained stuck at the Soul Transformation level.

After more than another century, today he finally saw the dawn of hope.

"Fellow Daoist Long, may I ask where Brother Jiuyuan found the Spirit of Heaven and Earth required for his Dao Integration?"

After a lively celebration, one of the cultivators present couldn't help but ask out of curiosity.

Long Yueyou hesitated slightly, but then remembered that everyone here was close. With his brother now successful in Dao Integration, the existence of that Essence of Heaven and Earth was no longer a secret.

Seeing the eager, expectant eyes of the crowd, Long Yueyou couldn't hold back his pride and began to explain slowly, "It was truly destined for my brother!"

"He guarded Tianling Prefecture for over thirty years. During a certain mission, he stumbled upon a strange sight deep within a mountain range."

"Although the mountain appeared to be ordinary, its originally solid rocks had all turned into a pitch-black, viscous swamp."

"The swamp emitted an ominous aura, and any creature that wandered into it, even magical artifacts or attacks, would be absorbed, assimilated, and disappear without a trace."

"Generally, such a dangerous area would instinctively make any creature with a sense of spiritual awareness feel uneasy and avoid it."

"But my brother discovered something peculiar."

Long Yueyou paused here, as if trying to heighten the suspense. Seeing the eager expressions of the surrounding cultivators, he continued with a satisfied look, "Around the black swamp, all creatures behaved like moths to a flame, rushing in and sacrificing themselves without hesitation."

"Even cultivators were no exception. My brother's Dao heart is incredibly firm, but after staying near the swamp for some time, even he almost couldn't resist the urge to enter."

Some of the cultivators immediately grasped what was happening and gasped, "A fatal allure..."

"Could it be that this black swamp is the Spirit of Heaven and Earth?"

Long Yueyou nodded slightly. "Exactly! My brother guessed as much. Trapped at the Soul Transformation stage for years, he was naturally ecstatic."

"However, he knew that the Spirit of Heaven and Earth is a manifestation of the laws themselves, making it challenging to handle. He forced himself to resist the temptation and retreated."

"After sealing off the entrance with a secret technique, he quietly returned to Tianling City."

"He kept it to himself and began to prepare in secret."

"It took him half a month to prepare before he found another excuse to return to the mountains."

"After setting up a formation to seal off the mountain and prevent outsiders from noticing, he began to attack the black swamp."

"But just as before, every attack vanished without a trace, like a stone sinking into the sea."

Long Yueyou narrowed his eyes, recalling his brother Long Jiuyuan's description: "For three days, my brother tried every possible method, but nothing could damage the black swamp."

"At that moment, he remembered all the scenes of being trapped at the Soul Transformation stage over the years. Finally, he made up his mind."

"With a determination to either succeed or die, he leapt into the black swamp!"

The cultivators present gasped in shock.

They asked themselves if they would have the courage to do the same.

Who knew what lurked within that swamp? Jumping in was like embracing death.

Even if Dao Integration failed, a Soul Transformation cultivator could still command respect and power.

But once life was at risk, there was nothing left to discuss.

But Long Jiuyuan did it anyway!

While the crowd marveled, they couldn't help but feel a deep admiration for him.

"I've heard that Long Jiuyuan is courageous beyond compare. It seems the rumors were not only accurate but even underestimated him!"

"No wonder Brother Jiuyuan, with just his Soul Transformation cultivation, can be on friendly terms with many Dao Integration Immortal Sages!"

"Haha, it's a great honor for us to call him Brother Jiuyuan!"

Listening to everyone praise Long Jiuyuan, Long Yueyou was filled with pride and satisfaction.

After all, he and Long Jiuyuan were brothers, deeply bonded.

Praising Long Jiuyuan was like praising himself!

After a moment, Long Yueyou cleared his throat and continued, "After entering the black swamp, my brother's protective artifact began to be consumed at a frightening rate."

"Mind you, this artifact was specially crafted by a Dao Integration friend skilled in refining."

"If it had been an ordinary protective artifact bought in the Tianxuan Pavilion, it would have long since been eroded."

"With only a limited amount of safe time left, my brother stayed calm. Instead of wandering aimlessly, he closed his eyes, focusing on the changes within the swamp."

"The black swamp was like a deep, dark ocean, with no signs of life besides my brother."

"But my brother could vaguely sense waves of vibration occasionally rippling through the swamp."

"It felt like something was stirring deep within."

"So, my brother followed the source of this vibration, heading deeper into the swamp."

"Just as he was running out of protective artifacts, he finally discovered the source of the disturbance!"

At this crucial moment, Long Yueyou suddenly paused.

His eyes flashed with a strange light before he lowered his voice and said to everyone, "In the center of this swamp, a shadowy, humanoid figure could be faintly seen."

"Not a cultivator, but a distorted figure made from the murky, dark swamp."

"The Spirit of Heaven and Earth!"

"But that wasn't the most shocking part. Besides the swamp, there was something else."

Long Yueyou took a deep breath and revealed the answer.

"It seemed to be a broken sword, exuding a fierce, murderous aura that even my brother, who has killed countless Five Elders Association cultivators, found terrifying."

"The broken sword was struggling violently, and the swamp was containing it like a cage!"

The black swamp, transformed into a sea, held the sword within its depths!

This eerie, surreal scene left everyone in awe, speculating about the origin of this broken sword.

Meanwhile, far away, Li Fan, who was flying towards a certain destination, suddenly halted.

"The Heavenly Calamity Sword?!"

Chapter 553: Han Yi Re-enters the Trap

“Or rather, a part of the Heavenly Calamity Sword.”

A glint of sharp insight flashed in Li Fan’s eyes.

The spirit of heaven and earth had become a cage, entrapping the remnant sword filled with fierce, fiendish energy.

The scene described by Long Yueyou immediately reminded Li Fan of the battle in the skies above Congyun Sea, where the Azure Wind had sealed a sword.

But unlike what Li Fan had witnessed before, Long Jiuyuan’s chance to achieve Dao Integration had arisen in Tianling Prefecture.

The spirit of heaven and earth was also represented by the Black Marsh, rather than the Azure Wind.

But…

The protagonist in both cases was, remarkably, a remnant sword brimming with fiendish energy.

Li Fan was almost certain that whether it was the one in Congyun Sea or in Tianling Prefecture, each must be a part of the original, complete Heavenly Calamity Sword.

“The Heavenly Calamity Sword, once wielded by the old fisherman, had shattered and scattered across various locations.”

“Yet, even these fragmented swords are powerful enough to contend with the spirit of heaven and earth.”

“Moreover, the hilt of the Heavenly Calamity Sword later managed to break through the Azure Wind’s confinement. Even in the Cloud Water Heavenly Palace, the tortoise-snake Taiyi could barely suppress it, forced into a state of deep slumber.”

“If the fragments alone are this formidable, how powerful would the complete Heavenly Calamity Sword be?”

Li Fan’s heart trembled.

From what he knew, there were already three pieces scattered across different locations. Could there be more elsewhere?

What was the true secret of the Heavenly Calamity Sword that made the spirit of heaven and earth repeatedly manifest to suppress it?

And the master of the Heavenly Calamity Sword, the old fisherman—what level of cultivation did he possess?

“The fisherman, the Heavenly Doctor, the Sage…”

Li Fan narrowed his eyes.

These three were evidently old acquaintances, yet their pasts remained shrouded in deep mystery, as though covered by an impenetrable fog.

Li Fan had a subtle feeling.

Their shared secrets might be crucial to him!

“When enemies meet, their eyes blaze with hatred. I hope that soon, when the three of them clash, I can gain a glimpse into their mysteries.”

While locking onto Zhou Kebao’s presence and monitoring Long Yueyou’s movements, Li Fan resumed his journey to his original destination.

According to Long Yueyou’s account, when Long Jiuyuan saw the stalemate between the Heavenly Calamity remnant sword and the Black Marsh, he too was deeply shocked.

Yet he didn’t let the easily attainable chance for Dao Integration cloud his reason.

Long Jiuyuan understood that the balance between the remnant sword and the Black Marsh was delicate and that attacking recklessly would likely break it.

That might lead to backlash from both the remnant sword and the Black Marsh.

After all, for a Soul Transformation cultivator to interfere with a Dao Integration struggle would be nothing short of a provocation.

Thus, Long Jiuyuan quickly made up his mind; despite the destruction of his protective artifact and serious injuries, he fled from the sea of the Black Marsh.

After a brief recovery, he set up the “Heavenly Severing Earth-Isolating Formation” in the surrounding area.

This formation had one purpose: to sever the connection between the spirit of heaven and earth and the world itself.

Though the spirit of heaven and earth manifested the laws of nature, if isolated, it would weaken like water without a source or a tree without roots.

The Black Marsh, initially on par with the Heavenly Calamity Sword, started to weaken, giving the sword an opening!

Long Jiuyuan lay in wait, observing the changes in silence.

As expected, a few months later, the Black Marsh in the mountains began to boil, surging with violent energy.

The phenomenon lasted for almost half a day, until finally, a sword shadow broke free from the Black Marsh’s prison, soaring into the sky and disappearing from sight.

The marsh sea melted away like snow, rapidly contracting into the humanoid form of the **Black Marsh**.

Determining the direction in which the Heavenly Calamity Sword had fled, the Black Marsh was just about to pursue it when…

Long Jiuyuan made his move!

He struck with full force, holding nothing back.

The outcome of the battle was already determined.

The Black Marsh was annihilated.

Long Jiuyuan successfully achieved Dao Integration!

“I recall that in the great battle in Tianling Prefecture in my previous life, a newly integrated Dao powerhouse fell. I wonder if this newly ascended Immortal Sage will escape such a fate.”

This thought flickered through Li Fan’s mind as he approached his destination.

It appeared to be a deserted island.

The island itself wasn’t important; it was the man on it who was Li Fan’s true target.

It was none other than the long-missing Han Yi!

After narrowly escaping death from Li Fan and retreating into the ocean’s depths, he hadn’t surfaced since.

Only recently had Li Fan sensed his return to the cultivation world.

He hadn’t returned through the previous underwater cavern but had emerged from ruins a thousand miles away.

Han Yi looked quite haggard, his clothes torn and bloodstained.

He seemed severely injured and had chosen a nearby island for rest.

Upon detecting this scene, Li Fan decisively pursued him.

Concealing himself, Li Fan watched Han Yi from a distance.

They hadn’t met in a long time, and Han Yi was still at the peak of Qi Condensation, yet to achieve Foundation Establishment.

However, a new, inexplicable aura clung to him.

Li Fan hadn’t noticed it before.

But now, due to Han Yi’s injuries and resulting unconsciousness, the aura emerged naturally, assisting in Han Yi’s recovery.

His pale face regained color, and his weak breathing grew steady and strong.

A strange glint flickered in Li Fan’s eyes.

After a moment’s thought, Li Fan revealed himself and first used the “Binding Insect” spell to immobilize Han Yi.

Then, he activated the “Cloudwater Dream-Illusion Technique” to enter Han Yi’s dreamscape.

Perhaps due to the recent calamity he’d endured, Han Yi’s mental defenses were surprisingly fragile.

It didn’t take long for Li Fan to break through.

In the dreamscape, Li Fan gained insight into Han Yi’s experiences over the recent period.

That day, after being drawn into a sudden underwater vortex, Han Yi lost consciousness.

When he awoke, he found himself bound tightly, stripped of all clothing.

Surrounding him were terrifying creatures with human bodies and beastly faces.

They danced in a circle, seemingly engaged in some ritual.

At the center blazed a fierce fire.

It looked like Han Yi was being offered as a sacrifice to the flames.

Horrified, Han Yi struggled to break free.

But to his despair, there was no trace of spiritual energy in the area.

For some reason, his spiritual power had also dried up.

As helpless as a mortal, Han Yi could only watch as the beast-faced creatures chanted and prepared to throw him into the flames.

Just as Han Yi thought he was doomed, the sky darkened.

Suddenly, torrential rain poured down, quenching the fire in moments.

Han Yi’s heart leaped with joy.

The monsters, however, were terrified.

They threw Han Yi aside and began kowtowing in terror.

The rainstorm continued for half a day.

The creatures remained kneeling in the rain, pleading for mercy.

Only after the rain stopped did they slowly disperse.

Han Yi, intended as a sacrifice, was then imprisoned once more.

Chapter 554: The Black Bird and the Fate of the World

During his captivity, Han Yi had attempted to escape. However, the monsters seemed to place great importance on him, maintaining a seamless, constant watch. They never gave Han Yi the slightest opportunity.

Faced with the relentless, predatory stares of his monstrous guards, Han Yi could only swallow his frustration and abandon his escape plans. Fortunately, the conditions in this prison weren’t too unbearable; the monsters would bring him food and water on time each day, so he wasn’t overly uncomfortable.

After about ten days, the monsters once again tied Han Yi up tightly and brought him to the stake for another sacrificial ritual.

“My fate is sealed! If I’d known it would end like this, I might have preferred to die at the hands of that other cultivator!” Han Yi thought to himself.

Just as he was despairing, something unexpected happened. Thunder rumbled across the sky, and soon a torrential downpour fell, instantly extinguishing the fire that had taken so much effort to ignite.

This turn of events left not only the monsters shocked and terrified, but Han Yi was also stunned. He stared in bewilderment, barely registering when he was brought back to his cell.

The third sacrifice was particularly delayed. Among the monsters, it seemed some internal conflict was brewing. Even from within his cell, Han Yi could hear faint sounds of fierce arguments coming from outside.

After a full thirty days, they finally brought him out again. This time, the day seemed carefully chosen—clear and bright. Each monster wore an expression of grave seriousness, and there was no trace of the noisy scene from the previous sacrifices.

In contrast, Han Yi felt utterly calm. Not only was he unafraid, but he even found himself anticipating something.

The monsters chanted a mysterious hymn in unison and began dancing wildly. Amid the singing and dancing, Han Yi was brought closer and closer to the fire.

Just then, a strange wind swept through, throwing the previously orderly monsters into disarray, scattering even the carefully stacked firewood. Flames burst forth, setting the monsters’ village ablaze, yet Han Yi stood miraculously untouched.

Seeing this, Han Yi couldn’t help but laugh aloud, while the chaotic monsters looked at him with fearful glances, carefully observing his figure among the flames.

From that point on, Han Yi’s status in the monster village changed dramatically. Not only was he no longer imprisoned, but he was given the most comfortable house in the village, and his meals were now the best the village could offer. When the monsters saw him, they no longer glared menacingly but instead bowed their heads in submission, avoiding his gaze.

Despite his improved treatment, Han Yi, a proud Qi Condensation stage cultivator, found it difficult to accept this almost primitive lifestyle. He considered escape again but ultimately decided against it. This world was vastly different from the cultivation world and lacked spiritual energy. Here, he was no different from an ordinary, powerless human. In the monster village, he was safe; attempting a reckless escape could lead him straight into danger and cost him his life.

Thus, Han Yi decided to temporarily settle in the village. He began learning the monsters’ language, hoping to communicate with them. Over time, Han Yi slowly came to understand his situation. This village was called “Zhu,” located in an endless wilderness inhabited by similar human-beast hybrid creatures.

The fewer human traits a creature had, the stronger it was. Villages like Zhu, with human bodies and beast-like faces, were among the weakest. In the depths of the wilderness, it was said that fully beast-like supremes possessed the power to split mountains and rivers.

Han Yi, fully human in appearance, was seen as an “aberration,” a being that offended the divine. He was destined to be burned alive as a sacrifice to appease the gods’ wrath. Yet, after three failed sacrificial attempts, the monsters of Zhu village sensed that Han Yi, this aberration, was extraordinary and reluctantly abandoned their plans to burn him.

In the village's central altar, Han Yi saw a statue of what the monsters called their god. It was a majestic, proud mystical bird, standing tall. Seeing the statue, Han Yi was deeply moved, confirming his suspicions. This deity strongly resembled the fabled black bird of destiny recorded in the "Heavenly Fate Sutra" he practiced.

“Though there is no spiritual energy here, preventing me from cultivating, the ‘Feather of the Black Bird’ I prepared for Foundation Establishment in my dantian feels as though it has returned home,” he thought, feeling an inexplicable energy flowing through the air, allowing the feather to undergo a gradual transformation.

According to the "Heavenly Fate Sutra", the Black Bird Clan’s powers, cultivated to the utmost, could achieve a state where “Heaven’s fate aligns with my fate, and my destiny is Heaven’s mandate.” Heaven and self would become one, indistinguishable. Achieving such a state in the Xuanhuang Realm was virtually impossible. Not even a beast lord could reach that level.

Thus, the Sutra suggested an alternative approach: to find a small world as a personal sanctuary. Merging with the small world’s fate would be much easier, and once achieved, the Black Bird would become akin to the very essence of Heaven’s Way within that small world, capable of transforming landscapes with a mere thought.

“What the Feather of the Black Bird is absorbing must be this world’s fragmented, wandering ‘Heavenly Fate,’” Han Yi pondered. “The heavenly fate of this world should have belonged to the Black Bird that once ruled here. Under normal circumstances, an outsider like me shouldn’t be able to absorb it—even with the Sutra and the Black Bird’s feather.”

“The only possible explanation…” Han Yi’s eyes gleamed with excitement.

“…is that the Black Bird that once fused with this world’s fate has perished!”

“And it couldn’t have been too long ago, for the world’s original fate has yet to recover. The Heavenly Fate remains broken, scattered like unclaimed treasure.”

“The path of fate is incredibly subtle; most cultivators can’t detect it. Even if they do, it would be useless to them,” Han Yi thought, a glint of ambition and greed flashing in his eyes.

“This place is nothing short of a treasure trove for me!”

“By instinctively reaching out for survival, I can manipulate the dispersed Heavenly Fate to summon wind and rain. If I could fully absorb the Heavenly Fate of this world…”

Han Yi’s eyes glowed with a fierce determination and boundless ambition.

“I narrowly escaped death, and now fortune favors me!” He looked out over the vast, unknown world with a surge of boldness.

“When I return to the Xuanhuang Realm with control over the Heavenly Fate of this world, I shall soar like a dragon, unstoppable!”

Imagining the scenes awaiting him, a faint, uncontrollable smile crept onto Han Yi’s lips.

Chapter 555: The Star of Fallen Wilderness

However, things rarely go as one wishes in life.

For a period after this, Han Yi lived quite comfortably in this “Zhu” village.

With no worries about food or clothing, he spent his spare time using the "Mystic Black Bird Feather" within his dantian to absorb the drifting Heavenly Fate in the world around him.

Whenever the Zhu village was attacked by monsters from other settlements, he secretly activated the "Authority of Heavenly Fate," conjuring winds and storms, shifting sand and stone to help them repel waves of foreign enemies.

As a result, Han Yi’s standing in the Zhu village rose significantly.

The monsters of Zhu village went so far as to build a towering stone structure as his palace residence. They also assigned him ten strong and formidable guards for his protection. They even intended to offer him the village’s most sacred “maiden.” Seeing her cat-headed, humanoid form made him shiver with discomfort, and he swiftly declined.

Just when Han Yi thought he would play the role of “Son of God” in Zhu village for a long time, an unexpected event occurred.

One night, he suddenly awoke from a deep sleep, inexplicably filled with a sense of dread.

Rising quietly to inspect the area, he was shocked to find that the ten personal guards, who should have been guarding him day and night, had all disappeared without a trace.

Realizing something was wrong, he barely had time to gather his belongings before attempting to flee the palace.

At that moment, he discovered that while he had slept, nearly a hundred masked soldiers in black had surrounded his palace.

In the flickering firelight of the palace, Han Yi could vaguely make out that these soldiers had a higher degree of beastification than the inhabitants of Zhu village. Although they retained human forms, their limbs were robust and their hands had transformed to resemble those of beasts.

This group was clearly targeting him.

Sensing the danger, Han Yi steeled himself and activated all the Heavenly Fate he had accumulated over recent days.

As the soldiers searched for his whereabouts, a stream of light suddenly brightened the sky, illuminating the night. A massive fireball plummeted directly toward Zhu village.

The black-clad soldiers were momentarily stunned by the sight, and the villagers of Zhu, awakened from their slumber, looked up at what seemed like the arrival of an apocalypse, bewildered and terrified.

Meanwhile, Han Yi, sensing the depletion of the Heavenly Fate he had amassed, hid himself in a corner made of solid stone.

Initially, Han Yi thought that the meteor he had summoned with all his strength would crush all of the black-clad soldiers to death.

Unexpectedly, as the fatal threat approached, one of the soldiers let out a thunderous roar, and his body expanded several times over. He launched himself into the air, charging directly toward the meteor.

“Boom!”

With a violent explosion, the figure fell back to the ground even faster than he had ascended, drenched in blood and mangled beyond recognition.

Yet the meteor Han Yi had summoned veered slightly off course, falling instead toward the village center, where the houses were densest.

“Boom!”

The ground shook.

In the blink of an eye, Zhu village was reduced to ruins, with cries and wails echoing through the air.

The black-clad soldiers, although they avoided a direct hit from the meteor, couldn’t escape the aftermath, suffering heavy casualties.

Having prepared in advance and with a trace of residual Heavenly Fate shielding him, Han Yi was unharmed.

Knowing he couldn’t stay any longer, he used the cover of night and chaos to escape Zhu village alone.

After fleeing more than ten miles, just when he thought he was safe, he sensed a breeze approaching from behind. Panicked, he tried to dodge but was a fraction too slow, failing to avoid it completely.

A blade-like gust sliced a deep wound across his back.

Enduring the searing pain, Han Yi turned to find his assailant—and to his shock, it was a familiar face.

The Zhu village high priest, Si!

Feigning anger, Han Yi demanded to know why Si had attacked him, all the while secretly gathering Heavenly Fate, hoping for a chance to survive.

Si, perhaps believing that Han Yi was already cornered and destined for doom, didn’t rush to strike again. Instead, he revealed the reason behind the attack with bitter resentment.

It turned out that since Han Yi had repeatedly performed “divine miracles,” Si, who had once held a revered position and enjoyed the villagers’ respect, had gradually been “pushed out.”

It was easy to understand.

As the village priest, his primary duty was to represent the villagers in communicating with the gods—praying to the gods on their behalf and delivering divine decrees to the people.

But with Han Yi’s presence, performing miracles almost akin to a god incarnate, why would the villagers seek out a mere “spokesperson” when they had a living divine presence?

Losing his role as the interpreter of divine will, Si’s former honor was all but overtaken by Han Yi.

Watching Han Yi reside in the grand palace, witnessing villagers bow in reverence whenever he appeared, jealousy festered in Si’s heart like a venomous snake.

Even though Han Yi displayed godlike power, Si harbored thoughts of “killing a god.”

But clearly, no one in Zhu village would support such a plan.

So, he set his sights on Kang village, more than ten miles away.

Kang village was much stronger than Zhu village, with inhabitants at a higher level of beastification. Yet despite this, the two villages had never had serious conflicts. The boundless wilderness offered nearly inexhaustible resources.

Even if Kang and Zhu occasionally clashed, they usually resolved it peacefully through negotiation.

Si’s secret visit, however, changed everything.

Si informed the Kang village leader about the “divine descent” in Zhu village. He warned that with divine assistance, the Zhu village chief’s ambitions were swelling. He intended to use the power of the god to sweep through the wilderness and become the “Emperor” uniting it all.

Yet, subtle changes began rippling through Luoyan Province, unnoticed by the world.

...

The narrative shifted back to the Fisherman.

He walked steadily on the ground, not soaring through the skies as other cultivators might. Yet his speed was astonishing. With each step, he seemed to traverse thousands of miles, his figure flashing as he advanced toward the Congyun Sea.

When he reached Yuandao Prefecture, just one step away from his destination, a blue-robed figure appeared in his path.

"Fellow Daoist, I advise you to turn back," the figure said.

The Fisherman frowned, studying the man.

Clad in a blue Daoist robe, the figure had white hair, a deeply wrinkled face, and the frailty of someone nearing their end.

"Have I met you before?" the Fisherman asked uncertainly.

The blue-robed man coughed and replied in a hoarse voice, "You’ve grown forgetful, old friend. Last time we met, you still remembered me. Now, have you truly forgotten who I am?"

He added, "It wasn’t so long ago—just two centuries past."

The Fisherman lowered his head, lost in thought. After a moment, he shook his head.

"I can’t recall. But it doesn’t matter... you won’t live much longer."

The Fisherman's tone was indifferent, as though commenting on the weather.

The blue-robed man froze for a moment.

"Yes," he said softly, "I won’t live much longer..." His voice carried a trace of melancholy.

"Will you still try to stop me?" the Fisherman asked.

"My duty leaves me no choice," the blue-robed man replied without hesitation.

Hearing this, the Fisherman wasted no words. He extended his right hand and grasped at the air.

Suddenly, crisscrossing black threads materialized around the blue-robed man, forming a massive web that ensnared him.

The blue-robed man let out a muffled groan as his body vanished, leaving only a single blue feather glowing within the shrinking net.

When the net retracted into the Fisherman’s hand, he examined the feather, his brow furrowing briefly in confusion.

Shaking his head slightly, he placed the feather into his basket and continued toward the Congyun Sea.

Shortly after his departure, a faint, ghostly figure reappeared at the scene.

"After my death, let the floodwaters rise," the figure muttered with a cold snort.

He cast a deep, lingering gaze at the Fisherman’s retreating form before vanishing.

In Qingyun Prefecture, two prefecture away luoyan, another perspective unfolded.

When his spiritual senses were severed, Li Fan immediately knew something was wrong. Reacting swiftly, he retrieved a sacred artifact gifted to him by the Five Elders Association—a statue of the Manifestation of Heavenly Sovereign Statue.

Unlike the five-faced, ten-armed version used for communication with the Five Elders Pavilion, this statue depicted the Heavenly Sovereign of Sorrowless, its serene visage exuding compassion.

The statue served one purpose: to protect the divine sea consciousness from collapse during a fatal attack.

The artifact activated just in time.

It felt as though a heavy blow had struck Li Fan’s head. His mind buzzed, his vision fragmented into countless overlapping images, and his body swayed.

Coughing up blood, he instinctively clutched his head. Even the body protection charging progress of [Truth] dropped by 5%.

Fortunately, he suffered only minor injuries and avoided disaster.

With the power of his heavenly internal law domain and five-elemental energy circulating within him, Li Fan quickly recovered his vitality.

Chapter 598: Reunion of Heavenly Doctor and Old Fisherman

The Xuanhuang Heart Refining Mantra began to operate automatically. Within a few breaths, even the injuries to his divine sea consciousness had completely vanished.

“Hoo…”

“Not only did he effortlessly dismantle my [Formless Killing Intent], but he also managed to strike my divine sense from such a great distance.”

“If it weren’t for the protective shield of the Manifestation of Heavenly Sovereign Statue, my sea of consciousness would have suffered severe damage.”

Li Fan glanced at the Manifestation of Heavenly Sovereign Statue statue in his hand. After emitting a faint white glow, it dissolved and disappeared without a trace.

“Old Fisherman… a being above Dao Integration is indeed terrifying.”

Although Li Fan was astonished, he was far from fearful. Instead, a glimmer of excitement flashed in his eyes.

He hadn’t anticipated things to proceed so smoothly.

When he noticed Han Yi wandering aimlessly into the heartlands of the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance and pausing in the Luoyan Prefecture, Li Fan already had a vague sense of what was about to unfold.

Previously, he had subtly implanted the ideas of the Azure Wind Trapped Sword and the whereabouts of the Old Fisherman into Han Yi's mind using the Cloud-Water Illusionary Dream technique. He had hoped to use Han Yi as a medium to relay information about the Azure Wind Trapped Sword to the Old Fisherman.

In Li Fan’s expectations, Han Yi would need additional guidance to find the Old Fisherman within the vast regions of several provinces. However, as if guided by destiny or influenced by unknown factors, Han Yi moved with unexpected precision, directly locking onto the Old Fisherman’s location.

The Old Fisherman was a shared opportunity for all. Cultivators within the heartlands of the Ten Thousand Immortals Alliance would instinctively search for his whereabouts. Once discovered, news of his presence spread quickly, with mutual cooperation ensuring swift dissemination.

Li Fan, having experienced countless cycles of reincarnation, was naturally well-versed in this pattern.

Thus, when he saw the Old Fisherman through his perspective of heaven and earth, Li Fan couldn’t help but feel immense joy. His plan to orchestrate a battle between the Old Fisherman, the Heavenly Doctor, and the Sage seemed to be on the brink of success.

When Li Fan initially implanted the thoughts into Han Yi's mind, he also left a wisp of "Heavenly Calamity Energy" in his body. Though almost undetectable, under Li Fan’s deliberate manipulation, it could erupt in a manner that the Old Fisherman wouldn’t overlook.

Through Han Yi’s memories, the Old Fisherman could see the location of the Azure Wind Trapped Sword. Similarly, he could witness the appearance of the Heavenly Doctor in the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace.

Li Fan was confident that both the Sage and the Old Fisherman harbored irreconcilable hatred toward the Heavenly Doctor.

The Old Fisherman, after reclaiming the Heavenly Calamity Sword and restoring some of his power, would undoubtedly seek a confrontation with the Heavenly Doctor. This would undoubtedly complicate matters for the Heavenly Doctor.

The plan was perfect. However, Han Yi proved to be an unexpected variable.

Despite the Old Fisherman appearing before him more than ten times, Han Yi failed to notice anything amiss, leaving Li Fan, who had been tailing them from afar, repeatedly shaking his head.

Of course, Li Fan wouldn’t risk his own safety by intervening. Instead, he kept his distance, patiently observing.

Fortunately, while the process deviated slightly, the outcome ultimately aligned with his expectations.

When Han Yi, lost in his thoughts, charged at the Old Fisherman, even Li Fan couldn’t help but silently applaud his courage.

However, when the Old Fisherman merely cast a glance in Han Yi’s direction, Li Fan immediately sensed trouble.

...

“Thankfully, I was prepared for this and am no longer the novice Foundation Establishment cultivator I once was.”

Exhaling deeply, Li Fan, now recovered, began pondering his next steps.

“If everything proceeds as expected, the Old Fisherman should now be heading toward the Congyun Sea.”

“Gods clash, mortals suffer. My true body should steer clear of the chaos.”

With a thought, Li Fan shifted his attention to his clone, Zhou Qingang.

At the Congyun Sea, the storm was gradually subsiding. Most cultivators had turned their focus to cultivation and studying the techniques they had recently acquired, leaving the administration hall unusually quiet.

Unnoticed by anyone, Li Fan directed Zhou Qingang to approach the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace. The place was almost deserted, with only a few cultivators occasionally passing through in groups.

After a moment of contemplation, Li Fan decided to wait outside instead of entering the palace immediately.

Three days passed.

A sudden gust of wind rustled his robes.

Li Fan raised his head toward the sky and extended a hand, feeling the intensifying wind. A sense of anticipation swelled within him.

“The Old Fisherman is in place. Will the Sage come?”

Based on past experiences, Li Fan was almost certain of the answer.

The faint aura emitted by the Heavenly Doctor’s statue alone was enough to attract the Sage. With the addition of the Old Fisherman, an old acquaintance, the Sage had no reason not to appear.

Moreover, the timing perfectly aligned with the Sage’s expected arrival.

Li Fan was confident that his plan was coming to fruition.

“Next, I need to find a good spot to watch the show.”

Retrieving a gourd of fine wine from his storage ring, Li Fan gave it a gentle pat and entered the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace. After completing three trials with lightning speed, he stayed behind, sharing drinks and idle chatter with Senior Brother Qin.

While outwardly relaxed, Li Fan was silently awaiting the inevitable confrontation.

However, as time dragged on without the anticipated scene, Li Fan grew increasingly restless.

“Ha! Junior Brother, you’re quite insincere. Pretending to drink with me while your thoughts wander!”

“Tell me, which junior sister has caught your eye? Don’t worry, share it with me. If suitable, I’ll act as your matchmaker!”

“Of course, a senior sister isn’t out of the question either!”

Senior Brother Qin, half-drunk, burst into hearty laughter.

Li Fan was about to respond when the entire world suddenly shook violently. Senior Brother Qin, mid-drink, froze, trembling uncontrollably.

“Heavenly Doctor…”

All traces of drunkenness vanished as he ground his teeth in anger.

Boom!

Layers of illusions shattered, and when Li Fan reappeared at the gates of the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace, the first thing he saw was the confrontation between the Old Fisherman and the Heavenly Doctor.

Unlike in his previous life, the once thunderous wails and lamentations within the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace had subsided significantly, as if something was suppressing their outbursts of hatred and sorrow.

The Old Fisherman glared at the Heavenly Doctor, his body enveloped in surging black calamity energy. Yet, the Heavenly Calamity Sword fragment was nowhere in sight.

Though silent, the murderous intent in his eyes was unmistakable. For some reason, however, he refrained from attacking.

The Heavenly Doctor, on the other hand, appeared rather indifferent, even slightly amused.

“How interesting.”

“Tian Jue, have you regained your memory?” the Heavenly Doctor asked.

Chapter 599: The Gathering of Three Venerable

“Tian…”

“Jue…”

The old fisherman murmured these two words softly, his expression initially filled with confusion.

As he repeated them over and over, it seemed as though he recalled something, a faint light flickering in his clouded eyes.

“I am…”

“Tian Jue?”

As the fisherman’s memories gradually returned, the black fiendish aura already surging around him grew even thicker.

It became like a rolling black tide, spreading and raging outward.

However, all the fiendish aura instinctively avoided the Heavenly Doctor before it could even approach him.

Between the two opposing figures, an expansive empty space was formed.

The Heavenly Doctor remained unmoved by the ominous aura. Observing Tian Jue’s still-dazed state, he showed a trace of surprise.

“Your memories haven’t returned? Then how…”

The Heavenly Doctor's eyes gleamed as he prepared to take action.

But suddenly, a distant voice reciting ancient scripture echoed through the air:

“Heaven’s law is solemn, observe the rules and follow the order.”

“I have obtained the dharma, spreading it to all beings.”

“Heaven’s law cannot be defied.”

The Heavenly Doctor’s expression finally shifted noticeably.

Narrowing his eyes, he turned to look in the direction of the voice.

“Moo!”

Without warning, a massive green ox the size of a small mountain appeared before him.

Its horns tilted upwards, and its four hooves moved swiftly, charging forward as if to crush the Heavenly Doctor in one blow.

“Beast!” The Heavenly Doctor snorted coldly.

He did not dodge but simply raised his right hand, where a glimmer of silver light appeared.

The silver light transformed into a fine thread, piercing the azure ox like lightning.

In an instant, the thread wove back and forth countless times, slicing through the ox entirely.

The green ox was reduced to countless unrecognizable fragments, yet it continued its forward momentum, scattering into the air.

“Condense!”

A furious shout rang out suddenly.

Moments later, the green ox that had been dismembered by the Heavenly Doctor miraculously reassembled, its original form emerging from the scattered remains.

Leaping above the Heavenly Doctor, it prepared to trample him underfoot.

Yet the Heavenly Doctor still did not evade.

Before the ox could touch him, its body suddenly shrank.

From a fully grown, robust beast, it became a newborn calf in the blink of an eye.

Finally, it dissolved into a wisp of energy, vanishing into the heavens and earth.

“Tianyu…” The Heavenly Doctor looked toward the direction from which the ox had come, a trace of amusement appearing on his face.

An ethereal figure with white hair and a hunched posture emerged at that moment.

“Sage!”

“Sage!”

With the arrival of the Tianyu, the previously cowering disciples of the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace seemed to find their backbone.

No longer fearful, they began to cheer fervently.

“Ah…”

Tianyu sighed softly, as though comforting them, and waved his wide sleeve.

The cries within the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace momentarily quieted. Only then did his gaze fall upon the Heavenly Doctor and Tian Jue.

Clap, clap, clap.

The Heavenly Doctor began to clap lightly.

“Well…”

“What a coincidence.”

Without looking at the two figures surrounding him, the Heavenly Doctor raised his head, as though searching for something, a thoughtful expression on his face.

“Heavenly Doctor!”

Finally, the Tianyu could no longer suppress his anger.

“You’ve been hiding all these years, and now you finally dare to show yourself?”

His voice was like thunder, filled with boundless rage, reverberating throughout the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace.

“Hmph.”

The Heavenly Doctor merely snorted coldly, offering no reply.

This dismissive attitude ignited the Tianyu fury.

“If that’s the case, then there’s nothing more to say. Prepare to die!”

“Tian Jue, why aren’t you acting yet?”

The Old Fisherman was still in a state of confusion at this moment.

However, as the Sage roared in anger, the two of them worked together seamlessly, as if they were old comrades-in-arms with years of experience.

In perfect coordination, the next moment, the Heavenly Calamity Sword was drawn with a resonant clang.

Even though it was merely a broken sword, an overwhelming aura of murderous intent surged continuously from it, coalescing and refusing to dissipate, taking the form of a sword.

From deep within the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace, a massive tremor reverberated.

The Turtle-Serpent Taiyi let out a painful wail as the hilt of the Heavenly Calamity Sword, feeling the call of its master, began to struggle free from the Turtle-Serpent's body.

At that moment, an azure brilliance enveloped the entirety of the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace.

A giant blue hand emerged, pressing the restless hilt of the Heavenly Calamity Sword back into place.

The Taiyi once again let out an earth-shaking howl of agony.

Then, as if suppressed, its cries grew quieter and quieter until it fell into slumber once more.

"Grandmaster Hanhai?"

From various corners of the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace, astonished exclamations could faintly be heard.

"At least someone here is still clear-headed," the Heavenly Doctor commented coldly.

In the next moment, the azure ocean in the sky rapidly contracted, condensing into a flickering phantom that seemed as though it could shatter at any moment.

The figure appeared before the three—Heavenly Doctor, Sage, and the Old Fisherman.

"Greetings, esteemed ones," the figure said with neither arrogance nor servility.

"Hanhai, what is the meaning of this?"

Suppressing his anger, Tianyu asked in a low voice upon noticing Hanhai's strange attitude.

"The culprit is right in front of us. With the blood feud of the past, why not exact our revenge now?"

"Master Sage is right—blood debts must indeed be repaid in blood. But…"

Hanhai did not look at the Heavenly Doctor but instead turned to the Old Fisherman, who still seemed somewhat bewildered while holding the sword.

"The one who slaughtered the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace back then was not the Heavenly Doctor."

"It was Tian Jue!"

His words carried a raging resentment, and Hanhai's azure phantom trembled violently.

His furious gaze locked onto the other party as if tumultuous waves churned within him.

"Hmm?" Tianyu was slightly stunned, then shook his head and sighed.

"Hanhai, in the end, you cannot escape the effects of time. Has even your memory of that day become muddled?"

"The butcher who exploited the vulnerability of our rear defenses and massacred every major sect—is it not this traitor before us?"

Tianyu's gaze bore into the Heavenly Doctor, and the flames of hatred reignited in his eyes.

"One person may err. But could the countless souls who perished on that day all be mistaken?"

Hanhai shook his head resolutely and said, "I do not know about other sects; they are unrelated to me.

"But the one who destroyed my Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace was undoubtedly Venerable Tian Jue!"

He spoke with absolute certainty.

"Though his appearance matched that of the Heavenly Doctor, and even his aura was identical…"

"His ruthless, annihilative actions and his cold, emotionless demeanor could never have been mimicked by the Heavenly Doctor."

"Most importantly, the weapon used in the massacre was none other than the hilt of the Heavenly Calamity Sword, now sealed within Taiyi."

"The Heavenly Calamity, a weapon of unparalleled power, can only be wielded by Venerable Tian Jue."

These words made even the Sage hesitate. He turned his head to glance at Tian Jue.

It seemed Hanhai's words had stirred something buried deep within Tian Jue's mind.

He stared blankly at his own hands.

After a long while, he suddenly spoke.

"Yes, it was me who did it…"

"What!" The Sage's hunched figure straightened instantly as he stared incredulously at the Old Fisherman, who had just confessed.

Chapter 600: The Doctor of Ten Thousand Years

It felt like falling into a nightmare, unable to escape.

Tian Jue's entire body trembled uncontrollably.

"That's right, I killed them all!"

"Qin Tang, Xiao Chong, Fu Yun..."

"I killed them all!"

Even the wise Sage seemed unable to process this answer for a moment.

"Why...?" he asked, dazed.

At that moment, Tian Jue's expression suddenly shifted.

"No..."

"That's not right!"

"It wasn't me at all!"

Tian Jue lifted his head abruptly, glaring at Heavenly Doctor with hatred.

"It’s all your doing!"

"All those memories in my mind—they're fake!"

"Ever since that day I met you, everything has been strange!"

"Everything spiraled out of control..."

Pointing the Heavenly Calamity Sword directly at Heavenly Doctor, Tian Jue demanded:

"Was it you?"

Facing Tian Jue's accusations, Heavenly Doctor's expression remained unchanged.

"I thought you'd have improved this time."

"Turns out, you're still the same as ever."

He shook his head slightly, a trace of regret in his voice.

"What do you mean?" Tianyu noticed a peculiar undertone in Tian Yi's words and asked sharply.

Heavenly Doctor didn’t answer but continued speaking to himself.

"I’ve explained this to you countless times. Sometimes, even what you see with your own eyes might not be real."

As he spoke, he somehow produced a set of nine silver needles, arranging them mid-air.

"Everyone seems so eager to kill me."

"Well, I have no choice but to resolve things my way."

Seeing the nine silver needles in Heavenly Doctor’s hands, Tianyu's expression grew darker.

Without waiting for further explanation, Tianyu acted decisively.

"The law cannot be defied!"

"Bind!"

It was as if an invisible force wrapped around Heavenly Doctor’s body, causing his movements with the silver needles to pause slightly.

"Weaken! Injure! Seal! Cripple! Damage!"

One by one, these words left Tianyu’s mouth, each further slowing Heavenly Doctor's actions.

Meanwhile, the Old Fisherman, his eyes glowing red, gripped the Heavenly Calamity Sword tightly. His body turned into a cloud of black murderous intent, merging with the sword.

Like a sharp arrow, it pierced straight toward Heavenly Doctor.

Bound by Tianyu, Heavenly Doctor seemed utterly powerless, unable to resist.

In an instant, the black sword energy pierced through Heavenly Doctor’s chest.

The black aura surged like a raging tide, engulfing Heavenly Doctor entirely.

In no time, the previously calm Heavenly Doctor vanished from the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace.

"Is Heavenly Doctor dead?"

Watching from the shadows, Zhou Qingang was stunned.

The battle between Heavenly Doctor, Tianyu, and Tian Jue had produced no residual shockwaves.

For now, his clone observing in secret remained safe.

But the scene unfolding before him was far beyond his expectations.

He had thought Heavenly Doctor, with his formidable power, would at least put up a fight. Instead, he was struck down instantly by Tianyu and Tian Jue’s joint assault...

"With his power, how could he fall so easily?"

"Wait..."

A sudden realization hit Li Fan as he looked to the sky.

The Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace trembled violently.

Two dark clouds appeared in the sky above.

When he saw what they were, Li Fan’s breath caught.

They were giant hands, piercing through the heavens, descending upon the Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace.

Sage and the Fisherman were both shocked, staring up at the hands with dread.

They tried to resist, but their bodies were immobilized.

Terror spread across their faces.

"Don’t worry; this procedure will be over quickly."

A booming voice from above—Heavenly Doctor’s voice—reverberated across the heavens.

Li Fan’s heart raced, his mind nearly frozen.

It was clear now—the figure that Tianyu and the Fisherman had confronted was merely a statue of Heavenly Doctor.

And these giant hands were Heavenly Doctor’s true form!

In the face of these colossal hands, even the powerful Tian Jue and Sage seemed like insects on an operating table, utterly helpless.

The hands seized them, suspending them in mid-air.

The nine silver needles from the statue now floated toward the hands, initiating "treatment."

Watching from afar, Li Fan felt an uncontrollable chill.

"Heavenly Doctor... What on earth is he?"

The overwhelming strength Heavenly Doctor displayed far surpassed Li Fan’s estimations.

Even if he wasn’t at the Longevity Immortal Realm, he was not far from it.

Li Fan immediately considered abandoning his clone to avoid Heavenly Doctor tracing it back to his true body.

But then he realized...

No, Heavenly Doctor has obviously discovered that someone is plotting this behind his back, not insignificant ants observing from the sidelines.

However, if the clone suddenly disappeared now, it would be too obvious and might draw attention instead.

Caught in a mental battle, Li Fan chose to gamble.

He watched the "surgery" with bated breath, hoping Heavenly Doctor still adhered to human logic and wouldn’t overthink.

From Li Fan's current perspective, he still couldn't discern the reason.

He only felt silver light scattering, and the angry curses from Tianyu and Tian Jue gradually grew weaker.

Finally, as if sinking into a deep sleep, becoming almost inaudible.

As the surgery concluded, the Fisherman reverted to his confused, memory-lost state, wandering off.

The Sage, meanwhile, resumed his scholarly demeanor, disappearing with his ox cart.

After completing everything, the two giant hands retracted the silver needles.

Gently pressing down.

The Cloud-Water Heavenly Palace, which had been filled with wailing sounds just moments before, instantly fell silent.
