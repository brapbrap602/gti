Chapter 1151: The True Culprit Behind The World’S Destruction

“Could it be…”

“Longevity Immortal?”

A thought flashed through Shang Shaojun’s mind uncontrollably.

From what he knew, among the survivors who came from other cultivation worlds, there were indeed beings who had reached the Longevity Immortal Heavenly Sovereign realm.

Although they were far from being as invincible as the Longevity Immortal Heavenly Sovereigns of the Xuanhuang World, they were still entities no ordinary Dao Integration Immortal Sage could hope to contend with.

“It should be as I thought.” Shang Shaojun’s heart tightened, and his attitude toward Li Fan became even more cautious.

The two continued deeper into the Chizha Abyss, where the fusion of laws from different worlds had reached its peak.

In the darkness, new anomalies began to emerge.

A faint blood-red glow illuminated the path ahead.

Fragments of shattered continents hung upside down and drifted above their heads.

From afar, one could faintly see ruins scattered across those lands, and within the ruins, countless dense white bones.

Every fragment of the floating worlds was utterly silent and lifeless.

Thick crimson threads, like the roots of an enormous tree, grew out from those broken continents, stretching into the depths of the abyss and vanishing into the darkness.

At times, chilling sounds echoed through the air.

It was like a blade scraping across bone, or the heavy, rasping breath of someone on the verge of death.

The eerie atmosphere of this place made both Li Fan and Shang Shaojun instinctively stop in their tracks.

Seeing Li Fan quietly observe the strange sight ahead, Shang Shaojun pondered for a moment before sending a voice transmission:

“These broken worlds… seem to still be alive?” he said uncertainly.

Holy Embryo Li Fan did not reply.

He simply waved his hand, sending out a streak of sword light that shot straight toward one of the shattered worlds.

Shang Shaojun’s heart tightened immediately.

Although he had witnessed Li Fan’s extraordinary swordsmanship before, he knew that even a dying world’s counterattack was something not to be underestimated.

The silvery sword light crossed space in an instant and arrived upon the ruined land.

What surprised Shang Shaojun was that Li Fan’s strike did not seem intended to destroy.

After piercing through the barrier and descending, the sword light remained suspended, motionless.

Even the path it left behind in the air had not dissipated.

The sword light solidified, forming a thin silver thread.

It connected Li Fan and that broken world together.

Li Fan floated gracefully, standing atop the silver line. His eyes closed, as though he was sensing something profound.

“This is…”

At first, Shang Shaojun could not understand what Li Fan was doing.

But as he watched, an image suddenly arose in his mind—of a mortal physician diagnosing a patient’s pulse.

“With a sword as the medium, he seeks the balance between life and death.”

“Could it be that this one’s mastery is not limited to the sword, but extends deeply into the Dao of Healing as well?”

Shang Shaojun was inwardly shaken.

Then Li Fan’s next action made his heart leap violently.

Li Fan gently tapped his foot on the silver thread.

A faint ripple spread along the line, transmitting into the broken world.

At first, there was only a subtle tremor, barely noticeable.

But then, as if responding to some hidden resonance, the vibration grew stronger and stronger.

The once-dead land suddenly awoke.

A deep rumble echoed from beneath the ground, and torrents of molten lava erupted upward amid the world’s furious roar.

The entire realm began to stir once more…

Before Li Fan’s voice had even fallen, Shang Shaojun felt himself struck by an immense force. His body spun uncontrollably backward, and his blood and qi surged violently within. He had already suffered light internal injuries.

Startled, Shang Shaojun looked back and saw that the fragmented world Li Fan had been “diagnosing” was now collapsing in on itself, violently self-destructing toward its final annihilation.

It was only a broken remnant to begin with, fragile beyond repair.

It could not withstand such fierce internal energy fluctuations.

The land was completely torn apart, dissolving into endless crimson flames.

As the flames burned out their final light within the darkness, they marked the demise of an entire world.

Shang Shaojun watched the scene in silence, dazed by what he saw.

Li Fan stood beside him and calmly explained, “If one uses external force to destroy this world, it will not only cause the world’s backlash, but it may also alert the being behind all of this.”

“But if I forcibly ignite the last remnants of the world’s power and let it perish naturally...”

As Li Fan spoke, countless sword shadows emerged behind him.

Sword lights intertwined, flaring brightly before vanishing into the void.

“Enter the formation.”

Hearing Li Fan’s transmitted voice, Shang Shaojun’s heart trembled again.

Yet he had been so shocked already that numbness was beginning to set in. He followed almost instinctively.

Once he stepped into the formation space, he saw sword shadows rapidly circling all around.

A thousand swords flew in unison, opening up a hidden sanctuary within the chaos.

Before entering the sword formation, Shang Shaojun had also seen Li Fan repeating the process, one after another destroying several broken worlds ahead.

The destructive shockwaves released with each world’s collapse turned the depths of this Chizha Abyss into a scene like a purgatory.

Countless dark-red ashes, carried by wild torrents of energy, erupted outward from the abyss.

It was like ten thousand ghosts wandering through the night, like a frenzied stampede of beasts.

The Thousand-Sword Formation seemed to strain under the pressure.

It kept shrinking tighter and tighter.

The rotating sword lights spun faster and faster, almost forming complete concentric circles.

Yet Li Fan showed no sign of worry about the formation’s endurance.

He stood with eyes closed, motionless, as if performing some unknown task.

“Wait.”

Sensing Shang Shaojun’s confusion, Li Fan sent a brief voice transmission and then fell silent.

Shang Shaojun took a deep breath, his mind gradually calming under the cool aura surrounding him.

The sword lights danced around, completely sealing off all outside views.

Even his divine sense was blocked; he could not perceive what was happening beyond.

But from the heavy pounding sounds occasionally striking the sword formation, he could tell that the destruction of several world fragments had unleashed terrifying external impacts.

“If not for him, I would have been forced to retreat in disgrace by now.”

“Sword arts, healing arts, formation arts... How could there exist someone so proficient in all disciplines?”

Shang Shaojun’s mind churned with thoughts. Even with the support of the Immortal Heart Mantra, he could not suppress his growing speculation about Li Fan’s true identity.

He did not know how much time had passed.

At last, the storm outside the sword formation began to subside.

But Li Fan still made no move to withdraw the formation.

Judging from his increasingly grave expression, it seemed that something outside remained, something even more terrifying than the destruction of the worlds themselves.

“The mastermind behind it all?”

The image of countless shattered world fragments hanging upside down in the void flashed through Shang Shaojun’s mind.

Such a sight could not have formed naturally.

To think that a being capable of treating entire worlds as mere playthings might now be lurking nearby, his scalp tingled at the thought.

“Can this sword formation truly conceal us?”

“If it finds us, there will be no escape—only death.”

Chapter 1152: Reforging The World With A Single Hand

Although Shang Shaojun was the commander of the Qingxuan Army, he had never personally faced an existence at the Longevity Immortal Heavenly Sovereign Realm.

As he waited in anxious silence, unease gnawed at him. Every moment felt like an eternity.

After what seemed like ages, the endless swirl of sword light around them finally stopped.

Li Fan, who had been sitting with his eyes closed, slowly opened them.

As the sword light dispersed, Shang Shaojun instinctively looked around.

Those few shattered worlds that Li Fan had just annihilated with sword light somehow reappeared once more!

They now hung upside down above their heads, bound together by eerie crimson threads that intertwined and thickened continuously.

Countless strands extended endlessly toward the Chizha Abyss.

“Not only did they not disappear, they’ve actually…” Shang Shaojun felt a chill seize his heart. His gaze turned toward Li Fan in horror.

He even began to doubt whether what he had seen earlier, the destruction of those worlds—had been nothing but an illusion.

“He’s been here.”

Li Fan spoke in a low voice.

“Who is he?” Shang Shaojun could not help but ask.

The Holy Embryo Clone of Li Fan shook his head.

“You’ll know soon enough.”

Li Fan made a hand seal and pointed forward into the void.

The thousand beams of sword light that had formed the great sword formation appeared once again before the two of them.

Then they began to revolve at tremendous speed.

As the sword light spun faster and faster, faint and indistinct images started to appear upon the luminous curtain formed by the swords.

“To be able to do all this,” Li Fan said quietly, “he must be an existence of incomparably great power. If we had secretly spied upon him just now, even with my supreme formation concealing us, even with the destruction of worlds as natural cover…”

“We would still have been discovered.”

Only now did Li Fan explain why he had used a formation to block Shang Shaojun’s perception during their surveillance.

Shang Shaojun’s heart trembled as Li Fan continued, “Even the sword lights I used to record this scene were not focused in one place. Each sword light recorded only a fragment of information. Only when they are combined together can we see the full picture of what happened.”

“This way, the chance of being discovered is reduced to the lowest possible level.”

As Li Fan murmured, the images upon the sword curtain shifted.

The fragments of shattered worlds reappeared, followed by the eruption of world-destroying power that swept outward in a devastating torrent.

Just as this violent scene began to fade…

A figure suddenly appeared in the center of the storm.

The sword light recording did not capture the man’s true face.

Or perhaps he had not revealed his true form at all.

Shang Shaojun only knew that at the moment this mysterious being appeared, all the chaotic energy and destructive fluctuations instantly calmed.

The figure observed the surroundings for a brief moment, as though puzzled.

Then, his gaze turned toward the direction of the Chizha Abyss.

Even though it was only a recorded image, even though his features were blurred, even though they were separated by layers of time and space—

When that gaze swept across them, Shang Shaojun felt his body and very soul freeze completely.

He could not move. He could not even think.

Fortunately, the figure only examined the area briefly and did not probe further.

He retracted his gaze.

Shang Shaojun collapsed as if after a great battle, gasping heavily, drenched in cold sweat, yet still staring fixedly at the sword curtain that continued to shift.

The mysterious figure pressed both hands downward into the air before him. Instantly, all the violent storms of that space froze still.

With a wave of his hand, he gathered up the remaining ashes of the destroyed worlds.

Then…

The figure seemed solemn, cautiously taking out an equal number of world fragments from an unknown place.

It was like a giant from an ancient myth, one who could open the heavens and split the earth.

He suspended those shattered worlds upside down at the bottom of the Chizha Abyss.

Then, with a gentle motion of his finger, streaks of strange crimson threads flew out from within those broken world fragments.

They seemed to possess a will of their own, flying directly toward the Xuanhuang Realm.

From within the Chizha Abyss, a deep roar resounded again.

That was the sign of two worlds fusing together.

After finishing all this, and once the inverted worlds had stabilized, the figure finally departed.

...

The vision ended abruptly.

When Li Fan withdrew the thousand sword lights, Shang Shaojun was still immersed in the unbelievable scenes he had just witnessed.

“The Xuanhuang Realm actually hides such a being?”

“The legendary immortals who pluck stars and grasp the moon would be no greater than this.”

Recalling the moment when that figure took out world fragments and gently lifted them, Shang Shaojun could not suppress a trembling fear that spread through his body.

Yet Li Fan’s reply instantly pulled him out of his endless awe and dread.

“This person, though powerful, is still no match for a True Immortal.”

His tone was calm, as if he had truly seen an immortal before.

“It seems my guess was correct. The bizarre situation within the Xuanhuang Realm—there must be someone behind it, carrying out an unknown conspiracy.”

Before Shang Shaojun could reply, Li Fan continued.

“The figure we just saw is one of them.”

“What does Young Lord Shang plan to do?”

Shang Shaojun was momentarily stunned.

What could he do?

That unknown and terrifying existence’s strength must be at the level of a Longevity Immortal Heavenly Sovereign, or even higher.

As a mere Dao Integration cultivator, what could he possibly do?

“I must return immediately to the headquarters of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance and report this matter.”

Shang Shaojun spoke with righteousness and resolve.

Li Fan only gave him a faint, ambiguous smile. “Are you sure?”

“If you truly insist on doing so…”

“Then I fear today will be the last time we ever meet.” Li Fan’s voice was low and quiet.

Shang Shaojun’s face changed. “What do you mean?”

“The Chizha Abyss obviously conceals a great secret. Many years ago, wise members of the Immortal Alliance once tried to investigate on their own, but their proposal was rejected by the Alliance itself...”

“You still do not understand, Young Lord? Within the domain of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, how could such massive activities remain hidden from their notice?”

Li Fan spoke indifferently.

Of course Shang Shaojun understood this truth—he simply never dared to admit it to himself.

Now that Li Fan had mercilessly exposed it, his expression shifted repeatedly.

He knew clearly that if all this truly involved, or was even led by, the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, then the meaning behind it was unimaginably terrifying.

Even after witnessing countless conspiracies and power struggles, Shang Shaojun now felt a deep, suffocating helplessness.

“You need not despair, Young Lord.”

“This matter has clearly surpassed what either of us can handle. To pollute the Xuanhuang Realm by using fragments of destroyed worlds and tainted Heavenly Dao, such an act makes them the enemies of all beings in this realm.”

“Although some of the high-level members of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance may be involved, it is impossible for them to cover the sky with one hand.”

“The Immortal Ancestor wanders the heavens and no longer concerns himself with worldly matters. However, I have heard that the transmitters of his teachings possess a special method to call him back...”

The Holy Embryo Li Fan spoke in a gentle, guiding tone.

Hearing this, Shang Shaojun’s eyes suddenly lit up. “Indeed. With the situation now so dire, perhaps only by calling back the Immortal Ancestor can it be resolved. But… to contact the Heavenly Sovereign himself, only…”

Shang Shaojun furrowed his brow slightly.

Meanwhile, Li Fan silently recalled the vision he had just seen.

“Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor…”

Chapter 1153: Immortal Heart Falls To The Mortal Realm

It was not that Li Fan deliberately concealed that person’s figure.

Rather, the mysterious figure appearing at the bottom of the Chizha Abyss was too powerful. The environment at the abyss’s base, where the laws were already unstable, simply could not accommodate the existence of such a realm.

As a result, from the outside, everything appeared blurred.

However, based on the aura inadvertently revealed by that figure, Li Fan was more than half certain that the person was the Immortal Ancestor Heavenly Sovereign.

"But compared to the impression of a weathered old man, he seems younger. What’s going on here?"

The Holy Embryo Li Fan fell into deep thought.

At this moment, Shang Shaojun also snapped out of his contemplation and transmitted his voice: “I know a way to summon the Heavenly Sovereign back, but relying solely on this vague image…”

“It’s not enough. We need more evidence.”

“Even with just the two of us, we cannot directly present the collected evidence to him. Convincing him would be incredibly difficult.”

Hearing Shang Shaojun’s apprehension, Li Fan smiled and responded: “Shaojun, there’s no need to be overly pessimistic. You and I may be weak on our own, but in the vast Xuanhuang Realm, we can surely find like-minded allies.”

“We can gather evidence while seeking trustworthy individuals to strengthen our cause.” In his words, Li Fan already implied teaming up with Shang Shaojun.

“But the Chizha Abyss…” Shang Shaojun still hesitated.

Li Fan immediately spoke with a serious tone: “As the saying goes, if the skin is gone, where will the hair attach? If the entire Xuanhuang Realm falls into crisis, what use is it for you, Shaojun, to suppress the abyss and ensure its stability?”

“Besides, I’ve already thought of a perfect solution to suppress the abyss’s demonic sounds even if you travel far.”

As they spoke, the two had returned to the entrance of the abyss.

Li Fan’s eyes narrowed, and a giant sword, seemingly forged from tens of thousands of distinct sword lights, instantly materialized across the abyss.

Pointing into the void, the giant sword danced nimbly with Li Fan’s movements.

Correspondingly, strands of continuous dark red threads were sliced apart.

Freed from the constraints of the original world’s rules, these threads should have scattered chaotically and then dissipated.

Yet, under the motion of Li Fan’s hand waving in the air, they slowly gathered together.

Silently, they wove into a massive red net!

Witnessing this, Shang Shaojun’s heart trembled slightly once more.

Though not as awe-inspiring as the mysterious figure effortlessly manipulating a broken world, Li Fan’s casual act of weaving the broken laws of the world into a net was enough to demonstrate his extraordinary mastery of the Dao.

"The reason why the Demonic Sound Tide is so difficult to guard against is due to the conflict between the laws of the Broken World and the Xuanhuang Realm, all concentrated in this narrow region, too intense without any buffer. So, I slightly altered the spatial structure here, delaying the fusion of laws to the greatest extent possible."

"In fact, that mysterious figure could have easily done this, concealing their tracks so no one would notice anything unusual. Yet, they did not. Perhaps this is a form of confidence," Li Fan said, shaking his head slightly.

"With the buffer of this web of laws, the power of the Demonic Sound Tide's outbreak is expected to be reduced by half."

As Li Fan spoke, he gently pinched his fingers, transforming the giant sword in the abyss back into countless rays of sword light.

Countless sword shadows, following a certain pattern, soared like dragons, faintly forming a formation.

Shang Shaojun saw the sword formation spiraling around the outer edge of the web of laws, its center seemingly forming a massive vortex.

The dark red embers overflowing from the web of laws were occasionally drawn into the sword formation.

"This formation, with the sword intent to annihilate everything, shatters the broken laws accumulated in the Chizha Abyss. It can extend the cycle of the Demonic Sound Tide's outbreak by more than double."

"Shaojun, you may command the sword cultivators of the Qingxuan Army to come here. Not only can they comprehend the sword intent condensed in the formation, but they can also help charge the formation."

Speaking calmly, Li Fan had already laid out a solution to the Demonic Sound Tide, which the Qingxuan Army feared.

Shang Shaojun looked at the formation and the web, speechless.

Since childhood, those around him had called him a genius.

But in front of Li Fan...

"Truly, one mountain is always higher than another. I was too shallow before."

With his worries resolved, Shang Shaojun had no reason to refuse Li Fan's company.

The two returned to the Eternal Immortal Fortress and announced that the Demonic Sound Tide had been temporarily suppressed, eliciting thunderous cheers from the Qingxuan Army.

Then, Shang Shaojun gathered his trusted aides and passed down the method to control the formation, which Li Fan had named the "Sound-Extinguishing Sword Formation."

With all matters arranged, before departing, Shang Shaojun privately summoned Yan Huan.

"Rest assured, Shaojun. The technique deduced by the Derivative Law Jade is very effective. The brothers who occasionally lost their sanity have rarely had relapses recently," Yan Huan replied respectfully, bowing his head.

...

From their conversation, Li Fan also learned why Yan Huan had urgently used a precious Derivative Jade Token to seek the Derivation Law Jade's deduction of a technique.

The Qingxuan Army, stationed outside the Chizha Abyss for years, was subtly influenced by the Demonic Sound. Some soldiers became mentally unstable, even turning their blades against their comrades in frenzied acts.

In the past, the number of affected individuals was small, and dealing with them was relatively easy.

But over time, nearly one-fifth of the Qingxuan Army in the Broken Palm Immortal City had contracted this strange affliction.

Not long before the Holy Embryo Li Fan arrived, a large-scale outbreak had occurred among the Qingxuan Army.

The casualties were heavy.

The most unbearable part was that they did not die from the Demonic Sound Tide but at the hands of their own brothers.

The Qingxuan Army's morale was low, and their unity was shaken.

With no other choice, Shang Shaojun used his hard-earned Derivative Jade Token to request the Derivation Law Jade to address the issue.

"The 'Heart-Forging Demon-Repelling Technique' allows practitioners to help others strengthen their Daoist heart and expel malevolent thoughts from their minds. Though it can only address one person's affliction at a time, making it less efficient, it is indeed effective. The recovered soldiers even seem to have developed some resistance to the Demonic Sound," Shang Shaojun explained to Li Fan.

"How does this Heart-Forging Demon-Repelling Technique compare to the secret technique I taught you, Shaojun?" Li Fan asked.

Shang Shaojun was momentarily stunned, then blurted out, "It is far inferior!"

"Then, Shaojun, why not pass down the 'Immortal Heart Mantra' to prevent everyone from suffering the Demonic Sound's torment?" Li Fan said with a compassionate expression, transmitting his voice.

Shang Shaojun's eyes widened, his heart trembling.

"The Immortal Heart Mantra..."

He finally learned the name of that supreme secret technique, and the words "Immortal Heart" sparked endless thoughts in his mind.

While shocked by Li Fan's generosity, Shang Shaojun felt reluctant to share such a secret technique with others so freely.

Chapter 1154: The Transformation Of Rebuilding The Foundation

"Isn't this a bit wasteful?" Shang Shaojun quietly transmitted his question to Li Fan in the presence of his trusted aide, Yan Huan.

Seeing Li Fan's enigmatic smile, Shang Shaojun realized his question might have been inappropriate. He let out a soft cough to mask his embarrassment.

"The name 'Immortal Heart Mantra' is too conspicuous. Shaojun, you might consider giving it a more ordinary name before passing it down," Li Fan advised.

Recalling the technique derived from the Derivation Law Jade, Shang Shaojun turned to Yan Huan and said, "This is the [Heart Forging Technique], passed down by this senior. It will protect you from the erosion of demonic sounds. Spread it throughout the entire army as quickly as possible; ensure everyone learns it."

The name "Heart Forging Technique" was so commonplace that Yan Huan showed little excitement upon hearing Shang Shaojun's words. He merely bowed, accepted the order, and began practicing it on the spot.

"To possess such a secret technique and yet remain unaware of its value is truly..." Shang Shaojun remarked with a sigh, watching Yan Huan's retreating figure.

"If they knew how precious this technique is, they might become overly cautious and struggle to master it," Li Fan countered.

"The Qingxuan Army numbers a million strong. Though I've strictly forbidden its spread, the leakage of the Immortal Heart Mantra is likely inevitable. Aren't you worried, Daoist friend?" Shang Shaojun asked, looking at Li Fan with confusion.

He found Li Fan's actions since arriving at the Eternal Immortal Fortress utterly incomprehensible. It wasn't for fame, nor for profit.

"Xuanhuang cultivators cling to their secrets, influenced by the notion that techniques cannot be practiced together. Over time, their mindset has subtly shifted," Li Fan said.

"In the ancient cultivation world, people would sit together, discuss the Dao, and freely share knowledge. Such scenes are no longer seen."

"I dislike that."

The Holy Embryo Li Fan spoke calmly, his words carrying an air of transcendent detachment in the eyes of others.

"Let it spread. As long as it aligns with my will, what does it matter if it's an immortal technique?"

Shang Shaojun gazed at Li Fan's figure, momentarily entranced. Such boldness was truly rare in this world.

Being around such a figure, Shang Shaojun felt a surge of heroic spirit within himself.

...

Standing outside the fortress with Li Fan, Shang Shaojun gazed at the vast white mist. "Where do we go next?"

Having inexplicably taken on the burden of "saving the Xuanhuang Realm," Shang Shaojun felt a trace of uncertainty as they prepared to set out. But at this point, there was no turning back.

Glancing at Li Fan beside him, Shang Shaojun was certain that any attempt to back out now would be met with a swift sword strike.

"The fate of the Xuanhuang Realm rests on me alone. This feeling..." Shang Shaojun mused to himself, "It's not bad."

Li Fan extended his hand, and a small sword, no longer than his palm, instantly appeared in his grasp.

It seemed to be sensing something, spinning continuously.

Finally, the sword tip pointed in a direction and stopped.

Shang Shaojun looked in the direction the small sword indicated.

"Anything that exists must leave traces. Though that mysterious figure is immensely powerful, as long as they appear in the Xuanhuang Realm, they will inevitably interact with reality. By pursuing them, we can find more evidence," Li Fan said, his eyes gleaming.

Shang Shaojun found Li Fan's reasoning sound and nodded in agreement.

Unbeknownst to him, the direction the small sword in Li Fan’s hand pointed to was not toward tracking the mysterious figure.

Instead, it pointed to one after another of the enigmatic regions Li Fan had previously explored in his memories.

Of course, different paths lead to the same destination. They were bound to find, in those places, ample evidence of the Xuanhuang Realm being altered.

...

While the Holy Embryo Li Fan guided Shang Shaojun to uncover the truth hidden beneath the Xuanhuang Realm’s surface, in the small world of Great Qi, Master Yin’s experimental base had been preliminarily established.

"When I last met Mr. Bai, he told me that with my Foundation Establishment method, I might achieve the 'Primordial Golden Core,' 'Physical Nascent Soul,' or 'Heart-Mind Soul Transformation.' You could refer to these concepts for further deduction," Li Ping, Li Fan’s clone, said to Master Yin.

Master Yin had just performed a bizarre act in front of Li Ping.

His body split open from the middle, and another version of himself struggled to crawl out from within.

Then, turning back, he devoured his own fleshly shell.

The sound of flesh and bones being chewed echoed through the experimental base, with Master Yin’s face smeared with blood.

Yet his expression remained utterly calm, his eyes filled with focus as he ate himself.

It was as if he wasn’t eating but conducting some kind of research.

Even with Li Ping’s countless past memories, watching Master Yin made him feel a slight chill.

It wasn’t about courage; it was simply contrary to human nature.

Chewing slowly, Master Yin finally consumed the other version of himself entirely.

Li Ping noticed that Master Yin’s outwardly displayed cultivation level had fallen from Foundation Establishment to Qi Condensation.

Only then did Yin Shangren wipe his blood-stained hands on his clothes and explain, "To deduce the next stage of the method, I must first walk the correct path."

"My Foundation Establishment method has only half-succeeded. So, I must destroy my foundation and cultivate anew."

He glanced up at the sky: "It might cause some trouble, but leave it to me."

As Master Yin spoke, Li Ping suddenly sensed something descending upon the Holy Capital City.

Having cultivated the Heavenly Emperor’s Qi Classic and fused the entire dynasty’s fate into one, Li Ping alone represented the Great Qi Dynasty at this moment.

An external intrusion could not escape his perception.

It was as if cold worms were crawling slowly across the surface of his skin. After probing contact, the worms extended sharp spines, attempting to pierce into the core of the Great Qi small world.

"Hmm? This place seems to form a self-contained system. Their retaliation might be weaker than expected," Master Yin remarked.

Li Ping knew that the "they" Master Yin referred to were the lingering spectres of the old world.

It seemed that Master Yin’s forceful alteration of his state had drawn the attention of his kind.

The sound of dripping rain began.

Yet no raindrops fell.

Above Li Fan’s head, a thick layer of dark clouds had appeared without notice.

They loomed over the experimental base.

The hard basalt stone ground, amid the sound of dripping rain, suddenly became as muddy as soft earth.

One pale arm after another burst out from the ground.

Li Ping frowned slightly, attempting to float into the air to avoid them.

But in this strange, altered space, he found he had lost the ability to fly.

Fortunately, the target of these world specters was not him.

To him, those arms were merely like illusory shadows.

Countless arms crawled across the ground, swarming toward Master Yin.

Master Yin, however, revealed a sinister smile.

He grabbed the nearest arm, yanked it out, and stuffed it into his mouth, chewing voraciously.

Chapter 1155: Burying The Old, Opening A New Path

The clone Li Ping clearly saw that the arms torn to shreds by Master Yin's teeth showed no blood flowing out.

Instead, they were full of black pus.

Within the pus, countless tiny tentacle-like faces in agony emitted sharp, eerie wails. Yet Master Yin ignored them completely and swallowed them down directly.

Puff...

Master Yin's skin seemed to be sliced open by a sharp knife, and those tentacles he had swallowed into his belly drilled out from his body once more.

But this time, all the faces had transformed into Master Yin's own likeness!

Li Ping hid in a corner of this mutated space, quietly observing the changes in the field.

On the surface, it looked like Master Yin's mad, cannibalistic frenzy.

In truth, it was the fusion and collision of laws from different remnant worlds.

However, unlike the carefully selected laws from the Abyss that were meant to be integrated into the Xuanhuang Realm, what was happening here...

Was chaos, disorder, and destruction.

It was like being thrown into a garbage heap, where they frantically turned on each other for even the slightest chance of survival.

The struggle among the world spectres did not last long.

Perhaps because this realm was not the native Xuanhuang Realm, or perhaps due to the protection of the Great Qi Holy Dynasty's fortune, the phantoms that had descended were far weaker than Master Yin.

After he swallowed all the arms scattered across the ground into his belly,

The cracks across his body slowly began to heal.

“It has been a long time since I last enjoyed such a delicacy; I truly missed it a little.”

Master Yin's voice was no longer the previous bizarre dual tone but had returned to a normal human voice.

However, the aura he inadvertently emanated made Li Ping, who now possessed perfected Soul Transformation battle prowess, feel as if thorns pricked his back, secretly chilled to the bone.

Master Yin harbored no hostility, though. After resolving the hidden threat of the phantoms, he directly began cultivating [Foundation Built Upon Oneself].

Li Ping narrowed his eyes. In his view, the law fluctuations around Master Yin were rapidly diminishing.

They did not vanish inexplicably but were slowly drawn into Master Yin's body.

Master Yin's figure shifted like a veil of smoke, unstable and changeable. Those faces he had absorbed reappeared on his skin's surface.

Terror wandered endlessly across them as they struggled to break free from his body.

It looked as if Master Yin's body had simultaneously sprouted over a hundred fleshy tumors ready to burst at any moment. Grotesque and terrifying.

But Master Yin remained utterly resolute.

He sat in quiet meditation, showing no trace of panic.

Gradually, those faces seemed pulled by some force, retreating back into his body.

His eyes were tightly closed, seemingly still adjusting.

The aura on his body was also undergoing drastic changes at a visible speed.

As if from a sharp and radiant young man, in an instant he transformed into a steady-tempered master who had experienced countless tribulations.

"Thank you, Senior, for your kindness!"

After a long while, Sun Erlang opened his eyes.

His gaze was calm and profound.

"What did young friend experience?" Chen Tianhai asked knowingly.

"In the era of the Ten Great Immortal Sects of antiquity, before the great calamity descended. I was originally a rural commoner. By coincidence, I witnessed two Golden Core cultivators fighting and perishing together. Mustering my courage, I collected the resources from their corpses."

"It took three years before I finally opened the storage rings left by the two Golden Cores. From then on, I officially embarked on the path of cultivation..." Sun Erlang said indifferently.

Chen Tianhai knew that although Sun Erlang had glossed over it in one sentence, in that simulated world, just the process of opening the two storage rings had been extremely thrilling.

The old friends of the two Golden Cores came one after another, searching for the whereabouts of the inheritance.

Possessing a mountain of treasures but unable to enter the door. He staked everything, exhausted his family fortune, and finally obtained an extremely rudimentary cultivation method. Stumbling along, he eventually entered the Qi Condensation realm and could use the resources in the storage rings.

Sun Erlang recounted his experiences in the simulated world in detail, ultimately ending at the peak of Dao Integration with no hope of breaking through.

Although Chen Tianhai had already seen the other's experiences, though he still listened with great interest.

"If not for that one mistaken thought of mine back then, perhaps the Xuanhuang Realm today would still look like this."

Chen Tianhai thought to himself.

Chapter 1198: The Xuanhuang Realm’S Sinner

From a certain perspective, the Xuanhuang Realm had become what it was today, and Chen Tianhai bore a large share of the responsibility.

Because it was precisely he who had unauthorizedly activated the Dao Deviation Stone Mother and performed tens of billions of deductions.

Only then had the Immortal Ancestor glimpsed that one sliver of possibility for victory.

The fall of the Ten Great Immortal Sects and the six Longevity Immortals made Chen Tianhai an outright sinner!

Naturally, the countless deductions by the Dao Deviation Stone Mother could not be hidden. When his master, the Daoist Xingji, returned and discovered that this terrifying outcome was most likely caused by Chen Tianhai's internal sabotage, he flew into a rage. He intended to execute Chen Tianhai on the spot to appease the world.

However, after repeated consideration, the Daoist Xingji ultimately did not act.

First, because of the deep master-disciple bond, akin to father and son, Daoist Xingji ultimately softened.

Second, if this news truly spread, it would surely turn the Taiyan Sect into a target of public criticism, and it might even lead to the Taiyan Sect being erased from the Xuanhuang Realm. After all, traitors are always more hated than enemies.

But the tragic deaths of his old friends and the extreme self-reproach in his heart made it impossible for the Daoist Xingji to truly pretend nothing had happened.

Thus, he could only strip Chen Tianhai of his status as the first direct disciple, throw him into the Taiyan Sect's great prison for eternal suppression, and publicly declare that Chen Tianhai had defected.

Chen Tianhai stayed in the dark, sunless cell for who knows how long.

By the time he was released, it was when the calamity had descended and the Taiyan Sect faced a life-or-death crisis.

The World was in chaos, and the once-renowned Ten Great Immortal Sects were each struggling to survive.

The Taiyan Sect's disciples could use the Dao Deviation Stone to continuously run complex deductions, thereby suppressing the killing intent brought by the inability to cultivate techniques together. They had not fallen into the extreme chaos like the other immortal sects.

But it had already reached the limit.

Not only the Dao Deviation Stones within each cultivator's body, but even the Dao Deviation Stone Mother itself, due to the sudden appearance of [Extreme Variables], had overturned all previous deductions and begun rapidly re-deducing in order to seek a slim chance of survival for the Taiyan Sect. Its computational power was nearly exhausted. Before his master, the Daoist Xingji, perished from exhaustion, he released Chen Tianhai.

And pointed out a path for him.

Abandon the physical body; information is indestructible.

"Fortune and misfortune are interdependent. Although you committed a great error back then, it also allowed you to deduce the Immortal Ancestor's details."

"Now the Immortal Ancestor has been drawn away by our final measure and has temporarily left the Xuanhuang Realm. This has also bought us a brief respite to preserve our strength."

"Otherwise, I fear all our immortal sects' inheritances would have been destroyed!"

Before he could fully convey his last words, Daoist Xingji had already fallen.

And before the calamity of the inability to cultivate techniques together completely enveloped the Taiyan Sect, Chen Tianhai finally completed the act of integrating the entire Taiyan Sect into the Dao Deviation Stone Mother.

Fortunately, his master had already eliminated all opposing forces within the sect before his death.

Otherwise, Chen Tianhai would not have lasted until that moment.

Once every person in the sect could experience and access the near-infinite computational power of the Dao Deviation Stone Mother, Chen Tianhai knew that his master's decision had been correct.

And after re-integrating the segmented Dao Deviation Stones into his body, mixed with the wills of over a thousand cultivators from the Taiyan Sect, the Dao Deviation Stone Mother itself underwent some profound and wondrous change.

It birthed its own intelligence.

A collective consciousness belonging to the Taiyan Sect.

During the thousands of years the Taixuan Realm had secluded itself from the world, Chen Tianhai had reviewed the past and re-analyzed what he had done back then.

At that time, he had repeatedly felt uneasy and had even suspected he had fallen victim to the Immortal Ancestor's hidden move.

However, whether it was the sect's restrictive treasures, his master, or even the other Longevity Immortal experts from the Taishang and Heavenly Sword sects, none had found anything amiss with him.

Chen Tianhai could only assume it was simply his own inner demon at work.

"The Immortal Ancestor's methods are truly cunning eand hard to guard against."

"It's a pity that his details were not fully explained by my master before he passed, and the other knowledgeable ones in the immortal sects have long vanished without trace. But after so many years, I have deduced a rough outline..."

While listening to Sun Erlang recount his simulated experiences, Chen Tianhai's thoughts surged.

"By the way, Senior, the cultivation techniques I learned in the simulation world..." Sun Erlang hesitated.

"Of course they are real." Chen Tianhai said indifferently.

"But you had best not learn or use them in reality. Otherwise, you might invite pursuit from the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's Guardian Hall." Chen Tianhai added.

"The Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's Guardian Hall..." Sun Erlang murmured, yet there was no fear in his eyes.

"Very well. Since Young Friend has gained greatly, please return now. Do not keep your master waiting too long." Chen Tianhai issued the order to leave.

"Yi Shu will accompany you to the Great Qi small world."

Before the words had even faded, Sun Erlang felt his vision blur.

When he came to his senses, he was already outside the blue transparent building.

Yi Shu stood smiling nearby, seemingly having waited for quite some time.

"Hmm?" Sun Erlang immediately noticed that the current Yi Shu was different from before.

In fact, he was different from all the other cultivators in the Taiyan Sect.

No longer the cold, completely uniform collective aura, but a living, breathing existence with his own vitality.

"Thanks to you, Young Friend, I have temporarily regained a physical body in reality and can go out to enjoy myself for a while." Yi Shu smiled as he explained the current state of the Taiyan Sect to Sun Erlang.

"Information is indestructible..." After hundreds of years of tempering in the simulation world, Sun Erlang was noticeably much steadier.

Yet he was still shocked by the Taiyan Sect's fantastical concept.

"Young Friend need not be surprised. If you truly experienced it personally like we have, you would know that having or lacking a physical body makes little difference. In fact, a real body is nothing but a burden." Yi Shu said with a chuckle.

"But occasionally switching things up is not bad either."

Yi Shu followed Sun Erlang out of the Taixuan Realm, heading straight for the Congyun Sea and the entrance to the Great Qi small world.

Clearly, the Taiyan Sect was no stranger to the location of the Great Qi small world.

However, after passing through the Immortal Severing Grand Formation and seeing only a vast expanse of empty void with no small world in sight, Yi Shu finally showed a trace of surprise.

Sun Erlang was first stunned, then his memories from a hundred years ago seemed to resurface.

He said somewhat embarrassedly: "Senior, please wait a moment. Our Holy Dynasty of Great Qi is currently adrift in the void, floating without fixed position. I do not know where it is right now..."

"Let me ask."

Moments later, a golden light shot out from the darkness and arrived before the two in an instant.

"Greetings, Senior Hundred Flowers!" Sun Erlang recognized the newcomer and respectfully saluted.

Daoist Hundred Flowers glanced over Yi Shu without saying much.

"Follow me."

The glazed golden light enveloped the two. The surrounding void twisted as if distorted, emitting strange radiance.

When the golden light dissipated, they had already returned to the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi.

Chapter 1199: The Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior

“Senior Hundred Flowers, it seems your cultivation has advanced quite a bit?”

After experiencing a hundred years of simulation, Sun Erlang was now a completely different person. He naturally noticed the changes that had occurred in Daoist Hundred Flowers during the time he had been away.

He could not help feeling a chill in his heart.

As for Yi Shu, he looked with interest at the five interlocking rings in the sky that echoed and connected with each other, as well as the six bright stars surrounding the rings.

“Senior Hundred Flowers has relocated two more Cave-Heavens. Truly laborious. These new two Cave-Heavens seem even larger in scale than the previous ones. No wonder I feel that the speed of Great Qi’s movement through the void has obviously increased significantly.” Sun Erlang thought to himself.

“The Holy Dynasty of Great Qi truly lives up to its reputation.” Yi Shu sensed the various forces enveloping the small world around him at all times and spoke with genuine sincerity.

“To think at most within fifty years, a mere mortal world could undergo such earth-shaking changes. I have truly become curious about your master, young friend.”

Daoist Hundred Flowers inexplicably glanced at Yi Shu once more.

Yet she said nothing, merely giving a cold snort.

After bringing the two before the Holy Emperor’s throne, she left straight away.

Looking at the majestic figure seated high above, like a towering mountain, Sun Erlang’s heart trembled.

He had originally thought that after experiencing so many lifelike worlds in the simulation, and having seen those supreme Longevity Immortal realm cultivators from the former Ten Great Immortal Sects, he would appear more at ease when facing his master upon returning.

But at this moment, Sun Erlang knew he was wrong.

When facing Longevity Immortal realm experts, what Sun Erlang felt was the pressure from the gap in realms, the might of the strong over the weak.

But facing his own master, the Faceless Holy Emperor of Great Qi.

The reason Sun Erlang’s body, mind, and even divine soul could not stop trembling was due to the unfathomable mystery of the Faceless Holy Emperor, and...

The natural condescension of a higher life form toward a lower one.

“Master, I have returned.” Sun Erlang knelt on one knee, just as always.

Yi Shu merely cupped his hands slightly.

“It is good that you have returned. It seems this trip yielded quite a harvest for you.” The voice of the Faceless Holy Emperor Li Ping echoed in the vast, towering Holy Emperor’s throne.

Sun Erlang recounted every detail of his experiences without omission.

After listening to Sun Erlang’s report, Li Ping then said to Yi Shu: “Since the Taiyan Sect has been so courteous, we cannot be negligent either. During this time, you shall lead him on a tour around the Holy Dynasty.”

“No restrictions. Wherever this guest wishes to see, you may take him.”

Sun Erlang was slightly stunned, then hurriedly said: “Disciple understands.”

“Go ahead.”

There was actually no intention to exchange words with Yi Shu.

Although Yi Shu was the collective consciousness incarnation of the Taiyan Sect, he also possessed his own personal temperament.

He tilted his head slightly, looking at the Faceless Holy Emperor seated high above, and cupped his hands once more.

Until he left the Holy Emperor’s throne, that Holy Emperor did not utter another word.

“A thousand words are not as good as one sight. After you take this walk, Senior, you will naturally have the answer in your heart.” To prevent the other from feeling awkward, Sun Erlang quickly explained.

“Very well, then let me see exactly what is so special about this place.” Yi Shu responded with a smile as well.

However, his smile seemed somewhat different from before.

When he saw that almost everyone in the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi was cultivating the same kind of technique, a trace of surprise flashed in Yi Shu’s eyes.

When he saw the Immortal Spirit Towers placed casually throughout the Holy Dynasty, constantly emitting spiritual energy outward, the smile on Yi Shu’s face slowly disappeared.

When he observed the Heaven and Earth Myriad Spirits Great Formation connecting the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi with other small worlds, Yi Shu’s expression had already become one of shock and uncertainty.

“Young friend, do you know...”

Yi Shu’s words reached his lips but were swallowed back.

Sun Erlang thought the other was shocked by the Holy Dynasty’s sights. Seeing that the other ultimately did not speak, he did not press further.

Next, Sun Erlang took Yi Shu to visit some important sites in the Holy Dynasty.

For example, Wang Xuanba’s exotic beast cultivation base, and the forging site of the Sky-Patrolling Arrows.

As they walked along, Yi Shu grew increasingly silent.

Traces of deep puzzlement and doubt flashed in his brows from time to time.

Seeing the other’s expression, Sun Erlang, who had been constantly shocked while in the Taiyan Sect, could not help feeling a sense of satisfaction and secretly pleased.

“Senior Yi, is there anything else you wish to see?”

Yi Shu did not respond for a short while.

His gaze looked into the distance, as if wanting to take in this entire miraculous small world.

“If it weren’t for you saying you want to counterattack the Xuanhuang Realm, I would almost think this is some hidden secret realm of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance.” After a long time, Yi Shu suddenly said this.

“Aside from the number of cultivators and top experts being a bit fewer, you indeed have the qualifications to challenge the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance.”

“However, based on what I have seen so far, it is still not enough to form an alliance with our Taiyan Sect.” Yi Shu reached this conclusion.

“Young friend, the waters of the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance are far deeper than they appear.” Yi Shu spoke with implied meaning.

Sun Erlang did not feel dissatisfied with Yi Shu’s reaction. Instead, he nodded slightly, deep in thought.

After thinking for a moment, Sun Erlang ultimately brought Yi Shu to the underground space beneath the Holy Emperor’s throne, where Yin Shangren resided.

He did not see Yin Shangren himself.

But just by looking at the hundred or so young children here who were already at Foundation Establishment stage despite their tender age, Yi Shu showed signs of losing composure for the first time.

“This...” With his eyesight, he instantly saw the special nature of these Foundation Establishment children.

“How is this possible?”

In that instant, the entire Taiyan Sect in the Taixuan Realm, from top to bottom, all turned their gazes toward the Great Qi small world.

Even the Dao Deviation Stone Mother, which constantly bloomed with azure light, seemed to pause briefly.

“Not long ago, a young boy advanced further on the basis of Foundation Establishment Built Upon Oneself Method, creating a Primordial Golden Core.” Sun Erlang said softly.

“Foundation Establishment Built Upon Oneself?!” Upon hearing this term, Yi Shu’s entire person froze instantly.

It was not a figure of speech; his thoughts truly fell into stagnation.

Sun Erlang, who carried the Dao Deviation Stone, could also see at a glance that this was the manifestation of the Dao Deviation Stone’s computational power operating at its limit.

“Impossible!”

After a long while, Yi Shu blurted out.

The children here, who were bitterly pondering and diligently cultivating, all turned their gazes toward Yi Shu.

There was some displeasure in their eyes.

Because they had been taught by Yin Shangren since childhood, and the three words “impossible” were what they resisted most.

Yi Shu realized his loss of composure and lightly coughed to cover it up.

He slowly walked before one of the delicate and lovely little girls and observed her carefully.

The more he looked, the more shocked he became.

Sun Erlang saw that Yi Shu’s hand even clenched tightly uncontrollably.

“Beyond the variables? Beyond the variables?”

“No, this is not beyond the variables, this is...”

Yi Shu suddenly raised his head, as if meeting the eyes of the Faceless Holy Emperor seated on the Holy Emperor’s throne.

“The Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior?!”

Chapter 1200: No One Can Save The Heavens From Collapsing

Many years ago, when Yi Shu first learned of the Du'e Sect's theory, he was just like most Taiyan Sect cultivators and held it in absolute contempt.

When a calamity arrived, instead of actively seeking ways to counter it, they chose to hide like turtles and pin their hopes on some vague, illusory calamity-savior. This was completely baseless and an unpredictable variable.

However, as one of the Ten Great Immortal Sects with a lineage inherited from the Grand Mage of Heavenly Capital, the Du'e Sect's tradition had already appeared in archaic times. Surviving for so many years proved that it indeed held some unique truth.

It was simply that outsiders could not glimpse the profundity of the Du'e Sect.

Today, when Yi Shu arrived at this Holy Dynasty of Great Qi, which seemed to have suddenly sprung from a crack in the stones, and witnessed numerous technologies that rivaled the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance's highest level, as well as a brand-new cultivation method completely different from current practices, one that could thoroughly overturn the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor's foundation in the Xuanhuang Realm.

In Yi Shu's mind, the words spoken by the Du'e Sect back then instantly resurfaced.

At that time, the Great Calamity had just begun to show signs. The core leaders of the Ten Immortal Sects gathered together to discuss possible countermeasures.

Each sect freely expressed its views, yet only the Du'e Sect's attitude was extremely passive.

"Fellow Daoists, as the saying goes, if one can avoid a calamity through mere struggle, then is it truly a calamity?" The Du'e Sect leader, Lu Chengheng, sat with eyes lowered and a look of complete detachment.

"Is this the reason your Du'e Sect has placed those so-called [World-Observing Divine Eyes] everywhere?"

The most hot-tempered Lord of the Indeterminate Prison, Xiahou Yan, wore an unpleasant expression and retorted on the spot.

"Xiahou, you do not understand. This is a necessary measure to await the appearance of the Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior." Lu Chengheng said indifferently.

"Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior, my ass. In the face of calamity, all living beings perish. Once everyone is dead, where could this savior possibly emerge from?" Xiahou nearly cursed aloud.

"Are you questioning the Grand Mage's inheritance?" Lu Chengheng's single sentence left Xiahou Yan speechless.

"Besides, there is still all of you." Lu Chengheng smiled, then added meaningfully, "The Xuanhuang Realm is not so easily destroyed. If there is any Immortal World in this star sea that can survive to the very end, it will certainly be our Xuanhuang."

The ten sect leaders heard this and none offered any rebuttal.

After a brief silence, perhaps to reassure everyone, Lu Chengheng briefly explained: “The notion of crossing calamity is not baseless talk. Great tribulations and cycles of destruction and rebirth are, by nature, part of the workings of Heaven and Earth. Even the grand catastrophe of the Immortal Realm may not be beyond the expectations of the True Immortals.”

"You are all in constant panic. Just look at the Grand Mage. Has He shown even the slightest disturbance?"

The Taishang Sect master, who had remained silent until now, Xuan Ji, finally spoke: "The Grand Mage's cultivation reaches the level of creation. Even in the immortal ruins, He treads as if on flat ground. Disasters capable of destroying the Xuanhuang Realm hold no concern for Him. We cannot match that. Brother Lu, I will not stop you from choosing to seclude yourselves. But in facing the calamity, you cannot contribute absolutely no effort."

Under everyone's collective gaze, Lu Chengheng finally nodded and made a compromise.

However, Lu Chengheng's final words plunged everyone present into silence once more.

"One day, you will all know that our Du'e Sect was right."

At this moment, spanning thousands of years, the collective consciousness of the Taiyan Sect and Yi Shu's body suddenly recalled Lu Chengheng's words from back then.

After gaining a superficial understanding of the principle behind [Foundation Establishment Built Upon Oneself] , the Dao Deviation Stone Mother incorporated this variable into its derivations of the Xuanhuang Realm's future.

The originally clear future became blurred and indistinct, as if encountering a chaotic storm.

Even with the Stone Mother's current god-like computational power, it grew somewhat confused for a time.

The Taiyan Sect had actually once attempted to derive a brand-new cultivation method capable of overturning the Immortal Ancestor system.

But...

Creating something from nothing was not the same as deriving the future based on existing data.

The Dao Deviation Stone Mother had only determined that such a possibility existed. However, the probability was too small.

It was even smaller than the probability that the Immortal Ancestor would survive the encirclement by Longevity Immortal realm cultivators back then.

Beyond the sheer difficulty of creating the new method itself, what mattered more was the Immortal Ancestor's omnipresent influence on the Xuanhuang Realm.

Just as cultivators gradually forgot the Xuanhuang Realm's calamities and infinitely overlooked the terrain changes that had been occurring for thousands of years.

Deriving a new method was not only the ultimate expression of creative power.

It was also a contest against the Immortal Ancestor's influence.

Thus, in the Dao Deviation Stone Mother's derivations, which clearly understood the current strength of the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor, the probability of a new method being born naturally approached zero.

Yet something far beyond the derived possibilities had precisely occurred.

In a remote corner of the Xuanhuang Realm floating in the void, unknown to anyone.

In a force that had only recently emerged.

Not only had the brand-new method quietly appeared, but it had even developed to the Golden Core stage!

It truly struck like a thunderbolt, causing even the Dao Deviation Stone Mother's derivations to stutter slightly.

Because based on all currently known factors, no reason could be found for the new new method to emerge at this time point.

It could only be explained by the Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior, something beyond common sense and outside the variables.

The Ten Sects had privately discussed the Xuanhuang Calamity-Savior to some extent.

They reached a nearly unanimous conclusion: If such a so-called savior truly existed, he must either come from outside the Xuanhuang Realm or be the reincarnation of some powerful expert.

A native cultivator growing up solely by his own power, wanting to single-handedly save the heavens from collapsing...

Was fundamentally impossible.

No matter how talented, it would not work.

The environment of the Xuanhuang Realm could no longer produce the foundational soil capable of opposing the Immortal Ancestor.

"Just what is the background of this Faceless Holy Emperor of Great Qi?"

At this moment, in Yi Shu's mind, or rather in the Taiyan Sect's collective consciousness, all contempt had completely vanished.

Instead, they regarded the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi as an equal power, or even one a level higher.

And they truly began deriving the possibility of allying with the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi.

As one of the former rulers of the Xuanhuang Realm, now compressed by the Dharma-Transmitting Immortal Ancestor's Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance into only the Taixuan small world, it was impossible for the Taiyan Sect to harbor no resentment.

Even though they no longer needed territory or resources, the fact that what they once possessed had been stripped away left a resentment in the Taiyan Sect's consciousness that lingered like a nightmare.

"Back then, sects such as the Grand Dao and the Taishang, although they outwardly opposed the Ten Thousand Immortal Alliance, had in reality already colluded with it and become no different from the Immortal Ancestor's lackeys."

"But presumably, like us, they have not fully submitted. What they lack is merely an opportunity."

And the appearance of the Holy Dynasty of Great Qi, especially the appearance of the brand-new method, gave the Taiyan Sect a glimpse of that sliver of hope.

However, after all, it was a sect that had survived since the ancient calamities.

Any decision concerning their fate was made with the utmost caution.
