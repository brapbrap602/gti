Chapter 1401: That Was Amazingly Splendid. (1)

“Enemy ahead! Enemy, soldiers!”

“It is Mount Hua!”

“Mount Hua is attacking, Commander!”

A frantic, no, a desperate wail poured out.

But that cry did not reach Ho Ga-Myung. Frozen like a stone in place, he stared with wide eyes at the onrushing figures.

‘Why?’

Ho Ga-Myung, faced with something beyond the limits of his imagination, could think nothing, could make no response.

‘How?’

Why is the Heavenly Friends Alliance, the Mount Hua, here?

It shouldn’t have been possible to find them. Even if they had crossed the Yangtze, in this vast Southern land it would have been impossible to track their whereabouts. So how did this happen?

“Why…”

A stifled voice slipped out between his lips. What had been a mutter swelled and finally burst into a shout.

“Whaaaaat forrrrr!”

The whole forest echoed.

Footsteps shook the earth, and with that rumble a hundred Mount Hua disciples charged as one. The rage in their eyes poured out directly onto the Ten Thousand People Clan members who threatened Mount Hua’s swords.

No one had given orders. Nothing had been prepared in advance.

Yet everyone’s will to run toward a single place naturally formed their ranks into a single shape.

A wedge piercing the enemy. No, closer to a straight, thrusting blade, Mount Hua’s swordsmen formed a formation like a long spear and, with explosive force, drove toward the Ten Thousand People Clan’s encirclement.

“Oh, they’re coming!”

“B-b-block them… No! Get out of the way! Move! Move!”

Urgent screams burst out everywhere. But they soon had to face one-armed swordsmen who, like asura, let out a killing intent and charged.

“Oooooo!”

The Plum Blossom Sword, filled with red sword qi, carried boiling rage and struck the enemy’s neck.

Swoooosh!

A Ten Thousand People Clan disciple’s head, cut off with a face twisted in terror, shot into the air.

Having severed a life in an instant, Un Geom leapt into the enemy lines at an even greater speed.

“Break in!”

Kraaaang!

Hyun Sang slammed his foot into the ground and spread his sword. Red sword qi bloomed and spread like plum blossoms in all directions.

“R-retreat… Gyaaah!”

Ten Thousand People Clan disciples were helplessly pierced by the sword energy.

The two swords at the front were strong. And the swords of the Mount Hua disciples that followed were even more powerful.

Swoooosh!

Red sword qi kept bursting like fireworks. From above, it would look like red plum blossoms blooming in sequence along the path of the vanguard.

Truly a beautiful, fantastical sight.

But the beauty concealed a deadly blade.

Shick! Shick!

Each time the sword qi, fluttering like petals and seeming infinitely delicate, scattered in all directions, crimson wounds inevitably bloomed on the enemies’ bodies.

“Aaaaaaargh!”

A cold, blue killing intent rippled in the eyes of Mount Hua’s swordsmen.

Because they could see clearly what was before them: their sahyung, wounded all over the body and dying.

Crunch!

No one shouted insults at the enemy. No one cheered those around them. Instead, they poured their overflowing anger and hatred into their blades without reserve.

Even shouting was a luxury. There was only one thing they had to do now: save their bleeding sahyung-sajaes.

Swoooosh!

The honed red sword energy mercilessly ravaged the enemy. Mount Hua’s swordsmen, united as a single red blade, moved forward, only forward.

“D-damn, stop them! Don’t let them break through…”

Krrraak!

The one who had tried to shout in panic fell backward. A black poisoned dagger stuck in the mouth of the one who died without even closing his eyes.

“P-poison!”

“It’s the Tang Family! Aah! Bow your heads!”

Swoooosh!

They tried to reorganize their ranks somehow, but countless daggers flew from above and black poison dispersed, so there was nothing they could do. The Ten Thousand People Clan’s formation began to crumble.

“Provide cover! Don’t let Mount Hua gain a foothold over us!”

At Tang Zhan’s shout, the Tang Family members screamed at the top of their lungs.

In front, Mount Hua’s disciples fired like a single blade, while overhead the Tang Family launched hidden weapons and poison that scattered.

It was a truly vicious combination. Even a Ten Thousand People Clan at full strength would find it difficult to face this, not to mention the Ten Thousand People Clan who had nearly exhausted themselves pursuing from the Southern Island to here.

“Why, why are Mount Hua’s bastards here!”

“D-don’t push, damn you!”

Those trying to retreat, those desperately trying to fight, and the hesitant all tangled together as the formation fell apart.

Around the red-blooming Plum Blossoms, green flashes of light embroidered the air.

At that moment, a Ten Thousand People Clan disciple shouted.

“Damn it! Don’t panic, there aren’t that many of them…!”

Kraaaang!

But his body was swept away by a massive white sword qi that came from somewhere. Following it, swordsmen dressed in sky-blue robes appeared.

“Save the Young lord and the alliance members!”

“Acknowledged!”

A blue sky was cast over the Plum Blossom grove. The Namgung Family’s swordsmen, fitting the name of blue heavens perfectly, rushed with all their might toward the Ten Thousand People Clan disciples.

“Palace Lord! We’ve arrived!”

Following them came swordsmen in ice-cold white robes, and warriors of the Beast Palace led by Beast Palace Lord Meng So also charged in.

“Sweep away the Ten Thousand People Clan bastard!”

“Yes!”

With Meng So’s roar booming as if to echo to the heavens, the Beast Palace forces charged fiercely like predators hunting prey.

Those who called each other friends. Those who called themselves the alliance members had gathered here to protect those they must protect.

And then.

“Save Mount Hua’s Sword Saint! Now is the time to repay at least a bit of debt!”

“Yes!”

With Southern Island Sect Leader Geum Yang-Baek’s cry, the Southern Island’s disciples led by Gwak Hwan-So also charged with all their strength.

Though their bodies were already exhausted, those who spoke of chivalry with their mouths and of justice with their swords had duties they must perform even if it cost them their last breath.

United in one will, they surged toward the enemy like lightning.

“Damn it! Why are they here…!”

“Block them! Blo…”

Swords flew continuously toward the enemies who slashed like madmen.

Swoooosh!

A severed neck shot into the air. Hyun Jong gripped the Plum Blossom Sword tightly, having cut an enemy’s head off with a single, merciless strike.

His children were right there.

Bleeding all over their bodies.

Seeing disciples too weak to raise their bodies, a fierce anger unlike anything he’d ever felt before rose in Hyun Jong’s chest.

Who did this?

Who dared make those children bleed?

Swoooosh!

A sword steeped in immense internal qi cleaved a retreating Ten Thousand People Clan member in two at once. Warm blood splashed and stained the white hair, but Hyun Jong walked the blood-soaked path without hesitation.

This was the price for his hesitation.

The blood he should have shed was being shed by the children.

Really though, who are they? Who made those children bleed?

Kraaaaang!

The Purple Cloud Divine Arts unleashed at full intensity.

A tremendous sword qi fell forward, causing an explosion so massive it was hard to even stand.

Shick!

At that moment a blade flew from the flank and brushed Hyun Jong’s cheek. Skin split and blood spattered, but Hyun Jong felt not even the smallest pain.

What truly pained him was not wounds carved into this old body.

Each nick on the children’s bodies, every drop of blood spilled here, lodged into Hyun Jong’s heart like rusted daggers.

“Ugh, aaaah!”

Kraaaang!

Hyun Jong stomped the ground after cleaving an enemy in two with a single strike.

“Cover the former Sect Leader!”

Mount Hua’s swordsmen moved in perfect unison to Hyun Jong’s left and right.

Their will was communicated without words. No order was needed; everyone looked to the same place.

“D-damn, stop…”

Krrrak! Krrrak! Krrrak! Krrrak!

Green flashes of light struck and pierced the skulls of those charging at Hyun Jong without fail.

Hyun Jong advanced through the collapsing bodies turned cold corpses. Red sword energy rose from his blade.

“Hyun Sang!”

“Yes!”

Martial Hall leader Hyun Sang became his sword and drove back the enemies.

“Un Geom!”

“Yes!”

White Plum Blossom Boarding Hall Leader, Un Geom became his shield and pushed away the attackers at once.

Hyun Jong advanced along that path—the path Mount Hua’s blades had pierced and Mount Hua’s shields had guarded. The path opened by everyone’s united will.

Kraaaang!

And Hyun Jong walked toward the path cleaved open by his own blade.

Step. Step.

Hyun Jong’s steps gradually slowed.

Step. Step.

And after a moment, his footsteps stopped completely.

Having broken through all who stood in his way to come here, Hyun Jong silently gazed at those before him.

His vision kept blurring. If he parted his lips even slightly, sobs, no, wails, would pour out.

“Ah…”

A dazed voice escaped someone’s mouth, as if they couldn’t believe the reality of Hyun Jong being here.

Step.

Hyun Jong stepped a little closer and gave a heavy pat to the shoulder his hand reached.

“Sect…”

Jo Gul stared at him as if spellbound.

“Sect le…”

Yoon Jong, Tang Soso, and Yu Yiseol as well.

Hyun Jong, touching the shoulders of disciples still unable to let go of their swords one by one, finally stood before someone kneeling on one knee at the very back.

He stood in silence and just looked for a moment, at Baek Cheon, clutching Chung Myung as if protecting him even in this state.

They say the chest feels torn; this must be what they mean.

Just as Hyun Jong reached out with a trembling hand, Baek Cheon flinched and moved. Hyun Jong’s hand stopped abruptly.

“I report to the Former Sect Leader…”

Even so, Baek Cheon managed to compose himself and saluted Hyun Jong.

“The Great Mount Hua Sect’s… Acting Sect Leader Baek Cheon, I could not fully… carry out the order, but…”

“…”

“…I carried the mission out without betraying Mount Hua’s will.”

Hyun Jong’s paused hand moved slowly again and then settled onto Baek Cheon’s shoulder.

A hand no longer trembling gently patted Baek Cheon’s shoulder.

“Amazing.”

“…”

“Amazingly splendid.” This time Baek Cheon’s shoulders trembled in small motions. Hyun Jong accepted that tremor as it was and nodded slowly.

A gentle smile bloomed at the corners of his wrinkled mouth. Tears gathered at his eyes and the kindly smile reassured those who saw it.

“Leave the rear to me and the sahyung-sajaes. We’re here now.”

Mount Hua’s disciples, smashing through the enemy, surrounded and escorted their wounded sahyung like bodyguards.

Seeing backs imbued with resolute will, Baek Cheon finally let go of what he’d been suppressing and suppressed again. His eyes grew hot, and tears streamed down his face already dried by blood.

“…I receive the command, Former Sect Leader.”

Just as Chung Myung was not alone, they themselves were not alone.

They were here. The place they had to go.

The place they were supposed to return to had come to them here.

Chapter 1402: That Was Amazingly Splendid. (2)

“Hold the line! Defend with your lives!”

Rough voices swept across the battlefield.

Everyone surged like a torrent, encircling the besieged Five Swords and their party. Mount Hua’s disciples, Tang Family members, and even the swordsmen from the Namgung Family who arrived afterward rushed the enemies and pushed them back.

“Fall back, you dogs of the Evil Sect!”

Into the space created by the Heavenly Friends Alliance throwing their bodies, the remaining Mount Hua disciples plunged.

“Sahyung!”

“Damn it, sasuk!”

The sight of their sahyung-sajaes in shambles stirred fierce hatred in the Mount Hua disciples’ eyes.

Un Am’s thunderous voice stopped those who, unable to contain their rage, were about to turn and charge the enemies.

“Hold your positions!”

“S-Sect leader…”

“Our task is not to hunt the enemy! Protect your sahyung-sajaes. Let none of them breach this place!”

“Yes!”

Mount Hua’s disciples, their eyes filled with killing intent, drove back the Ten Thousand People Clan members who had targeted Chung Myung and his group. The Ten Thousand People Clan, thrown by the flurry of blades, found themselves repeatedly pushed back in confusion.

Mount Hua and the Heavenly Friends Alliance formed a solid wall the enemies could not penetrate.

“Yoon Jong!”

Meanwhile, Hyun Sang, who had rushed like lightning, was momentarily at a loss for words in front of Yoon Jong. As the stunned Hyun Sang barely gathered himself to check Yoon Jong’s pulse, Yoon Jong suddenly shook his head and weakly pointed elsewhere.

“Elder… Chung Myung, take care of Chung Myung…”

“Hey, you fool! Who’s supposed to care for who right now…!”

“Elder, hurry, Chung Myung is…”

Hyun Sang clenched his lips. At that moment Baek Cheon stepped forward and handed over Chung Myung he had been holding.

“Elder…”

“…”

“My qi… Chung Myung…”

Although the words came out haltingly, Hyun Sang immediately understood the meaning.

But he could not bring himself to take Chung Myung. He had no idea where to place his hands—there wasn’t a single uninjured spot; how could he touch him?

“Stand aside.”

“S-Sect leader. Chung Myung…”

“Get a hold of yourself!”

At that moment Hyun Jong shoved Hyun Sang aside and took Chung Myung in his arms. His sleeve instantly soaked with Chung Myung’s blood, yet Hyun Jong showed no flinch as he pushed his mighty internal qi into Chung Myung’s abdomen.

“I-is he alright? Is he going to be okay?”

As Hyun Sang asked urgently, Hyun Jong hardened his face and shouted instead of answering.

“Those who have any remaining strength, tend to the wounded! Now!”

“…Yes?”

“What are you all doing! Is Chung Myung the only injured one here?”

“Yes? Ah, yes! Former Sect leader!”

Those words snapped them to attention.

Of course Chung Myung was the worst off, but the others had suffered injuries that words couldn’t fully describe.

Those with enough internal qi rushed over and began imbuing qi. Hyun Sang likewise seized Yoon Jong and poured qi into his back.

“…S-Sect leader.”

“Don’t speak, Gul!”

Un Am placed his hand on Jo Gul’s back and poured qi into him.

“I—rather than me, the others…”

“Don’t say another word!”

“I’m still fine! Start with the sasuk…”

“I told you to shut your mouth!”

Un Am bit his lip. Even now he had the mind to worry about others—whether that was kindness or stupidity, it was impossible to tell.

‘These foolish bastards…’

How could they neglect their own bodies so? He was proud, yet unbearably resentful.

Among them, there was one who obeyed the least.

“Soso! You can’t do that!”

“Let go!”

“Soso! Your injuries are severe too!”

“I’m not dead, so let go!”

Tang Soso repeatedly refused those trying to treat her and clung to Chung Myung, whom Hyun Jong was holding. Before Hyun Jong could stop her, Tang Soso grabbed Chung Myung’s wrist, checked his pulse, and lifted her head violently.

“Father? Where is the Tang Family Lord?”

“He’s holding back the enemy’s advance. He’ll be here soon.”

“Quick! As fast as possible! Former Sect leader! The sahyung is dying!”

Hyun Jong shouted as if there were no need to think.

“Un Geom! Bring the Tang Family Lord! At once!”

“That’s not necessary.”

At that moment, a streak of green light suddenly landed before them.

“Father!”

“This way.”

Tang Gunak seized Chung Myung as if snatching him away. His face, hard as rock as he checked his condition, went pale in an instant. Seeing his usually unshakeable calm crumble was more chilling than any shout or scream.

“What were you thinking!”

Tang Gunak bit his lip, laid Chung Myung out gently with hands like a carving knife, took a small box from his robe, opened it, and pulled out a dozen large golden needles.

“No one! Let no one approach us, absolutely no one!”

Hyun Jong didn’t need to say anything. The moment those words were heard, the Mount Hua disciples’ momentum shifted. Even those who could barely stand rose to protect Chung Myung, and they had to be restrained.

“Soso! Tang Pae! Help!”

“Yes!”

“Tang Family Lord. Soso also…”

“Do not interfere, step back!”

Baek Sang, who tried to stop the Tang Family Lord, paled and stepped back at the deadly command, realizing how dire the situation was.

“Tang Pae!”

“Yes, Lord!”

Tang Pae shoved aside the one pouring qi into him and leapt forward. As he attached himself to Chung Myung’s opposite side, Tang Gunak channeled qi into the extracted needles.

Wooooooom!

The golden needles in his hand trembled wildly. At the same time, Tang Gunak’s long robe swelled as if about to burst under the strain of the qi. The sheer force made even the Mount Hua disciples turn around for an instant.

As Tang Soso began to be pushed back by the qi wave, Hyun Jong grabbed her shoulder and breathed qi into her.

WOOOOOOOOOOM!

The golden needles on Tang Gunak’s palm thrashed like live carp and then quieted. The faint tremor ceased completely, and Tang Gunak thrust the needles into Chung Myung’s throat like lightning.

Chung Myung’s body convulsed on the spot. Tang Gunak pressed him down and shouted.

“Poisoned dagger, navel point, gui (face intersection) point!”

“Yes!”

Tang Pae and Tang Soso struck the pressure points Tang Gunak named.

“Three mm deeper at the navel point!”

“F-Father. Sahyung’s body…”

“Do as instructed!”

“Yes!”

Tang Soso gritted her teeth and struck the points Tang Gunak ordered. Black, dead blood gushed from Chung Myung’s mouth.

WOOOOOM!

Another large needle plunged into the center of Chung Myung’s chest. A long needle the size of an adult palm sank entirely into his body and disappeared.

“Universal Acupoint, Upper abdomen point, Foot paralysis point!”

“Yes!”

Tang Family Lord Tang Gunak was a warrior who had crossed into the realm of the absolute. Yet his face was soon soaked with sweat. The scene made clear how much spirit and power this needle technique was consuming.

Wooooooom!

Tang Gunak bit his teeth and poured power into the needles again. The sudden drain on his inner strength made his hands begin to tremble.

After a moment, the last of the twelve needles pierced into the very center of Chung Myung’s crown.

“Is it okay to do that to a person’s head?”

When someone finally couldn’t stand it and shouted, Tang Soso answered loudly.

“They’re not needles!”

“…Huh?”

She had no strength left to speak more, so she stopped answering and seized Chung Myung’s wrist, pouring qi into him.

The golden needles Tang Gunak used were not truly needles. They were spiritual items refined from rare ingredients, even the Tang Family rarely possessed them. Each one could not be valued in mere coin.

Within the Tang Family they were called the Rejuvenating Great Golden Needles. They were heirlooms permitted only to the Tang Family Lord.

In other words, Tang Gunak was, without a moment’s hesitation, expending a Tang heirloom that had been preserved for generations on someone who was not even a member of the Tang Family.

But…

“It’s not enough.”

Tang Gunak snapped his head up.

“Ice Palace! Bring the elders of the Ice Palace here! Beast Palace Lord too! Now!”

“Un Am! Hyun Sang!”

“Yes! Sect leader!”

“Open a path! Bring the Ice Palace and the Beast Palace here at once! Hurry!”

Hyun Jong and Un Am ran out with determined faces. Hyun Jong placed his hand on Chung Myung’s dantian and quietly spoke to Tang Gunak, who was channeling qi.

“…Tang Family Lord, Chung Myung…”

“I’ve done everything I can right now.”

“…Can he live?”

Tang Gunak did not answer. But Hyun Jong did not take the silence as a negative. The Tang Family Lord was pouring every ounce of remaining internal qi into tending Chung Myung—that showed his will to save him.

And Tang Gunak was not the only one showing that resolve.

“Stand aside, you vermin!”

Kraaaang!

Beast Palace Lord Meng So struck aside a dozen Ten Thousand People Clan members in one blow and arrived where they were.

“Where is the Mount Hua’s Sword Saint!”

As he bellowed loudly, Ice Palace people charged in behind him.

“Palace Lord!”

“Commander Han, not me! Over there!”

Seol So-Baek shook his head and shouted. Meng So and Han Yi-Myung immediately grasped the situation and rushed to Tang Gunak with all their might.

“Tang Family Lord!”

“Commander!”

Tang Gunak snapped his head to look at Han Yi-Myung.

“We need someone in the Ice Palace skilled the most in Yin techniques!”

“Eh? Th-that is…”

A look of embarrassment flickered across Han Yi-Myung’s face. Naturally, the one capable of the Ice Palace’s strongest Yin techniques was the Ice Palace Lord. The most powerful ice arts were cultivable only by the Ice Palace Lord.

But Seol So-Baek had not yet mastered all those ice techniques.

“It is not me, but the Palace Lord.”

“Then both of you, come!”

“Yes?”

Tang Gunak turned sharply to look at the Beast Palace Lord.

“Palace Lord! Can you use the Yang Heat techniques?”

“Th-that is the specialty of the South Sea Sun Palace, I…”

“Can you do it?”

Meng So’s face hardened.

“Is it necessary? To save Mount Hua’s Sword Saints?”

“Absolutely!”

Meng So nodded as if there were no need to think.

“Then I’ll do it! Even if I burn to death, I’ll pull it all out!”

Tang Gunak nodded and spoke quickly.

“From both sides of Mount Hua’s Sword Saints, push Yin qi and Yang qi into him. With all your strength.”

“W-what, Tang Family Lord? What do you mean?”

Meng So and Han Yi-Myung’s faces went pale in an instant.

The two qi are mutually antagonistic. When such opposing qi collide within a body, the outcome is predictably catastrophic.

They knew how to handle each qi, so they understood how dangerous this was. As the three before him hesitated, Tang Gunak shouted.

“Do as you’re told!”

But they couldn’t move easily. Tang Gunak, his face set, spoke with conviction.

“He will endure it.”

“…”

“If it’s Mount Hua’s Sword Saints, of all people, he will surely hold on!”

Meng So, Seol So-Baek, and Han Yi-Myung’s gazes turned to the fallen Chung Myung.

A wrong move and their hands might end Chung Myung’s life. So no decision came easily.

But in that moment Seol So-Baek knelt beside Chung Myung and grasped his hand.

“P-palace Lord!”

“I’ll do it!”

Seol So-Baek gritted his teeth.

“If it is this Taoist, he’ll endure! None other than Taoist Chung Myung can endure it!”

Firm belief shone in Seol So-Baek’s eyes. Seeing that, Meng So and Han Yi-Myung also nodded and took hold of Chung Myung’s hand.

“No one! Let no one approach…”

Tang Gunak, about to give final instructions, closed his mouth.

All he could see were the backs of the Mount Hua disciples surrounding them. The resolve on those backs was suffocating.

Tang Gunak drew a deep breath.

“I will definitely bring him back.”

He was holding everything of the Heavenly Friends Alliance. The one who showed what they must pursue.

And above all… he was their friend.

Chapter 1403: That Was Amazingly Splendid. (3)

A white sword qi streaked fiercely and poured into the midst of the Ten Thousand People Clan members.

KWAANG! KWAAAANG! KWAANG!

With deafening blasts that seemed to tear eardrums, the incoming sword qi detonated one after another.

“Aaaaaah!”

“Aaaaah!”

Those swept by the explosions let out agonized screams and were flung in all directions.

“Kuh, cough!”

Of course, not everyone died in the blasts. Some, by luck, survived the shockwaves.

But those groaning on the ground were met by a rain of poisonous needles that blackened the blue sky as they fell.

“Ugh, aaaaah!”

Sshhhh!

With the roar of a torrential downpour, needles thinner than hair pierced fragile skin, carrying deadly concentrations of poison.

Pt-tuk!

The thin needles pierced flesh with a tink sound. Those who felt that sound in their bodies rather than their ears were instantly gripped by unspeakable terror.

“No, don’t!”

The panicked tried desperately to pull the needles from their wounds, but the pierced areas had already blackened.

The poison of the Sichuan Tang Family—the Tang Family that had turned those four characters into a symbol of terror—was spreading through their bodies.

A faint discomfort turned into unbearable itching, which soon became searing pain that spread through them.

“Kuh… grrrr…”

The Ten Thousand People Clan members writhed on the spot. Blood flowed from their seven orifices, and black-tinged froth boiled up in their mouths.

“Get away! It’s the Tang Family! Those damned bastards use poison!”

Watching comrades blacken and die in an instant filled the eyes of the Ten Thousand People Clan members with terror.

It was incomprehensible.

How could those who used such vicious poison be called a Justice faction? Even the Evil Sects wouldn’t, couldn’t, use poison that malevolent.

“Do not panic! They’re just needles! If you block them, you’ll be fine!”

It wasn’t entirely wrong—the force carried by each needle wasn’t that great. The problem was that the people here were not only the Tang Family.

“Hiiyaaaap!”

A ripping scream—more a beast’s roar than a human shout—issued from a group that charged straight into the Ten Thousand People Clan members.

KWAAAANG!

Those hit on the shoulders were sent rolling on the ground, spraying blood.

The vanguard struck with tremendous force at those who tried to block them; others leapt over shoulders like leopards, swinging malformed weapons shaped like animal claws.

“Hey, Beast Palace!”

“These filthy barbarians!”

The members of the Southern Beast Palace, their identities proclaimed by the animal pelts draped around them, charged the Ten Thousand People Clan like enraged wild boars.

“Hahahahahaha! You Evil Sect scum! You’ve been looking down on us all this time!”

“Trample them! Completely!”

Where the Beast Palace burst forth, the Ice Palace swordsmen—emitting chilling killing intent—held the line.

“Do not break formation; stop the attackers completely. What matters is the safety of the palace lord and the survival of Mount Hua’s Sword Saint. Any foolhardy action will be met by my blade!”

“Yes!”

The raging Beast Palace and the Ice Palace swordsmen—two factions with utterly opposite natures—joined forces for a single purpose.

“C-commander!”

“We’re being pushed back! Commander! The encirclement is breaking!”

Ho Ga-Myung clenched his fist in silence.

‘Damn it.’

It was the inevitable result.

Of course, one couldn’t deny how formidable the Ten Thousand People Clan was. Among the four factions of the Four Sects Alliance that dominate the Southern land, the Ten Thousand People Clan was practically a leader-level sect.

Still, the Ten Thousand People Clan was only one sect within the Four Sects Alliance. No matter how strong a single force, it was impossible to fight alone against a coalition of the realm’s foremost sects.

Moreover, those Ho Ga-Myung led were not even the so-called ‘main force’ within the Ten Thousand People Clan.

So there was no choice but to be inexorably pushed back.

“Commander! Give an order!”

Ho Ga-Myung bit his lip.

He was right in front of him. The Plum Blossom Sword Fiend’s neck was at hand. If he had even one breath’s worth of time, he could have cut that throat!

“Why at this exact moment…”

How on earth had they found this place? He had already removed those beggar scum’s eyes long ago.

This was not in Ho Ga-Myung’s calculations. Perhaps it wasn’t even within that alliance lord’s plans.

To Ho Ga-Myung it felt as if heaven had abandoned them.

Ho Ga-Myung, who always prioritized composure, could not find calm in this moment.

“Commander! You must give some order!”

Perhaps moved by his officers’ urgency, an involuntary sigh escaped Ho Ga-Myung’s mouth.

“The troops…”

He bit his lip until blood appeared. A word he never wanted to speak, but there was only one order he could give now.

“Withdraw the troops.”

“Co-Commander!”

“Execute it!”

“…Yes.”

A weak reply came from the officers. Hearing it, Ho Ga-Myung squeezed his eyes shut. After a long moment of steadying himself, he opened his eyes and glared at the Heavenly Friends Alliance with eyes full of hatred.

‘Damn it.’

There was no way out now.

It was impossible for the exhausted Ten Thousand People Clan to stand against them. The longer they dragged it out, the greater the losses. If morale remained it might be different, but from the moment they lost sight of the Plum Blossom Sword Ghost, the clan’s spirit had plummeted without end.

If he could gamble everything—sacrifice everyone here—to kill the Plum Blossom Sword Fiend, he might try, but…

‘Mount Hua.’

Those hateful Mount Hua disciples stood around the Plum Blossom Sword Ghost, not moving an inch.

As if vowing not to surrender the Ghost’s neck even if the sky fell.

Seeing that look, an involuntary groan escaped Ho Ga-Myung.

There was no choice. No more.

“Did you not order the troops to withdraw?!”

“Yes! Commander!”

Blood trickled from Ho Ga-Myung’s lips down his chin to the ground.

Woooooooong!

Sweat running down Tang Gunak’s jaw fell to the ground in succession.

His face looked utterly spent, yet his eyes retained a terrifying focus, fixed on Chung Myung.

Chung Myung’s body was momentarily covered in pure white frost, then flushes of crimson flared and receded repeatedly.

Fierce yin qi and fierce yang qi. Tang Pae, who was straining to control the two forces raging within Chung Myung, let out a scream.

“Family Lord! No, not any more!”

Despite hearing the desperation in that scream, Tang Gunak didn’t look away.

“Family Lord! His qi and blood will rupture completely! It can’t continue!”

Across the long history of the murim world, there had never been a member of the Evil Sect who so easily toyed with the Justice faction.

This could not be described merely as excellence.

Jang Ilso seemed to see something others couldn’t. He admired it, and therefore acknowledged it.

But now, after trampling the Qingcheng — one of the Nine Great sects — so mercilessly, that feeling had completely changed.

“Have you taken care of everyone?”

“…A few rats did escape, but I’ll send a pursuit right now and they’ll be dealt with.”

“A pursuit?”

“Yes. From the Lower District Sect…”

“Stop the pointless gestures.”

When Jang Ilso cut him off indifferently, the Thousand Faced Scholar clamped his mouth shut.

“They’re just rats at best. No need to waste effort on them. Leave them be. They’ll spread the word — how utterly Qingcheng was destroyed.”

The Thousand Faced Scholar found himself nodding without thinking.

Not that he actively agreed; he simply believed Jang Ilso to be more correct than himself.

The Thousand Faced Scholar now realized he could never see what Jang Ilso saw.

“Will you leave the Blood Palace lord as he is?”

When the Thousand Faced Scholar asked, Jang Ilso glanced sideways.

The Blood Palace’s elite were slaughtering Qingcheng Sect’s disciples here, but the Blood Palace lord himself was nowhere to be seen.

Though the archers had been sent in time — so it was hard to say they hadn’t heard Jang Ilso’s orders — the lord’s absence felt strange.

Jang Ilso shrugged.

“At least he did the minimum.”

“You mean that’s enough?”

The Thousand Faced Scholar narrowed his eyes and asked.

“I don’t expect loyalty. He will merely take the reward for what he did — whether that reward is good or bad.”

The corner of Jang Ilso’s mouth lifted. It looked like a gentle smile at first, but the Thousand Faced Scholar caught a faint, chilling undercurrent behind it.

Jang Ilso was not one to waste things. In this situation he wouldn’t needlessly create conflict with the Blood Palace lord. Yes — for the Jang Ilso up to now, that would have been enough.

But the Thousand Faced Scholar knew.

Now Jang Ilso simply paid a bit more attention to the situation.

Jang Ilso never forgot.

Even if he temporarily yielded to the wall of reality, he never forgot that he had yielded. That was why Desolate Gold Elder met a miserable death, and the Black Dragon King, with one arm severed, became Jang Ilso’s dog.

Perhaps the Blood Palace lord’s fate would be little different.

If anyone continued to judge Jang Ilso from this awkward distance, one day Jang Ilso’s claws would sink into their throat.

And what of the Thousand Faced Scholar?

Was he really standing in a safe place now?

Now the Qingcheng was all flames everywhere. Before dawn it would be nothing but ashes.

The Qingcheng sect’s strength was great, and the Thousand Faced Scholar had prepared for massive losses when he climbed the mountain. Attacking the Nine Great Sects warranted that. He had to be ready to pay that price.

But now the Qingcheng sect was trampled beneath Jang Ilso’s feet with such ease.

‘If it had been a normal situation, this would never have happened.’

Attacked at an entirely unforeseen time by an entirely unexpected enemy, there would have been no chance to fight back. Crushing those who hadn’t steeled themselves was easier than twisting the wrist of a scrawny child.

But it’s always easy to judge in hindsight. The truly difficult thing is making that easy-to-judge outcome a reality.

There stood someone who calmly accomplished the impossible. The Thousand Faced Scholar stared at him.

Then Jang Ilso raised an eyebrow and asked.

“Why that look?”

The Thousand Faced Scholar flinched.

Jang Ilso’s gaze looked strange. Whether from the heat of the surrounding flames, or the boiling desire within him, his light-colored eyes shimmered with an indescribable gleam.

It was the eye of an ambitious villain. That gaze pressed down on the Thousand Faced Scholar.

From the moment they first assembled to form the Four Sects Alliance, to now burning Qingcheng, Jang Ilso had seen the world with that same look.

It wasn’t Jang Ilso who had changed. It was the Thousand Faced Scholar looking at him.

“Are you suddenly afraid?”

Jang Ilso sneered as he asked.

“Even if you’re scared, there’s no turning back. Now that the Qingcheng sect has been burned, the only road left is for either them or us to be annihilated.”

The Thousand Faced Scholar snorted a laugh.

He was used to hiding his true face and masking expression and feeling, but this rare laugh sprang clearly from genuine sentiment.

He could only laugh.

“Of course I’m afraid of the Nine Great Sects.”

“How honest.”

“I’m afraid of ruin too. It’s not exactly pleasant that the way to live like a makeshift king in the Southern lands has collapsed.”

Jang Ilso nodded slowly.

He despised the ambitionless, but he didn’t particularly dislike those honest about themselves. Acknowledging oneself was no small thing.

“But what I’m afraid of isn’t them or my own ruin — it’s you, Alliance lord.”

“…Me?”

Jang Ilso looked at him as if amused. His eyes were calm though they gleamed in the flames, but the Thousand Faced Scholar’s gaze trembled unmistakably.

“They say if a person fears someone, the method is obvious.”

“Yes.”

A grotesque smile touched Jang Ilso’s face as he answered.

“You kill them. So they can no longer frighten you.”

Jang Ilso glanced around and said.

“How about it? Conveniently, there’s two of you. You don’t seem without a chance.”

A hollow laugh escaped the Thousand Faced Scholar’s lips.

There might indeed be a chance. He too was the sect leader of the Lower District Sect. He wouldn’t necessarily lose to Jang Ilso. Jang Ilso’s unknown ability was a variable, but ‘unknown’ didn’t automatically mean formidable.

“Not a bad plan. But… I know an even better one.”

The Thousand Faced Scholar slowly lowered one knee. The knee that would never bow touched the ground.

Jang Ilso watched that profoundly symbolic sight with nothing more than an indifferent gaze.

“Sect leader of the Lower District Sect, the Thousand Faced Scholar Dam Yeo-Hae.”

The Thousand Faced Scholar bowed his head deeply, very deeply.

“I pledge my loyalty to Alliance lord.”

A man kneeling amid the roaring flames — it might be a grander sight than a burning the Qingcheng.

Jang Ilso twisted a smile and let out a low hum.

“Hmph. How sudden.”

“…”

“Wasn’t it always this way?”

The Thousand Faced Scholar raised his head. The moment their eyes met, he sensed it.

Those eyes hadn’t changed in the slightest.

What did it mean that even after securing the Thousand Faced Scholar’s loyalty, that unchanging gaze showed no sign of alteration?

And the Thousand Faced Scholar naturally understood what he must do.

His hand slowly moved toward his face. When the trembling hand covered his face and then fell away…

At last a different smile bloomed on Jang Ilso’s lips, unlike any before.

“So that’s the Thousand Faced Scholar’s true face.”

He gave a small nod. Then, as if teasing, he added.

“You didn’t have to show that.”

“It’s only natural to swear loyalty to the Alliance lord.”

Jang Ilso slowly approached the kneeling Thousand Faced Scholar. His red cloak billowed and then settled down.

Chin.

Jang Ilso lightly rested his hand on the Thousand Faced Scholar’s shoulder and inclined his head slightly.

“Listen to me.”

A whisper sounded in the Thousand Faced Scholar’s ear.

“I’m someone who doesn’t much care about intentions.”

“…”

“No one can know true sincerity. So it’s enough to know what one wants. What is it you want?”

The Thousand Faced Scholar’s shoulder trembled slightly.

“…Life.”

“How petty.”

Jang Ilso lightly tapped the Thousand Faced Scholar’s shoulder.

There were no other words.

There was no promise to guarantee the life he desired, nor any praise for kneeling and joining his ranks. He merely touched his shoulder once.

But the Thousand Faced Scholar did not ask for anything else.

Before this man, everything was meaningless. Promises, feelings, even displayed sincerity — all were just cards to be used when needed.

What the Thousand Faced Scholar desired was only mercy and value.

That cold, metal-forged flame should burn him and the Lower District Sect as late as possible.

“Finish it.”

“Yes, Alliance lord.”

Both his manner and tone had changed considerably.

This did not only mean the relationship between the Thousand Faced Scholar and Jang Ilso had changed.

The Four Sects Alliance, formed from five sects from the start — this signaled that the alliance would take a different form. In the blaze ignited using the Qingcheng as an offering.

“Shall we head to Emei?”

“We should.”

Jang Ilso’s gaze flicked to Byeok Hyyn-Ja’s corpse.

A worthless death. Its only value at most was buying a few units of time.

“News probably hasn’t reached Emei yet. Emei will be easy to take.”

“Right. But we still must hurry.”

A red line of a smile crossed Jang Ilso’s pale face.

“It would be unfair if only the Nine Great Sects suffered. We should make the Heavenly Friends Alliance feel the same. To burn Emei and trample the Tang Family, we’ll need to hurry.”

“Yes!”

The Thousand Faced Scholar bowed deeply and then turned his body.

Watching his receding back intently, Jang Ilso’s face twisted peculiarly.

‘A rat…’

It’s good to be perceptive. But those who lower themselves out of fear of death eventually show their true colors when they no longer think they’re in danger.

The Thousand Faced Scholar’s fate would be decided not by Jang Ilso, but by himself. In any case, he was useful manpower.

“Tsk.”

Clicking his tongue briefly, Jang Ilso cast his gaze toward the distance — not north where Ami was, but east where the Yangtze lay.

“Ga-Myung is going to scold me again.”

He chuckled and then turned his body.

The flames that had scorched the Qingcheng were spreading northward. Their ferocity was such that no one seemed able to stop them.
