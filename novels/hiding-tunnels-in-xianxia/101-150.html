Chapter 101

The old man felt a numbing tingle at the back of his mind.

Cold sweat ran down his spine as he swallowed hard.

He hoped his senses had deceived him.

But as the tiger-masked figure drew closer, that fragile hope quickly faded.

A savage, primal aura radiated from the golden markings he could glimpse on the skin beneath the robe.

The old man had faced Tier 3 demon beasts before. But their demonic qi had been nothing compared to that of the being before him.

What frightened him even more was the power hidden within. The energy leaking out was enough to leave him struggling for breath, yet he could not begin to gauge the strength coiled inside that body.

He had heard that demon beasts who underwent human-form transformation at Tier 3 usually retained features of their original species. Yet the figure before him was indistinguishable from a member of the human race, right down to his exposed hands and build.

With so much demonic qi and a perfectly human appearance... what else could he have been but a Tier 4 demon beast that had undergone human-form transformation, a match for a Nascent Soul great cultivator!

‘Why is a Tier 4 great demon here...?’

The great demons of Cangmang Forest sought no permission to enter the lands of the human race. Some came to capture cultivators who coveted their territory, others to seize things they needed, and some were said to venture out simply because they were bored.

Which of those reasons had brought this one here?

The old man looked at the tiger mask covering the figure's face.

A mask.

Only then did he remember the masks worn by the Two Heavens Society members around him.

‘It couldn't be.......’

The man in the reddish-brown robe beside him had also gone grim.

[Elder, that man....]

[Shh! Keep your mouth shut and stay still!]

In the presence of an expert at the Nascent Soul level, even a conversation through divine sense could be detected.

The man in the reddish-brown robe hurriedly reined in his magical power. The paltry pressure the Golden Core cultivators had exerted over the clearing vanished as though it had never been there.

“S-Senior, we pay our respects.”

The cultivators at Foundation Establishment or below whom they had brought along were already prostrate on the ground, unable even to raise their heads.

Lee Hyun clicked his tongue inwardly.

‘I used both the Aura-Concealing Art and the Spirit-Concealing Art, and they're still reacting like this.’

He had only come to rescue the Two Heavens Society members peacefully and settle the dispute amicably. Instead, they were treating him like a man-eating great demon that had burst out of the forest.

‘But I'm such a nice person...’

Lee Hyun spoke.

“How dare you oppress the Two Heavens Society... Is this Nanming's will?”

The Nanming cultivators' faces turned ashen the moment they heard those words.

Meanwhile, shock and elation filled the eyes of the Two Heavens Society members, including the man in the red deer mask.

‘So the Society Head's message was true! The one who came to help us... is a venerable Tier 4 great demon!’

They had never even heard of a Nascent Soul cultivator being active in the Two Heavens Society. Yet here was a Tier 4 great demon, an existence even harder to win over.

How had a secret network of rogue cultivators, organized into separate cells to siphon qi from spirit veins and trade medicinal ingredients, come to count someone so terrifyingly powerful among its members?

The old man from Nanming hurriedly gave a fist-and-palm salute, his lips trembling.

“S-Senior! I am Xiahou Heng, an Elder of Nanming's Zhenyang clan! We never intended to offend you!”

Lee Hyun had heard of the Zhenyang clan. It was not one of the twelve powers that made up the Nanming Twelve-Sect Alliance.

‘So the Twelve-Sect Alliance's main sects haven't intervened directly.’

“I hear you tried to interfere with the Two Heavens Society's trade network.”

“I-It's a misunderstanding! We merely wished to make a few adjustments to the distribution of medicinal ingredients along the border...!”

“Adjustments? Then perhaps I should make a few adjustments to the way the Zhenyang clan is run.”

Sweat beaded on Xiahou Heng's forehead.

“We have no intention of quarreling with either the Two Heavens Society or Cangmang Forest. But Tier 3 demon beasts have recently vanished from several regions of the outer seas, making demon-beast materials difficult to obtain. While searching for new suppliers, we learned of the trade network for medicinal ingredients here....”

“Tier 3 demon beasts have vanished? Were you humans responsible?”

Xiahou Heng's face went white in an instant.

‘Oh no!’

He had brought up the disappearance of demon beasts at sea right in front of a great demon of Cangmang Forest!

There was a good chance Cangmang Forest already knew that Nanming's great sects had established bases on islands throughout the outer seas and hunted demon beasts.

If this great demon had entered the lands of the human race over the disappearance of his own kind, the Nanming Twelve-Sect Alliance would be the first to come under suspicion here.

“W-We had nothing to do with it! We are still investigating what happened ourselves!”

At that moment, the man in the reddish-brown robe, Xiahou Zhen, quietly shifted his footing.

He had thought they only had to answer for interfering with the trade network. Now they were about to be blamed for hunting demon beasts as well.

If the great demon had really come because of his own kind, would he listen to anything the Elder had to say?

Xiahou Zhen glanced toward the edge of the clearing.

He had no intention of defeating his opponent. He only needed to obscure his opponent's vision and hold him in place for a single breath, just long enough to escape with the Elder.

The flying needles inside his sleeve silently fanned out.

In the next instant, dozens of flying needles burst outward!

Silver trails arced in at different heights, converging on Lee Hyun's face and upper body all at once.

“Xiahou Zhen!”

Xiahou Heng shouted, but it was too late.

Just as the web of flying needles reached his face, Lee Hyun raised his left hand and grasped at empty air.

Hummm!

A powerful surge of Earth-Origin Force gathered before his palm. The incoming silver streaks veered toward a single point in midair, then were sucked into his hand!

Crunch.

The tiger-masked figure clenched his hand, then opened it.

In that instant, the flying needles had been crushed into a tangled lump of metal the size of a fist.

‘...’

Xiahou Zhen gaped at the impossible sight.

He had painstakingly refined that set of flying needles. Though they fell short of magic treasures, they were exceptional supreme-grade magic tools that could pierce even an early Golden Core cultivator's body-protecting magical light when wielded with a Golden Core cultivator's magical power.

Yet a bare hand had crushed them like taffy!

Thud.

Lee Hyun dropped the lump of metal to the ground and took a step toward Xiahou Zhen.

Xiahou Zhen hurriedly retreated, raising his body-protecting magical light. A thick barrier of light enveloped his entire body.

Tap.

Lee Hyun was suddenly beside him, resting a hand on the barrier over Xiahou Zhen's shoulder.

‘When did he...!’

As the palm pressed down on his shoulder, the thick barrier of light crumpled.

Crack!

Xiahou Zhen's knees slammed into the ground, splitting the bedrock. The crushing pressure on his shoulder drove through his entire body.

“Urgh...!”

Lee Hyun only withdrew his hand after Xiahou Zhen had planted both hands on the ground.

Silence descended over the clearing.

The Foundation Establishment cultivators from Nanming and the Two Heavens Society scarcely dared breathe.

“Were you trying to test my strength?”

Xiahou Zhen could not answer.

Xiahou Heng hurriedly spoke.

“I apologize for my subordinate's insolence! The fool didn't know who he was dealing with...!”

Without waiting for him to finish, Lee Hyun turned to Red Deer.

“Have they already taken any of your goods?”

“They intercepted two of our shipments. We recovered some of the goods, but they said they would settle up for the rest once the terms of trade were decided.”

“And the payment?”

“We have not received any yet.”

Lee Hyun turned to Xiahou Heng.

“Hand it over.”

The old man hurriedly nodded.

“Of course. All the medicinal ingredients and payment we still owe....”

“Those belong to them. I'm telling you to hand over your storage pouches.”

“.......”

The clearing fell quiet.

The old man stared at the tiger mask. Cold sweat broke out as their eyes met.

He slowly untied the storage pouch at his waist.

“Please accept this as compensation.”

Instead of taking it, Lee Hyun looked at the old man's other sleeve.

A moment later, a second storage pouch emerged.

The man in the reddish-brown robe watched nervously before untying the storage pouch at his own waist. When Lee Hyun's gaze lingered briefly on his chest, he took another small storage pouch from inside his robe.

Lee Hyun suspended all four storage pouches in the air.

“Remove the restrictions too.”

The two men used their divine sense to disperse the energy sealing each pouch in turn.

Lee Hyun examined the contents: spirit stones, jade caskets, bottles of pills, and various spirit materials. As expected, the possessions of two Golden Core cultivators amounted to quite a fortune.

‘Good.’

He had plenty to spend spirit stones on at the moment.

Lee Hyun looked at the old man.

“Take out what you're still hiding in your sleeve.”

The skin around the old man's eyes tightened.

“Senior. That is my life-bound magic treasure. I have nurtured it for many years.”

“How generous of you.”

“The contents of my storage pouches alone would more than cover the compensation. Please....”

Lee Hyun did not answer.

Golden demon markings shimmered beneath the collar of his robe.

The old man's gaze fell on them, then moved away.

What would he have left if he gave up even his life-bound magic treasure?

He could always amass the wealth in his storage pouches again. His life-bound magic treasure was another matter. He had nurtured it with essence blood and magical power for years, and it had seen him through countless crises.

If he relinquished his connection to it as well, he would have no way to resist should his opponent change his mind.

The old man moistened his dry lips.

“...Understood.”

A thin, curved black blade slipped from his right sleeve.

A little over one chi (about 33 cm) long, it had no clear division between hilt and blade, and its ends curved in opposite directions.

Dark blood-red patterns surfaced along its spine.

Lee Hyun looked down at the blade.

At that moment, the old man bit the tip of his tongue.

Flash!

The black blade he had been respectfully presenting shot upward like lightning!

Red radiance erupted from the blade, engulfing Lee Hyun's head and upper body at once.

The old man did not expect to kill his opponent.

But his life-bound magic treasure far surpassed Xiahou Zhen's flying needles. A lightning-fast surprise strike with this blade at point-blank range was devastating!

Even against a Tier 4 great demon, all he needed was to inflict an injury. That would give him an opening to flee.

But before the old man's feet could leave the ground, Lee Hyun's right hand plunged straight into the red radiance.

Skrrrch!

A long tear opened in Lee Hyun's sleeve.

Sparks flew as the black blade scraped his bare arm. Instead of cutting flesh, it produced a bone-chilling screech like metal grinding against metal.

Lee Hyun's fingers clamped around the spine of the blade.

Through his divine-sense link to the magic treasure, the old man felt it being seized.

‘...!’

The old man hurriedly changed his hand seal.

Blood-red radiance poured from the black blade as it tried to spin, but it could only tremble in Lee Hyun's grip.

In the next instant, Lee Hyun stepped forward.

Boom!

The bedrock sank beneath his foot.

Just as the old man abandoned his magic treasure and tried to flee in a streak of light, Lee Hyun's left hand seized the back of his neck.

His body stopped dead just as it was about to shoot into the air.

‘No...!’

The old man frantically poured in magical power. His escape light stretched into a long streak, but his body did not budge.

Lee Hyun wrenched his arm downward.

Booom!

Dragged down, the old man's body slammed into the ground. Dust billowed across the clearing in a ring, and the escape light he had forced into being scattered.

“Ghh...!”

The old man spat blood.

Lee Hyun's left hand still gripped the back of his neck. When the old man barely managed to turn his head, he saw his own life-bound magic treasure in Lee Hyun's right hand.

That hand was bare.

The old man's eyes widened as he stared at those fingers.

Not even a drop of blood had welled up where the magic treasure's blade had touched Lee Hyun's skin!

‘.......’

Only then did he truly realize how close he was to death.

“Please spare me!”

The old man cried out desperately.

“Never again... I'll never interfere again! Take anything I have!”

Lee Hyun spoke without releasing the back of his neck.

“Break your link to the magic treasure.”

The old man did not hesitate.

He recited the mnemonic formula, his lips trembling, and the blood-red patterns on the black blade dimmed one after another. The blade stopped struggling in Lee Hyun's hand.

The old man's face grew even paler.

The aura that had permeated the magic treasure over the years had not been completely erased, but at least its owner could no longer control it for the time being.

Lee Hyun put away the blade and looked at the man in the reddish-brown robe.

The man was already holding out a long red iron ruler in both hands.

“Here it is.”

Even before he finished speaking, he was withdrawing the aura he had left in the magic treasure.

Lee Hyun extended a hand, and the red iron ruler flew to him.

“What else are you hiding?”

“Nothing.”

When Lee Hyun continued to stare at him in silence, the man hurriedly pulled a flat jade casket from inside his belt.

“Th-This is an emergency talisman. It slipped my mind....”

Lee Hyun took that too.

Keeping his head bowed, the old man likewise produced a small jade plaque from inside his robe and unfastened the bracelet around his wrist.

Before long, both men's attire had become remarkably modest.

Lee Hyun stowed everything he had taken.

Xiahou Heng spoke, carefully gauging Lee Hyun's reaction.

“Senior. We will remember what happened today until our dying day....”

Lee Hyun considered the matter for a moment.

He had intended to settle their interference with the Two Heavens Society's trade by recovering the goods and making them pay compensation.

But he had no intention of letting men who had aimed magic tools and magic treasures at his throat walk away.

They were already Golden Core cultivators. They might someday reach Nascent Soul or Spirit Transformation and come back for revenge.

Lee Hyun snapped the old man's neck with his left hand while making a short, sharp sweep toward Xiahou Zhen with his right.

The crack of breaking bone and a heavy thud rang out in quick succession.

Xiahou Heng collapsed on the spot, his neck shattered. Xiahou Zhen instinctively tried to dodge, but he too went tumbling across the ground, his chest caved in.

One after the other, the two Golden Core cultivators could no longer be sensed in the clearing.

...

The Foundation Establishment cultivators from Nanming barely dared breathe.

The two Golden Core cultivators who had led them moments ago now lay dead side by side. Their magic treasures, the magical power they had cultivated over the years, and even their clan's name had been useless against that tiger-masked figure.

Someone moved a foot to flee, and Lee Hyun's gaze turned toward him.

The man immediately sank to the ground.

“You didn't attack me, so I'll let you leave alive.”

Only then did people throughout the clearing release the breaths they had been holding.

“Th-Thank you!”

“We are grateful for your mercy, Senior...!”

“But leave your storage pouches.”

The cultivators reached for their belts in unison.

They removed pouches from their belts, from inside their sleeves, and even from beneath their robes. Storage pouches large and small piled up on the ground.

After confirming that they had lifted the restrictions, Lee Hyun called over the man in the rabbit mask from among the members. He was in charge of communications and settling accounts.

“Check for the goods they took.”

Rabbit hurriedly took out a jade slip.

By comparing its records with the jade caskets from the Nanming cultivators' storage pouches, they recovered most of the medicinal ingredients that had not yet been disposed of. The cultivators were made to pay for anything missing or damaged with their own spirit stones.

Only after the accounts were settled did Lee Hyun jerk his chin at the Nanming cultivators.

“Take those two with you as well. When you return, report everything that happened today, down to the last detail.”

The cultivators prostrate on the ground nodded repeatedly.

“And tell them that if they touch the Two Heavens Society's goods again, next time I'll visit the Zhenyang clan myself.”

“We will remember!”

They gathered the two bodies and left the clearing without daring to look back.

...

Having relieved the Zhenyang clan's Foundation Establishment cultivators of their belongings as well, Lee Hyun left with the members of the Two Heavens Society.

Only after they reached a small clearing beyond the valley did several members drop to the ground, gasping for breath.

Red Deer bowed deeply.

“We will never forget your help.”

The members behind him paid their respects in unison. Some even dropped to their knees.

“Rise. I merely came in response to the Society Head's message.”

Those words made Red Deer even more tense.

‘A single message was enough to bring a great demon like this?’

There were all kinds of rumors about the society head of the Two Heavens Society. But even one of the demon race's great demons was involved in its affairs!

Lee Hyun beckoned Red Deer and Rabbit closer.

Rabbit looked at him anxiously.

Was he going to demand a separate reward?

He had come at the society head's request, but he was still a Tier 4 great demon. Rabbit could not begin to guess what they could offer him without giving offense.

As the two approached, Lee Hyun took two jade caskets from his storage pouch.

“The Society Head said to put these up for trade as well.”

A faint medicinal fragrance drifted out as the lids opened.

Rabbit froze.

There were two pills, slightly different in color. Their surface markings and the pulses of medicinal strength they emitted matched the descriptions he had studied over and over in the illustrated guide.

“G-Golden Core Condensing Pills?”

“Yes. They were refined using a pill recipe from Baoguang Temple's tradition. Tier 3 demon cores were among the ingredients.”

‘...!’

The society head had a Tier 4 member of the demon race casually offering Golden Core Condensing Pills made from demon cores for sale?

And he had not taken on the task in ignorance, either. He was explaining the ingredients himself!

‘Just how terrifying must the Society Head be....’

Red Deer leaned forward.

“May I appraise them?”

Lee Hyun pushed the jade caskets toward him.

A moment later, Red Deer swallowed.

“They have lost none of their medicinal strength. The quality is good, too.”

Before attempting Core Formation, he had spent a long time searching far and wide for a Golden Core Condensing Pill. These appeared to have been refined to a higher purity than the one he had obtained back then.

Beside him, Rabbit studied the pill markings and took in the medicinal fragrance before speaking cautiously.

“Based on their medicinal strength, both are mid grade. They contain little impure qi and have been kept in good condition... each should fetch five thousand mid-grade spirit stones.”

Red Deer hesitated briefly before looking at Lee Hyun.

“May I buy one myself? There is a Junior I have been helping with cultivation....”

“Anyone may buy one, so long as they pay a fair price.”

Lee Hyun lightly tapped an open jade casket with a finger.

“But you understand the drawbacks of a Golden Core Condensing Pill made from a demon core, don't you?”

Red Deer nodded.

“Yes. I know that demon markings remain after Core Formation and make advancing to Nascent Soul difficult.”

He smiled bitterly.

“For that child, a chance at Core Formation now matters far more than reaching Nascent Soul someday.”

“Then that's fine.”

Red Deer opened his storage pouch. He took out bags of mid-grade spirit stones and made up the difference with high-grade spirit stones.

Once Rabbit counted them and handed them to Lee Hyun, Red Deer tucked one jade casket inside his robe.

Rabbit's gaze briefly lingered on the remaining jade casket.

As a late Foundation Establishment cultivator, he too desperately needed a Golden Core Condensing Pill.

But the thought of how many spirit stones he actually had quickly dampened his hopes. It was not enough.

“I will look for a buyer for the remaining pill at the same price. Is there anything the Society Head would like to receive besides spirit stones?”

“He said to take spirit stones, but useful items would do as well.”

Lee Hyun handed over the jade slip he had prepared.

The requests were for ready-to-install Tier 3 concealment formations and alarm formations, as well as an artisan able to repair damaged exceptional supreme-grade magic tools or refine magic treasures.

Rabbit read the jade slip and spoke.

“Completed Tier 3 formations are rarely available. Commissioning one to your specifications may be faster.”

“Then find out who accepts such commissions as well. I'll decide once I know what materials are needed and what it will cost.”

“Understood.”

Lee Hyun entrusted the remaining jade casket to Rabbit.

He still had fourteen pills on hand. The members at this base could not afford to buy them all. He would need to enlist the other bases in finding buyers for the rest.

After recording the consignment details, Rabbit put away the jade slip. Lee Hyun spoke again.

“Shut down this base today.”

Red Deer immediately bowed his head.

“Understood.”

“Move all the brokers elsewhere. Change your masks and the way you make contact, too.”

“Understood.”

Lee Hyun nodded. Red Deer hesitated for a moment, then sent him a voice transmission.

[I know this is presumptuous, but... is it called the Two Heavens Society because it has members from both the human race and the demon race?]

‘Huh?’

Lee Hyun thought for a moment.

[Don't pry too much.]

[....]

The tiger-masked figure vanished into the darkness.

...

Before visiting another contact point, Lee Hyun stopped somewhere secluded and checked his spoils for tracking restrictions or anything similar.

He checked for and erased the divine-sense marks on the storage pouches and other items, then sealed the two life-bound magic treasures separately.

There was too much loot to inspect in detail, so he left that for later and visited several contact points.

One was in the mountains, some distance from a cultivator market. Following a seam in the bedrock inward brought him to a small hidden stone box.

He first checked that the mark inside was undamaged, then took out a jade slip.

This time, he recorded requests for inheritances he planned to study, rather than items for immediate use.

He listed inheritances for Tier 3 formations and the artifact-refining art, along with information on flight magic tools or flying boats and on restrictions. He offered spirit stones in payment.

Lee Hyun was about to add an entry on the Puppet Art, but paused.

He already had the methods for crafting Tier 3 bone puppets from 《Profound Yin White Bone True Scripture》, along with the remains of the White Bone Flood-Dragon Puppet and the Six-Armed White Bone Demon.

Instead, he requested methods from various traditions for preparing bone materials.

‘I just need to learn how to avoid the telltale signs of Haigu Island's work.’

Lee Hyun also added information about the Golden Core Condensing Pills at the end of the jade slip.

He specified that they were Golden Core Condensing Pills made with demon cores using a recipe from Baoguang Temple's tradition, gave an approximate price, and included a warning about the demon markings they would leave behind. He planned to confirm the quantity and quality for each purchase request, then fill the orders in turn.

‘There should still be plenty of buyers.’

Lee Hyun left a mark verifying his identity as society head and placed the jade slip inside the stone box.

He put the pills back into his storage pouch.

‘With all this demonic qi, it'll be easier this way. I shouldn't go scaring the members every time I visit a base...’

Lee Hyun was on his way back to Cangmang Forest.

Deep underground, he alternated between the Metal Escape Art and the Earth Escape Art, surfacing once he reached the forest's boundary.

After passing the prohibition marker on the coast, he showed his Lulin Pavilion identification token, and the demon race guards bowed once again.

...

Upon reaching Qingshi Mountain, Lee Hyun went straight into the cultivation chamber of his cave abode and sank through the floor.

Five li (about 2 km) underground.

The ceiling rose more than ten zhang (about 33 m), and the floor stretched for dozens of zhang in every direction. The formation inscriptions Lee Hyun had carved glowed faintly across the rock walls. Heaven-and-earth spiritual qi diverted from the Tier 3 spirit vein on the mountain flowed in through the channels and formation inscriptions he had prepared.

Lee Hyun stood in the center of the cavern.

With each breath, pure energy suffused his flesh and blood.

“All right, come on out.”

Lee Hyun took four Bonefuzz puppets from his storage pouch and set them on the floor. Then he opened his spirit-insect pouch, and golden balls of fluff poured out, their wings buzzing.

Several fuzzbees slipped into the Bonefuzz puppets' skulls with practiced ease.

Clatter.

The Bonefuzz puppets rose one after another. After looking around the cavern, they scattered as though someone had given them orders and began clearing the stone fragments from the floor.

The other Tier 2 fuzzbees and the younger ones circled cautiously near the ceiling of the unfamiliar cavern. Their movements visibly slowed when they approached the formation inscriptions where the spiritual qi was densest.

Bzzzz― bzzz―

A young Tier 1 fuzzbee wobbled through the air and bumped its head against the rock wall. Rather than falling, the little fellow clung to a formation inscription, let its wings droop, and stretched.

‘...Is it drunk on spiritual qi?’

Lee Hyun picked up the little fellow between his fingers and set it on a rock at the edge of the cavern.

“Take your time getting used to it.”

...

Lee Hyun used the Soil-Turning Art on the floor at the center of the cavern.

The spirit soil he had mixed beforehand turned over smoothly, opening a deep pit. He lined the bottom with a layer of soil from Tunnel No. 3.

Then he turned the opening of his storage pouch toward the pit.

Hummmm―

Amid the light streaming from the storage pouch, the Heaven-Origin Divine Tree slowly expanded.

Supporting the entire tree with magical power, Lee Hyun gradually lowered it.

Once the root tips touched the fresh soil, he filled the spaces between the roots so no gaps remained, then used his fingertips to loosen the earth where the old and new soil met. He formed a gentle slope around the trunk to prevent water from pooling.

For a while, nothing happened.

Then the spiritual qi inside the cavern slowly began flowing toward the tree. Streams of spiritual qi entering along the formation inscriptions wound around the Heaven-Origin Divine Tree's trunk, and a faint light spread among its leaves.

Rustle―

The leaves swayed.

When Lee Hyun placed his palm against the trunk, he felt a lively energy unlike anything he had sensed in Tunnel No. 3. It gathered, dispersed, then seemed to reach outward once more.

‘It likes it here.’

Lee Hyun circled the tree, driving the formation flags of a Tier 2 Spirit-Concealing Formation into place one by one. He then inserted mid-grade spirit stones into the formation plate and activated the formation.

Only then did he use the Spirit Rain Art.

Patter, patter.

Clear raindrops struck the leaves and soaked into the soil.

He fixed a wooden sign to the rock wall at the cavern's entrance.

「Tunnel No. 4.」

He then reached inside his robe and laid out the storage pouches he had taken from the Nanming cultivators on this outing.

Thud, thud... There were more than thirty storage pouches!

Just as Lee Hyun cheerfully began opening the pouches to take stock of his spoils, the alarm formation on the surface signaled a visitor.

...

When Lee Hyun surfaced, he found a figure above the forest radiating a gentle but powerful aura.

It was Master Hu.

The moment he landed beside the lake, the elderly scholar in green jade spectacles examined Lee Hyun's face. Then he glanced over Lee Hyun's hands and the repaired stone steps at the entrance to the cave abode in turn.

‘What is he checking for?’

Puzzled, Lee Hyun paid his respects.

“Welcome.”

“Mm. Have you been well?”

Master Hu took something out.

《Neighborly Etiquette in the Village》, 《Three Ways to Resolve Disputes》

They were books written by members of the human race.

“...These?”

“These were at Lulin Pavilion. They were written by members of the human race. I heard you could read the human race's script, so I brought a few along.”

‘He must have liked the way I treated my neighbors last time.’

Master Hu cleared his throat.

“More importantly, there's something I need to tell you. I heard those birdbrains from Heiyi Ridge treated you disrespectfully.”

Lee Hyun nodded.

“The Black Horn Immortal Lord also expressed regret on hearing the details and wishes to apologize to you in person.”

“Is that so?”

“As it happens, a gathering will be held near Lulin Pavilion in fifteen days. The nearby mountain lords and great demons will be attending, and the Black Horn Immortal Lord will be there as well. Would you like to come?”

‘A gathering....’

Lee Hyun looked down at the two books in his hands.

With nearby mountain lords and great demons in attendance, several Nascent Soul-level old monsters would be gathered in one place. He would be a newcomer of uncertain lineage sitting among them.

‘It’ll be a problem if even one of them catches on.’

Still, there was something to gain.

He might learn about ancient inheritances of the demon race, rare spirit plants in Cangmang Forest, or sources of Tier-3 spirit materials he could use to make magic treasures in the future.

It would also be good to settle the matter with Heiyi Ridge.

To stay on Qingshi Mountain for the long term, he needed to get along with his neighbors. Above all, the invitation had come from Master Hu, who had given him a mountain with a Tier-3 spirit vein.

‘And it wouldn’t feel right to turn down kindhearted Master Hu’s invitation....’

Lee Hyun asked,

“Will the Tier-5 Demon Lords be attending as well?”

Master Hu’s ears perked up.

“Ah, no. The Demon Lords do not attend gatherings like this.”

Master Hu adjusted his jade spectacles and added,

“It is simply a gathering of nearby fellow practitioners to exchange news. We discuss matters between our territories and sometimes trade things we need.”

“I’ll attend.”

Master Hu nodded in satisfaction.

“About half a day’s journey north of Lulin Pavilion, there is a place where several enormous ancient trees have fallen. Their exposed roots intertwine to form a broad platform. Come to Lulin Pavilion on the morning of the gathering. We shall go together.”

“Understood.”

...

After seeing Master Hu off, Lee Hyun returned and laid out thirty-odd storage pouches on the storeroom floor in Tunnel No. 4.

As he emptied them one by one, the stone table quickly became too crowded. In the end, he had to push the rock wall farther back and carve three new rows of shelves into it.

“Let’s see...”

He had already returned the medicinal ingredients stolen from the Two Heavens Society and handed over the spirit stones set aside for compensation, so everything that remained was his to keep.

He started with Xiahou Heng and Xiahou Zhen’s storage pouches. Between them, the two Golden Core cultivators had possessed 120 high-grade spirit stones and about 5,000 mid-grade spirit stones, equivalent to 17,000 mid-grade spirit stones in total.

‘They had plenty of goods... but not many spirit stones for Golden Core cultivators. Is that because they belonged to a cultivation clan rather than a great sect?’

Large and small caves had been carved in tiers along the black cliffs. Red light spilled from each opening, while humans and demons carried ore and crates along the paths connecting them.

The sound of hammering metal rang out in the distance.

Clang. Clang. Clang.

Off to one side, three members of the Fire Crow clans were lowering red ore held in their long beaks into a stone pit. Parts of the ore were still molten and stretched in viscous strands.

Zhu Xingshan's workshop was built into the cliff inside the crater.

An iron plate hung over the entrance, engraved with a sword, a shield, and a spear.

Heat rushed over Lee Hyun as he stepped inside.

Red light rose from a circular pit in the center of the floor. Three thick iron pillars leaned inward above it, and a large hook-shaped artifact suspended between them slowly rotated.

An old man with a short beard and sparse eyebrows stood to one side.

His sleeves were rolled up to his elbows. He formed a hand seal with one hand and held a small hammer in the other.

The old man glanced at Lee Hyun.

His gaze immediately returned to the hook.

“Please wait a moment.”

Lee Hyun stepped back toward the wall.

The magical power he sensed from the old man was deep and steady. He did not need to probe him closely with divine sense to tell that he was no Golden Core cultivator.

‘Early Nascent Soul, is he?’

Tiny bubbles rose on the hook's red-hot surface.

The old man moved his fingers. The flames rising from the fire pit narrowed and clung to the hook's inner curve.

Tap.

The hammer struck once.

The entire artifact vibrated, and the interwoven threads of light beneath its surface shifted slightly toward the handle.

The old man raised the hammer again, then paused.

Only after a long while did the second sound come.

Tap.

Lee Hyun studied what had changed between the two blows.

Outwardly, there was almost no difference. But the energy flowed more smoothly along the hook than before.

‘I can't even tell what he's doing...’

As the artifact cooled, the old man set down his hammer and accepted Master Hu's jade slip.

After reading it, he looked at Lee Hyun again.

“I am Zhu Xingshan.”

“Li Dongwu. Forgive me for arriving unannounced.”

“A customer visiting a workshop is hardly an imposition. But the letter did not tell me what sort of item you wanted made.”

Lee Hyun laid the demon-beast materials he had prepared on the stone table.

There were several intact scales, bone material, and pieces of venomous fang. When he finally set down the remains of the Ink-Scaled Cord, Zhu Xingshan's gaze fixed on them.

The old man picked up the remains and gently pulled at both ends.

A faint black light shifted between the broken scales.

“Are these from an Ink-Scaled Venomous Python?”

“You recognize them?”

“I've never worked with the scales of these beasts before... Interesting.”

The old man fetched a slender iron needle and scraped at the inside of the broken cord.

Lee Hyun took out the black curved blade.

“If possible, I'd like these demon-beast materials made into a sword-shaped magic treasure, one that can withstand a large amount of my magical power. If they prove too difficult to refine, I would at least like to have this repaired.”

Zhu Xingshan lifted the curved blade to gauge its weight. He examined the patterns along its spine and fed magical power into it a little at a time.

“It's been well maintained. But there is a limit to how much power it can withstand. You seem to have obtained it from a human, but it was never meant for a Tier 4 great demon like you. It's the sort of weapon a cultivator in early or mid Golden Core would use.”

He set down the curved blade and divided the materials on the table into three groups.

“I'll need to study these further. Do you have the rest of the materials?”

“I brought everything.”

Lee Hyun opened his storage pouch.

A short while later, there was no room left on the table.

“Good. Let's move to the storeroom over there.”

...

Even a preliminary examination of the materials took half a day.

Zhu Xingshan freely admitted when he did not know something, and to Lee Hyun, that candor only made him seem more trustworthy.

After finishing his notes, the old man spoke to Lee Hyun.

“You said you wanted a sword-shaped magic treasure? I will need to examine the properties of the materials more closely first. If there are no unexpected problems, there should be enough to make both a sword and a suit of armor, with materials to spare.”

“Really?”

Lee Hyun brightened.

They agreed on the processing fee, which materials would be left in Zhu Xingshan's care, and what would need to be checked as the work progressed. Zhu Xingshan marked each item he accepted and gave Lee Hyun a copy of the record.

Only after paying fifty high-grade spirit stones as a deposit did Lee Hyun ask his next question.

“Would you allow me to watch the refining process?”

Zhu Xingshan looked up.

“Are you familiar with the artifact-refining art of the human race?”

“Not very well.”

Zhu Xingshan nodded.

“You may watch while I prepare the materials. But I have restrictions and techniques I cannot reveal freely, so I must ask you to withdraw when I use them.”

“Of course. I will help with the preparation wherever my knowledge allows.”

“Let's decide that after I've seen your skill. We can't afford to waste valuable materials.”

...

It was evening when Lee Hyun stepped outside the workshop.

The sun had disappeared behind the mountains, but the red glow of the craters and lava still lit up the surroundings.

Zhu Xingshan offered him a small guest room beside the workshop.

The walls were protected by heat-blocking restrictions, and a bottle of cool water stood on the table inside.

Lee Hyun looked around the room, then asked a question.

“How long have you been here, fellow Daoist Zhu?”

“About a hundred years.”

“May I ask why you're staying here?”

“I'm wanted by the Nanming Twelve-Sect Alliance. I was caught stealing materials several sect masters had entrusted to me.”

“......”

Chapter 107

Lee Hyun suddenly eyed Zhu Xingshan with suspicion.

Zhu Xingshan continued as though nothing were amiss.

“Don't worry. Do you really think I'd dare steal from a Tier 4 great demon? I know full well that great demons like you, who can assume a perfectly human form, have exceptional bloodlines and are immensely powerful.”

“Hmm....”

“I'm serious. If I get driven out of here too, where else would I live?”

‘Hmm....’

He felt rather uneasy now, but Zhu Xingshan had a point. Lee Hyun knew better than most how difficult it was to find a good spirit vein and a safe place to live.

Even so, he couldn't simply take the word of someone with a history of stealing materials entrusted to him.

“Once the work is finished, we will check what remains against the materials I entrusted to you.”

“Of course. Didn't I give you a written record earlier?”

“Yes. I will also check the materials periodically while they are being processed.”

“Do as you please. But if I'm interrupted during refining, I can't guarantee the quality of the magic treasures.”

The old man took no offense. If anything, his expression suggested he would have been surprised if Lee Hyun hadn't checked at least that much.

After Zhu Xingshan left, Lee Hyun sat down at the table and opened the record again.

The quantities of materials, the scope of the commission, and the processing fees had all been recorded in full.

‘I'll keep an eye on him for now.’

A Tier 4 artifact refiner was exceedingly hard to come by.

It would be a shame to cancel the commission merely because Zhu Xingshan was wanted by the Nanming Twelve-Sect Alliance. By watching him work and checking the materials in person, Lee Hyun should be able to spot any signs of trouble.

...

The next morning, Zhu Xingshan took Lee Hyun to the refining chamber beneath the crater.

The stone chamber was far larger than the workshop he had seen the day before.

Deep grooves branched across the floor, each carved with formation inscriptions. They all converged on the circular opening of a fire cavern at the center.

Zhu Xingshan adjusted the formation plate.

Rumble.

As the metal plate covering the fire cavern slid partly open, a jet of crimson flame shot up into the center of the chamber.

“This is Tier 4 Earth-Lung Fire.”

“Is there a reason you use earth fire from a fire vein like this rather than producing your own Tier 4 flames, fellow Daoist?”

“I could certainly craft with my own fire. But during a long refining process, my qi would seep into the materials, leaving the client with the trouble of removing it afterward. That's why flames drawn from heaven and earth are better for commissioned work. They also conserve magical power, which makes the work easier.”

Zhu Xingshan levitated a small piece of bone material.

It was a broken fragment from the Ink-Scaled Venomous Python's bones.

As the crimson flames enveloped it, its hard surface heated up, revealing black lines hidden within.

‘Oh...’

After a while, Zhu Xingshan drew the bone material out of the flames and looked at Lee Hyun.

“Try applying your own flame. I've already loosened the material with Earth-Lung Fire, so it shouldn't be difficult to work with. Keep the heat low.”

After a moment's thought, Lee Hyun accepted the bone material and held it aloft. Clear Yang Purifying Fire blossomed at his fingertips.

The pale golden fire divided into dozens of strands. Fine as threads, they slipped into the gaps the Earth-Lung Fire had opened.

Hissss.

Wisps of black qi slowly rose from the material.

Lee Hyun used careful fire control to vary the heat at each point of contact, withdrawing the flames as soon as the toxic qi escaped from that spot. Apart from lowering the flames' intensity, he made no effort to disguise his strength.

Even so, Zhu Xingshan never doubted that Lee Hyun was a Tier 4 great demon. The demonic qi he exuded was immensely powerful, and his delicate control over the divided flames scarcely seemed within the capabilities of a Golden Core cultivator.

After some time, Lee Hyun withdrew the fire.

“This is as far as I can go. I'm not confident about working any deeper.”

Zhu Xingshan lifted the bone material and examined it at length.

“Such delicate control! Not bad at all.”

This time, the old man brought over a fragment of scale.

“Try processing this too. I'll open up some gaps with Earth-Lung Fire first, just as before.”

Lee Hyun nodded.

...

From that day on, Lee Hyun began helping Zhu Xingshan with small portions of the work.

Since the magic treasures were intended for his own use, he also needed to see how magical power permeated the materials.

Zhu Xingshan occasionally answered his questions, keeping within the bounds of what he could reveal.

“Don't strip away any more here. If you burn away the material's inherent properties as well, there will have been no point in bringing such fine materials in the first place.”

Zhu Xingshan heated the scale with Earth-Lung Fire.

Its interior began to glow faintly, revealing the grain within.

“I'll shape the sword using a process similar to forging, taking this grain into account, then adjust its balance.”

“I see.”

Lee Hyun turned the scale over as he examined it. He sorted the processed materials according to the record, then took out the pieces they would use next.

Zhu Xingshan continued operating the formation plate.

Lee Hyun had been staying at the workshop for about ten days.

Zhu Xingshan was coming down to the refining chamber when he stopped at the entrance.

Two kinds of flame were circling above Lee Hyun's palm.

Pale golden fire burrowed inward as a pallid flame wrapped around it. Soon, the two began to shift positions.

Zhu Xingshan approached with a serious expression.

When Lee Hyun looked up, the old man waved a hand.

“Keep going!”

Lee Hyun turned his attention back to the flames.

The two streams of fire shrank to the size of a fingernail. A small, bright ember formed at their center.

Zhu Xingshan's eyes widened.

As Lee Hyun fed in a little more magical power, the ember brightened. But the outer flames wavered, and the old man spoke at once.

“This time, slow the flow you're holding on the left just a little.”

Lee Hyun moved his fingertips.

The ember tilted.

Just as he was about to compensate from the other side, Zhu Xingshan spoke again.

“Leave it as it is.”

Crackle!

The ember at the center came apart. The two flames spread as though repelling each other, then scattered above Lee Hyun's palm.

Lee Hyun gathered the remaining sparks.

“It always ends like this.”

Zhu Xingshan was silent for a moment.

“But how did you create these flames in the first place? Producing yang fire and yin fire at the same time, then holding them together... Just what sort of inheritance do you possess?”

“I merely copied techniques used by members of the human race I met in various places. I thought these flames might be useful together, so I experimented.”

“How long have you been studying them?”

“About five hundred years.”

“Hah... To devote five hundred years to mere flame practice! You pour time away like water. You must have inherited an exceptionally pure bloodline from a mighty ancient true spirit!”

“You flatter me.”

Watching Lee Hyun scratch his head, Zhu Xingshan grumbled inwardly.

‘These bastards of the demon race and their pride in their lifespans... How could even a Tier 4 great demon spend five hundred years practicing with flames and have so little to show for it? Does he take me for a fool? But this combination of flames....’

The old man pointed to the spot where the ember had just been.

“Could you show me that again?”

Lee Hyun summoned the two flames.

This time, he demonstrated the process slowly from the beginning, including how he divided the flames into fine strands. Whenever Zhu Xingshan asked a question, he answered while keeping the flames at a steady intensity.

He showed what happened when Clear Yang Purifying Fire took hold first, when White Bone Yin Fire seeped in first, and when he varied the proportions of the two.

“Can you maintain that central ember while doing anything else?”

“Simple tasks, yes. But it soon comes apart if I try to examine the inside of a piece of demon-beast material or adjust the heat separately.”

“As I thought....”

Zhu Xingshan took a small copper-red gourd from his sleeve.

When he removed the stopper, a tiny red point floated out.

It was a small ember.

The point stood out sharply even amid the workshop's red glow. Its rounded surface contracted, then slowly expanded again.

Zhu Xingshan sent a slender strand of qi toward one side of the ember.

The ember bent slightly out of shape, then quickly returned to normal. It did the same when he prodded it from the other side.

Lee Hyun leaned forward slightly.

Zhu Xingshan made a small opening in the ember. Two sparks broke off and drifted to one side.

After a moment, the sparks slowly changed direction, returned to the original ember, and rejoined it.

‘This is....’

Zhu Xingshan's hand remained motionless around the gourd.

Watching the ember, Lee Hyun recalled a scene from long ago.

The Azure Void Glass Flame had risen in a blue blaze from Tan Yunsheng's small gourd. Each time the firebird, transparent as glass, spread its wings, it drew in the qi of the heavens, and the enormous Ink-Scaled Flood Dragon's scales melted and fell away.

“Could it be... that this flame possesses spiritual nature?”

Zhu Xingshan nodded.

“It seems you've seen one before.”

“I once saw a cultivator of the human race use one. It felt different from the flames I produce.”

“Naturally. Cultivators use spirit fire as a loose term for any flame created with spiritual power, but true spirit fire refers to flames imbued with spiritual nature, like this one.”

Lee Hyun couldn't take his eyes off the red ember.

Zhu Xingshan fed a little magical power into it, and the flame brightened.

When he reduced the supply, it shrank again, but the ember's internal flow remained unchanged.

“The slightest imbalance causes your two flames to separate, so you have to keep holding them together. But this one can preserve its own nature. A little interference from me isn't enough to disrupt it.”

Lee Hyun nodded.

“Could I give my flames that kind of spiritual nature as well?”

“It's possible. But simply possessing spiritual nature won't make opposing flames merge on their own. A mistake could destroy an ember you've painstakingly nurtured. Still, you've already established a flow that allows your two fires to coexist, haven't you? The only problem is that maintaining it demands all your concentration.”

“That's right.”

“You might be able to make that flow part of the ember's own nature. Your skill at harmonizing the two flames is extraordinary, which makes me think spiritual nature could bind them into one.”

Lee Hyun's eyes lit up.

“Would you please offer me some guidance?”

Zhu Xingshan stroked his beard.

“There are several ways to obtain flames with spiritual nature. You may not know this, but the human race has a very long history of cultivation, and countless inheritances exist throughout the world. Some methods involve collecting an ember and nurturing it separately over a long period. Others involve refining natural flames that possess spiritual nature and incorporating them into one's own fire. In the Qianyuan Empire, a mighty Tier 5 cultivation nation of the human race, there would surely be countless methods of that sort.”

A quiet pride in the human race colored the old man's voice.

Lee Hyun nodded agreeably.

“The human race truly is impressive.”

“Indeed. But my own knowledge only extends to nurturing this flame. I can't claim to know how to merge your two fires.”

“Even that is an enormous help.”

Looking rather pleased, Zhu Xingshan fed magical power into the ember.

Whoosh.

The red flame stretched into a long tongue. The old man picked up a stone fragment from a corner of the refining chamber and held it against the tip.

The edge of the stone heated up and quickly softened.

When Zhu Xingshan drew his hand back, the long flame died down. Once again, only the original little ember remained suspended in the air.

Lee Hyun spoke up.

“There's something I'm curious about. That spirit fire is precious. Doesn't using it this way deplete the ember's spiritual nature?”

“The flames are fueled by my magical power, so that's mainly what they consume. As long as I don't damage the core ember or burn it up, using the flames won't wear away the spiritual nature I've worked so hard to nurture.”

‘Oh...! So that's how it works.’

Lee Hyun regarded the red point with growing appreciation.

He had more than enough magical power to fuel the flames. He was also confident he could nurture something over a long period. All he needed was a seed of spiritual nature, and he could produce spirit fire in abundance!

Zhu Xingshan tilted the copper gourd, and the ember slipped back inside.

“As it happens, there may be an opportunity to refine a natural flame right here in this volcanic range.”

“...! Really?”

...

They stood in the narrow corridor outside the refining chamber.

Zhu Xingshan pointed toward the enormous crater below their feet.

An ash-gray crust formed over the dark red lava, then split open again. Whenever red flames rose through the cracks, the formation inscriptions on the crater's inner walls briefly lit up.

“Farther down, there's a place where several fire veins converge. The Fire Crow clans call that inner region the Inner Fire Cavern.”

Lee Hyun peered into the depths.

“In some places, fire gathers and lingers, leaving embers that don't easily go out. Some possess a faint spiritual nature that allows them to retain their own properties. Embers of that kind, ones worth collecting and nurturing, are generally called fire seeds.”

Zhu Xingshan pointed to a small vent below the corridor.

Orange flames briefly spouted from it before quickly subsiding.

“The study of fire seeds is so vast and complex that it could be considered a discipline in its own right. Even I know only a little. For instance, powerful Earth-Lung Fire doesn't necessarily produce powerful fire seeds. Their spiritual nature also varies. Some embers have little burning power but a strong spiritual nature, while others burn fiercely but have a weak spiritual nature. Many members of the Fire Crow clans at Tier 3 and above refine fire seeds for their own use as well.”

Lee Hyun looked at the crater again.

‘So there were things like this underground all along.’

Until then, he had thought of earth fire as nothing more than a source of flame to draw on when conserving spiritual power or magical power.

“Would I be able to acquire a fire seed?”

“You'll have to discuss that with the Fire Crow clans. The fire veins belong to them. Sometimes they accept spirit stones, and sometimes they ask for materials or equipment they need.”

“I would be willing to pay with spirit stones or valuable spirit plants. I would appreciate a recommendation from you, fellow Daoist Zhu.”

“I'll put in a word. Since you're of the demon race, the Fire Crow clans are unlikely to object. You really are fortunate, fellow Daoist Li. Cultivators of the human race have a hard time obtaining fire seeds, yet you came to commission magic treasures and now have a chance to acquire one... I envy the demon race. Such long lifespans, too....”

“Haha... Whenever you need my help, fellow Daoist Zhu, please say the word. I will do whatever I can.”

As he spoke with Lee Hyun, Zhu Xingshan sensed something was off, and goosebumps rose on his skin.

‘Dammit, why does it feel as though I'm talking to someone of the human race? To think a vicious Tier 4 great demon who could devour us at any moment could be this polite! Has he studied the human race's etiquette? What a calculating fellow...’

The old man's gaze briefly drifted toward the refining chamber.

He thought of the processed bone material. Several pieces were so enticing that he wanted to use them to reinforce his own life-bound magic treasure.

‘Mmm... Could I get away with one more heist?’

At first, he had only intended to have a look. But after handling the materials himself, he had changed his mind.

When would he ever have another chance to obtain this much intact material from a Tier 4 Ink-Scaled Venomous Python?

Early-stage Tier 4 members of the demon race were generally harder to kill than early-stage Nascent Soul cultivators of the human race.

‘It's not as though he'll need every last piece for the sword and armor. Plenty gets discarded during processing anyway.... He probably won't notice if I pocket a little. He's not an artifact refiner....’

He had no desire to abandon such a fine workshop and go on the run.

It was a concern that this fellow Li Dongwu checked the materials so carefully, but how could he possibly see through the expert methods of a Tier 4 artifact refiner like Zhu Xingshan?

‘I even pulled a fast one on those bastards, the monstrous sect masters of the Nanming Twelve-Sect Alliance. Hiding a few pieces of material entrusted to me by an early-stage Tier 4 member of the demon race should be nothing....’

To advance along the immortal path, Zhu Xingshan steeled his resolve.

A lie was best hidden among truths!

‘I've been dealing with him in good faith, haven't I? All I need to do is make him genuine magic treasures.’

“Fellow Daoist Zhu?”

“Ah, right. Let's head back inside. I need to close the fire cavern I opened earlier.”

...

After that, Lee Hyun continued coming and going from the refining chamber.

He processed demon-beast material, practiced controlling the two flames together, and asked Zhu Xingshan about nurturing embers whenever he had time.

Zhu Xingshan lent him a thin booklet explaining how to examine a fire seed's properties and what precautions to take when gathering and storing one. He said it was a compilation of methods in general circulation and had nothing to do with his own secret teachings.

Lee Hyun's opinion of Zhu Xingshan gradually improved. Whenever Zhu Xingshan noticed signs of this, he grew more excited at the prospect of getting away with his theft...

A month passed.

Zhu Xingshan came to Lee Hyun's guest room.

“I've received permission. We'll go together tomorrow morning. I'll only accompany you as far as the entrance to the Inner Fire Cavern. The Tier 4 Elder of the Fire Crow clans who oversees the Inner Fire Cavern has agreed to guide you personally beyond that point.”

Lee Hyun closed his book.

“Will I be able to gather a fire seed straight away?”

“You'll need to inspect them first. You can't simply take every one that catches your eye. The Fire Crow clans are nurturing some for themselves.”

“How should I pay?”

“They said they'd decide after inspecting the goods. You should be able to pay with the spirit plants, spirit materials, or spirit stones you have.”

Lee Hyun nodded.

After Zhu Xingshan left, he checked the spirit stones and items he could trade in his storage pouch.

‘This should be enough...’

Lee Hyun summoned the two flames and kept trying to blend them in a swirling motion. His heart swelled with anticipation. After five hundred years of playing with fire, he finally had a clear direction!

...

The next morning.

Zhu Xingshan led Lee Hyun away from the northern crater where the workshop stood and toward the largest crater at the heart of the volcanic range.

The closer they came, the more forbidding the atmosphere became, a stark contrast to the outer workshop district. Enormous pillars of red iron had been driven into the crater's rim, and dark red fire curtains hung between them, blocking outside eyes and divine sense.

Flying overhead seemed to be forbidden here, for even Zhu Xingshan dispelled the light carrying him and landed before a suspension bridge strung between the cliffs.

Before the stone gateway at the bridge's entrance, warriors of the Fire Crow clans stood in ranks, clad in feathered armor and carrying long spears. From the tall watchtowers on either side, Tier 3 members of the Fire Crow clans kept a sharp watch on everyone entering or leaving.

“Please stop, Senior. Beyond this point lie the restricted grounds of the Inner Fire Cavern.”

Zhu Xingshan held out the crimson iron token he had obtained in advance to the guard blocking their way, a late-stage Tier 3 member of the Fire Crow clans. The guard channeled demon power into the iron token and only lowered the spear after verifying its mark.

“You may pass. However, if you stray from the designated path or use magical power without permission, the formation will activate immediately.”

Security was just as tight along the path leading down into the crater beyond the checkpoint. Patrols were stationed along the sheer cliffs, and a web of formations extended deep into the bedrock beneath their feet.

Lee Hyun quietly gauged the surrounding qi and found the defenses so formidable that even his Earth Escape Art or Metal Escape Art would have trouble getting him inside unnoticed.

‘So this is what an important clan facility is like? Things seem much better organized when an entire clan holds a territory, unlike a lone mountain lord like me, who claimed a Tier 4 spirit vein through personal strength alone.’

When they reached a broad stone platform below the crater, a member of the Fire Crow clans was waiting for them.

His head and torso were humanlike, though black feathers remained on both arms. His eyes were rimmed in red, and iron rods of varying lengths hung from his waist.

This was a Tier 4 member of the demon race who had undergone human-form transformation.

Zhu Xingshan approached first and offered a fist-and-palm salute.

“Elder He Yan, you're here early. This is fellow Daoist Li Dongwu, whom I told you about.”

The Elder of the Fire Crow clans, He Yan, gave Lee Hyun a slight nod.

“Greetings, fellow Daoist Li Dongwu. I am He Yan, in charge of the Inner Fire Cavern. I will guide you today.”

“Thank you for taking the trouble, fellow Daoist He Yan.”

“Not at all. Master Zhu told me about you. I hear you're very interested in the embers from our fire veins.”

Once the two had exchanged greetings, Zhu Xingshan discreetly stepped back.

“My permission only extends this far, so you two go on. I'll return to the refining chamber.”

After Zhu Xingshan left, He Yan turned and walked to the edge of the stone platform.

He Yan was startled to see Lee Hyun preparing to leap into the lava below.

“Fellow Daoist! Not that way!”

“Oh? This isn't the way?”

He Yan gave a bemused cough, then took a narrow path along the cliff instead of jumping into the crater.

At the end of the path stood a massive stone door engraved with several layers of restrictions.

He Yan took a short iron rod from his waist, inserted it into a hole beside the door, and channeled demon power through it.

Clack.

The stone door opened, and hot air rushed out.

He Yan did not enter at once, but crouched at the threshold instead. Holding a hand over a narrow crack in the floor, he studied the glow gathering at the tips of his feathers for a while.

“Let's take the western path down today. The fire qi is relatively calm there.”

...

The stone steps continued for a long way along the crater's inner wall.

The farther they descended, the stronger the metallic smell became. Red streaks branched like veins across the rock walls, and the black stones embedded between them each bore a different marking.

Suddenly, a torrent of pure white flame burst from the passage to their right with a deafening roar.

Whoooosh―

The passage, empty only moments before, filled with fire in an instant. The flames struck the opposite rock wall and split apart, then streamed downward along the deep grooves in the floor.

He Yan walked on ahead, apparently accustomed to it.

At the bottom of the stairs, the view suddenly opened up.

“Here we are.”

An enormous cavern lay before them.

Flames streamed down from points all over the ceiling and walls. Below, lava spilled over rocky ledges of varying heights and plunged into a deep pit.

Orange flames wreathed the rocks, while blue light rose from narrow cracks beside them. Nearby, something resembling red smoke hung in unnaturally dense clumps.

Lee Hyun extended his divine sense with meticulous care.

“Are all of those fire seeds?”

“No. Most of that is ordinary earth fire flowing out of the fire veins.”

He Yan drew a long iron rod from his waist and pointed its tip at a nearby rocky ledge.

A small hole lay beneath the fierce orange flames. When He Yan cleared away the stone in front of it with the rod's tip, a dark red glow about the length of one finger segment appeared within.

Whoosh.

Flames washed over the ledge, swallowing the tiny glow.

But when the flames had passed, it was still in the same place.

He Yan brought the rod's tip closer. The dark red glow stretched into a thin strand and crept a short way along the rod.

Lee Hyun leaned forward.

When He Yan raised the rod, the glow lifted free of the rock.

Then it happened.

The tip of the elongated glow suddenly snapped off. The ember clinging to the iron rod dropped, then moved against the rush of flames back to its original hole.

He Yan lowered the iron rod.

“As you saw, if you try to tear a fire seed with spiritual nature away by force, it will flee back to its nest, or the ember may shatter. I'll show you the embers farther inside that can be gathered. For now, take your time and look around.”

Lee Hyun looked deeper into the cavern.

One by one, he made out crevices in the rocks that had been hidden behind the flames.

Small embers nestled inside.

The place was like an entire exhibition hall for fire seeds!

Lee Hyun eagerly began examining them one by one.

‘Which of these guys will work with my flames....’
