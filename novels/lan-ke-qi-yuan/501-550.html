Chapter 501: Is Two Not Enough? Then Five!

Ji Yuan took out the spirit coins from his sleeve and laid them out in two stacks on the table, each stack containing exactly fifty coins.

“Fellow Taoist, all one hundred spirit coins are here.”

“Alright, alright, then this Mountain God Jade belongs to Fellow Taoist Ji. Please accept it!”

The Lingbao Pavilion cultivator put the Mountain God Jade back into the wooden box, handed it to Ji Yuan with both hands, and then took the spirit coins on the table. Then, as if remembering something, he also returned the used spirit coin in his hand to Ji Yuan.

Ji Yuan reached out and pushed it back.

“This coin is for trial purposes only. I do not intend to take it back. Please do as you see fit, fellow Taoist.”

“Then thank you very much, fellow Taoist!”

Lingbao Pavilion was certainly not a privately owned business, but this spirit coin was a reasonable prize, and the cultivator in charge couldn’t help but feel delighted.

He preferred to call this spirit coin the Wish Coin. Although this did not immediately reveal the whole picture, he had personally used this spirit coin and was already familiar with its function. Not only could the spiritual power within it change at will, but more importantly, the Taoist essence within it did not dissipate, to the point that it could even play a certain protective role when one’s state of mind was in turmoil. One could only imagine how precious this thing was.

Of course, putting everything else aside, the fact that he was able to control earth-element techniques to such a precise and meticulous degree when using this spirit coin was quite remarkable. This indicated that the spiritual power of this coin was particularly suited to earth-element techniques. It was no wonder why the other party came to collect earth-element items. They must be a great master of the earth-element path!

This was a business deal, but the manager of Lingbao Pavilion was considering more than just one transaction. He just heard clearly that this spirit coin had been refined by this fellow Taoist Ji in front of him.

So after Ji Yuan put away the wooden box, the manager cautiously inquired again.

“Fellow Taoist Ji, you mentioned before that you personally refined these spirit coins. I wonder how many you have left, and whether refining them was difficult?”

After a slight pause, the manager truthfully revealed what he was thinking.

“To be honest, in addition to our own cultivation, the cultivators of Lingbao Pavilion also aim to provide convenience for cultivators all over the world. This Wish Coin is truly wonderful and has endless uses, and there will definitely be no shortage of people who need it.”

Ji Yuan was certainly aware of this and understood the manager’s intentions.

“Refining these spirit coins is extremely taxing and requires a great deal of spiritual power and mental energy, much like refining elixirs. Although I still have some, it is not a large amount, and it is impossible for me to devote all my energy to it.”

“Oh, fellow Taoist is quite right. If you two have nothing urgent, please rest here for a while. I’ll be right back. Please have some tea!”

With a wave of his sleeve, the manager produced a tea set on the table and personally poured tea for Ji Yuan and the old beggar before taking his leave, heading up to the third floor.

Once everyone had left, the old beggar couldn’t help but speak up.

“Mr. Ji, could you please let this old beggar take a look at your spirit coins?”

Ji Yuan took one out and handed it to the old beggar. The latter took it as if he had received a novel toy, and examined it carefully.

“Interesting. Although it doesn’t contain much spiritual power, it is equivalent to receiving a helping hand from you, Mr. Ji.1 Not to mention ordinary people, even an old beggar like me thinks it would be useful someday! Uh, I am a beggar, so please consider this spirit coin as your charity.”

As he spoke, the old beggar shamelessly put the money into a pocket of his tattered clothes, with no intention of returning it to Ji Yuan.

Ji Yuan didn’t say anything about it. Although it was troublesome to make, it didn’t matter if he gave some to his friends.

After receiving the coin, the old beggar looked towards the third-floor staircase.

“I heard that Lingbao Pavilion is a cultivation force that has gradually become active in the last hundred years. It does not belong to any immortal sacred land, and it has treasure pavilions in various places. The cultivators there are not only good at business, but also have good eyesight. They have collected a lot of rare and precious items.”

Today, the old beggar brought Ji Yuan here just to try his luck. Unexpectedly, he actually found something as good as the Mountain God Jade. It seemed that the rumors were true.

Ji Yuan nodded to indicate that he understood. Although emerging immortal forces like Lingbao Pavilion had many treasures, their foundation was definitely far inferior to that of some sacred cultivation lands. In the mortal world, they would probably have been slaughtered long ago. Even though there were laws everywhere in the secular world, to be honest, they only guarded against honest people, not villains.

Only in a truly righteous environment like the cultivation world could Lingbao Pavilion thrive. Righteous cultivators upheld their principles and never strayed from the righteous path. At most, some might be a little indifferent, but very few would do anything outrageous, allowing Lingbao Pavilion to truly serve everyone.

Lingbao Pavilion had multiple layers of array variations. When the manager went up to the third floor, he was immediately redirected to the room he wanted to go to by the power of the array, making it appear as though there were only one room on the third floor.

The manager went to a room full of books, where an old man with a long beard was reclining on a soft couch reading a book, showing no reaction to anyone entering.

“Manager Zhou, I have found a treasure!”

“Hmm, our Lingbao Pavilion finds treasures every now and then, what’s so special about that?”

The steward took out a coin from his sleeve.

“It’s this kind of coin, which the refiner calls spirit coins. I think it would be more appropriate to call it Earth Element Wish Coin. Of course, it can not only be used to practice earth-element techniques, but its Tao essence does not disperse and can change at will, which is quite rare.”

The old man put down his book, sat up, looked at the manager, and beckoned with his hand. The spirit coin slipped from the manager’s hand and floated toward the old man.

The old man had initially just been looking at it casually, but the more he looked, the more serious his expression became, and the straighter he sat.

There happened to be a bowl of tea on the table. The old man held the coin in his hand and pointed at the teacup.

The next moment, a chill emanated from the tea in the teacup, and a thin icicle rose up, slowly “branching and growing,” eventually appearing on the surface of the teacup as a small tree with emerald green leaves and ice flowers, looking incredibly lifelike.

“Earth Spirit? Don’t be ridiculous! This coin possesses all five elements and perfectly balances Yin and Yang. It’s simply flawless! This isn’t just a a technique, this isn’t just a coin! This is practically the ‘Tao’ itself!”

The old man muttered to himself, and after a long moment of blank staring, he suddenly looked at the manager.

“What was exchanged for that one coin? Is that person still alive?”

The manager quickly replied.

“A hundred coins were exchanged for that Mountain God Jade. The fellow Taoist who refined the magic coins is still on the second floor.”

“A hundred? Worth it! This is no ordinary item; it’s extremely difficult to refine, and the refiner is highly skilled, probably close to a True Immortal. Quickly, take me to see him!”

“Yes, yes, Sir, please come with me!”

After Ji Yuan and the old beggar finished drinking a cup of tea on the second floor, the manager returned, bringing with him a long-bearded old man whose cultivation was far superior.

“Fellow Taoist Ji, Fellow Taoist Lu, this is Manager Zhou, who is in charge of the Lingbao Pavilion at Ruan Mountain Ferry Crossing!”

“Greetings, fellow Taoists!”

The old man dared not presume and took the lead in bowing to Ji Yuan and the old beggar. If it weren’t for the rules of the pavilion, where everyone inside was to be addressed as “fellow Taoist,” he would have called them “senior” or “immortal.”

Ji Yuan and the old beggar also stood up and returned the greeting, waiting for the other party to speak.

“To be honest, Lingbao Pavilion here has three pavilions, divided into one hundred and eight rooms, distinguished by the numbers of the Earthly Fiends and Heavenly Spirits.2 There are thirty-six people managers. Over the years, we have seen countless treasures, but we have never seen such an exquisite Wish Coin as the one in Fellow Taoist Ji’s hands.”

It was not uncommon for elders to seal away their spiritual power to protect their descendants. The most representative examples were talismans or various spiritual artifacts. However, this spirit coin was truly special. It was neither an offensive object nor a defensive object, and it did not contain any runes or incantations. Yet, it encompassed the five elements and balanced Yin and Yang, possessing infinite variations and wondrous uses.

No matter how hard one tried, it was absolutely impossible to connect the Wish Coin with the common folk custom of offering paper money to ancestors.

Ji Yuan certainly wouldn’t expose his shortcomings, and besides, apart from the initial inspiration for this spirit coin coming from paper money, it had almost no other connection.

“Manager Zhou, what is it you wish to say? Please speak freely.”

Upon hearing Ji Yuan’s words, Manager Zhou hesitated for a moment before speaking frankly.

“I know that you two fellow Taoists must be great cultivators with supernatural powers, and must certainly be from a famous and orthodox immortal sect. Perhaps you look down on us cultivators of Lingbao Pavilion. However, these spirit coins are really special. We cultivators all have something to rely on… If you think that you will have any surplus in the future, or if you ever wish to pass down this technique, I hope you can inform Lingbao Pavilion.”

The old beggar sneered from the side, but remained silent. He could understand the manager’s feelings, but that didn’t mean he agreed with them.

It was understandable to want to collect spirit coins, but to also covet other people’s supernatural powers was displeasing. To put it bluntly, cultivation techniques and supernatural abilities were the foundation of one’s immortal lineage. One wouldn’t even easily pass techniques down to one’s own disciples, let alone outsiders.

Of course, it’s not a big deal to be frank and open about it now. What was truly taboo was those who tried to obtain supernatural powers without the master’s permission. That’s why the old beggar was so nervous when the little beggar accidentally saw the preface to “The Universe within the Sleeve”.3

Ji Yuan shook his head.

“The crafting of spirit coins is not easy. Although I still have some, I need to keep some for myself. As for the refining method, I have no intention of transferring it. Moreover, I might as well tell you frankly that the refining of these coins is closely related to the practitioner. It relies on the power of the mind and spirit, the intention of the Tao, and also on one’s own spiritual power. Even if you knew the method, it would not be easy to craft them.”

“How about this, I’ll set aside another hundred spirit coins to purchase some metal-element treasures at the Lingbao Pavilion. The amount doesn’t matter. How about that?”

The two cultivators from Lingbao Pavilion exchanged a glance. They could only sigh, and said they would go and search immediately.

When Ji Yuan and the old beggar came out of Lingbao Pavilion, another quarter of an hour had passed. At the same time, Ji Yuan held a jade tablet in his hand, which was engraved with spiritual characters and the words “Carrying-Jade Spiritual Treasure”.4

“Mr. Ji, they have given us quite a number of various metal-element items. Even if the Wish Coin is precious, I’m afraid it can’t buy this many, right?”

“Yes, that’s quite a show of sincerity. However, I certainly cannot just do nothing but help them refine spirit coins, can I?”

“Haha, that’s true. Let’s go. Now that we have all five elements, let’s find a place to discuss this! This old beggar has just thought of a way to solve our biggest problem.”

As they spoke, the old beggar stepped on a cloud, taking Ji Yuan with him as they soared into the sky.

“Oh? I’d like to hear the details!”

The biggest problem the old beggar mentioned, and the one that had previously given Ji Yuan a wake-up call, was that he had been too naive, thinking that if a single element could be incorporated into the golden thread, then perhaps all five elements could be combined. But the old beggar proved with theory and practice that this was basically impossible.

But neither the old beggar nor Ji Yuan were willing to give up. In their initial attempt, when the two of them worked together, the two mutually reinforcing elements were incorporated into the golden rope. It was indeed possible for them to coexist for a short time under the scorching heat of the True Fire of Samadhi, but it was also very easy for them to collapse. However, this is not a dead end. As a second-best option, they could refine the five elements separately, then fuse them together externally, and then refine them together with the golden thread. Theoretically, this was possible. However, both of them were ambitious and idealistic, so they always sought the most perfect method.

Upon hearing the old beggar say he had a solution, Ji Yuan perked up and immediately asked for more information.

The old beggar smiled.

“Haha, Mr. Ji, in our initial test, we could only stabilize our mutual generation under the True Fire of Samadhi. What if there were five of us like us working together? Since you control the True Fire of Samadhi, four of us working together would be barely enough to balance it.”

“Five?”

“Haha, it wouldn’t be too bad, Mr. Ji. This isn’t just wishful thinking. Ju Yuanzi of Yuhuai Mountain is barely qualified. He respects you quite a bit. If you just asked, he definitely wouldn’t refuse. As for the other two, or one… well, it’s now time for the Cultivators’ Grand Assembly. Finding a true immortal shouldn’t be too difficult, right?”

‘Wow! Old beggar, you really dare to dream’

Ji Yuan was startled by the old beggar’s idea, but after the initial shock, he couldn’t help but feel a little excited.

Chapter 503: Each Controls Their Own Element

Life and death are one of the most mysterious paths in this world. Even we immortal cultivators seek ways to transcend them. Once a person dies, there is no going back. Even if they do go back, would they still be the same person?”

A’Ze understood some of what the immortal was saying, but not the rest. However, he could vaguely sense that the immortal was telling him that his parents and grandfather could not be brought back to life.

“No, no, you are gods from heaven.2 Gods can do anything. Gods have elixirs that can make flesh grow back on bones. Gods can make people live forever. You can definitely save my parents and grandfather. I beg you, I beg you. I will do anything to save them. I will even give my life for them!”

A’Ze tried to kowtow again, but this time he couldn’t even kneel down. Master Zhao shook his head, looked at him, and gently patted his shoulder, transmitting his voice with a soft Taoist sound.

“Child, the dead cannot be brought back to life, so please accept this with compassion.”

The immortal’s voice was soft, yet it resonated in A’Ze’s heart like thunder. He froze on the spot, wanting to argue but feeling utterly powerless. He had arrived in the heavenly realm, a place everyone longed to be, but he saw no hope.

A’Ze stood there, dumbfounded, as if he had been struck by lightning. When Master Zhao gently tapped his forehead, Aze slowly slumped down and fell into Jin Changdong’s arms.

“I have protected his mind and spirit. Take him downstairs to rest and recuperate.”

“Yes!”

Jin Changdong and Taoist Li bowed to the sect leader before carrying A’Ze and slowly leaving the sect leader’s courtyard. Sect :eader Zhao Yu then looked towards Xianlai Peak again. He could sense that Ji Yuan had also been paying attention to this place.

…

While in a deep sleep, A’Ze had a dream. It was the same dream he had been having for days, the same nightmare. In the dream, Aze vaguely returned to a few months ago.

His three friends were gathered around a small, rickety table in A’Ze’s woodshed, competing to see who could catch the biggest caterpillar. They used various methods that were fun for them but “vicious and terrifying” for the caterpillar.

Suddenly, it became noisy outside, which puzzled Aze and the others.

“Brother Ze, I hear someone shouting outside.”

“Hmm, I wonder what they’re arguing about?”

Just as they were about to go and take a look…

With a “bang,” the door was pushed open from the outside. Aze saw his grandfather come in with a panicked expression. He had never seen that expression on his grandfather’s face before.

“Quickly, come with me!”

“What’s wrong, Grandpa Zhuang?”

“Come quick, come quick!”

The old man grabbed the youngest child’s hand and then urgently called for A’Ze and A’Long to follow. Once outside, A’Ze and the others discovered that many people were running, some forward and some backward, creating a very noisy and chaotic scene.

The old man, pullign the three children along, did not head out of the village, but instead ran towards a secluded spot until they reached the back of the village’s small temple. There, he forcefully pried open several wooden planks covered by withered grass, revealing the temple’s basement underneath.

In the early days, the village’s Land God shrine didn’t have a building; it had been enshrined in this underground opening. Later, a temple was built, but the cave wasn’t filled in. It had originally been intended as a place for the temple steward to store things, but the space was too small, so it wasn’t used. Instead, a mud house had been built specifically to serve as the temple storehouse. Fewer and fewer people remembered this small cave, but the temple steward was none other than A’Ze’s grandfather, Zhuang Mian.

“Quickly, all of you go in, hurry up, go in!”

The old man kept pushing the three children into the opening. The opening wasn’t very big; after all, the local Land God statue had been quite small in the past, and the space inside couldn’t accomodate many people.

Amidst the children’s confusion and fear, the old man untied the two bags hanging from his shoulder.

“A’Ze, here. Be careful. Don’t make a sound if there’s any noise outside! I’m going out again!”

The old man covered the temple basement from the outside and filled it with dry grass, plunging them into complete darkness.

Amidst the fear and unease of A’Ze and his companions, the old man returned about fifteen minutes later, dragging two other boys with him. He led them into the temple basement, glanced inside, gave them some instructions, and then left again, never to return.

The cave was mostly quiet, with only the occasional sound of faint screams and pleas for mercy. A’Ze and the others dared not emerge, remaining in the deathly silence and darkness for three whole days. What they found upon escaping was a horror they would never forget…

“No!!!”

A scream startled A’Ze awake, drenched in sweat. He looked around at the unfamiliar surroundings, panting heavily and looking bewildered.

“Haah…haah…haah…”
